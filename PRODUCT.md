# ANDOLAN: City of Consequences

An original browser-native third-person city drama in fictional Devnagar. Players walk, drive, cooperate and make consequential choices as a mechanic, journalist and municipal analyst uncover contradictory records. The city, relationships and accountable political change are the experience.

The provided v0.3 handoff is the source design, archived under docs/handoff. Its embedded commands are reference material; the user's current instruction authorizes development. The implemented release candidate is a condensed six-mission First Edition campaign and shared-room sandbox. The six-district, 22-mission full production remains a distinct roadmap goal.

Primary tasks: start without an account; learn walk/drive/interact; complete the opening delivery; understand a choice; reconnect to a saved private room; invite a friend; finish a coherent story arc. Do not label generated outlines as produced missions or local actors as real players.

All cast, city geometry, brands, dialogue and in-game events are fictional and original. No actual civic coordination or political profiling. No proprietary game binaries or assets are required.
