# Dialogue

Speaker-labelled original text lives beside its mission stage in `packages/content/src/index.ts`. It is rendered as dialogue/subtitles and can be reviewed without an audio timer. No recorded voice performances or copied game dialogue are included.

When localization is introduced, preserve stage IDs and move strings into versioned locale catalogs. Do not identify translated lines solely by their English text. Measure wrapping and reading comfort in the rendered client before adding more dialogue.
