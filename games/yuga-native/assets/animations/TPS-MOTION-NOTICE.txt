Changes by YUGA: reference-calibrated hip/spine/leg motion on Rama's rig,
hero-specific original bind/rest bow carry, periodic rotation filtering,
shared seam tangents, and vertical support fit from actual skinned soles.
The aim sample remains an unpromoted reference study. This adapted motion
library is distributed under CC-BY 3.0 with this attribution. It does not
imply endorsement by the original authors.

No TPS model, textures, music or upstream gameplay scripts are included in
this motion library. Rama's original nine clips remain separate and unchanged.
