# Native art source library

Reviewed 16 October 2026, America/New_York. This is a finite production reading pass over **five source groups**: three cached literary/historical editions, the bundled MakeHuman license/provenance, and one existing YUGA concept board. The selected passages below were reread directly and the three text hashes were checked. The concept board was opened visually. No whole-book, all-traditions or all-Reddit review is claimed. [source-index.json](source-index.json) records immutable source locations, hashes and exact scope.

## Four motifs to build

| Motif and directly read evidence | Practical artist instruction | Original adaptation / boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A pale coastal city with selected gold and turquoise.** Griffith's *Ramayana*, Book V, Canto II, lines 45706–45750 describes palms, pools/lotus, pale mansions, broad streets, banners and burnished-gold gates. Canto III, lines 45794–45810 describes turquoise-inlaid courts and porticoes. | Read the district first as large pale stone masses and a recognizable gate. Reserve aged gold for gate edges, a few capitals and lamps; use a narrow turquoise inlay as a wayfinding accent. Keep the target backdrop quieter than the hero. | Sandstone/limestone materials, exact colors, wear, gate proportions, court plan and coastal placement are our art decisions. The literary scene is not a measured map or an archaeological reconstruction. A decorative doorway needs a readable boundary if it cannot be entered. |
| **A readable water garden, composed from a separate scene.** *Ramayana*, Book IV, Canto I, lines 37625–37649 and 37727–37730 names clear Pampā water, red/blue lotus, ripples, reeds and sedge. Fergusson's reservoir description, lines 17157–17183, distinguishes descending steps, entrance pavilions, niches and galleries. | Keep the bed visible in the shallow court, use low sparse lotus leaves, and cluster reeds at its perimeter. Build stepped edges and niches as calm repeated shapes. Give traversal edges an unambiguous silhouette in both warm and cool lighting. | Pampā and Lanka are distinct literary settings. Their combination is an invented court; the passages do not establish a Lanka swamp. Reservoir architecture is a separate historical reference. No depth, drowning, slowing, swimming or accessible stair route follows automatically from decorative water/stairs. |
| **Warm civic lamps and restrained cyclic pattern.** *Ramayana*, Book II, Canto VI, lines 10547–10625 concerns preparations for Rama's proposed consecration, including street trees erected to hold lamps. Ganguli's *Mahabharata*, Adi Parva, Paushya III, lines 2118–2198 includes a loom and wheel; the explanation identifies black/white threads with night/day and twelve spokes/six turners with year/seasons. | Put warm pools of light around a few human-scale lamp props and entrances, with cooler ambient fill preserving silhouettes. Use a narrow interwoven light/dark paving band; keep it less bright than attack/reward indicators. | Diya/lantern construction, flicker, lighting technology, ring geometry, day/night speed and match timer are original. The first passage does not establish the origin of Diwali. The paving is not a copied sacred diagram, scriptural time model or historical calendar reconstruction. |
| **Hero identity before ornament.** *Ramayana* lines 8504–8618 establishes Sita's earth/plough association in Janak's account and Rama's bow test. *Mahabharata*, Adi CCXVI, lines 19864–19910 introduces Ulupi as the naga king's daughter in the water episode. The inspected YUGA model-direction board adds distinct bow/drape, mace/tail and staff/leaf silhouettes. | Rama: bow, hand/string relationship and asymmetrical drape should survive gameplay scale. Sita: separate staff, braid and layered cloth silhouettes. Ulupi: reserve a clear water-ring staff silhouette and modest ripple language if carried into native production. Keep metal detail concentrated at face/weapon/major joins. | Indigo/gold Rama clothing, Sita's staff/support role, Ulupi's costume/powers, facial features, embroidery and all animation timing are authored game design. These selected passages do not specify them. The concept board is generated illustration, not costume history, a model sheet measured from a sculpture, or rendered game footage. |

Fergusson's Mahavellipore raths discussion, lines 12407–12512, was also reread for oblong roof mass, cell/entrance and portico distinctions. It is an old colonial account with speculative chronology and value judgments. Only those broad form descriptions are retained; its dating, architectural descent claims and religious judgments are not adopted. No scanned sculpture or historical image was inspected in this pass.

## Material and motion decisions for this pass

These are production hypotheses to review in the engine, not numerical facts from the books:

- Keep most large environment surfaces matte and comparatively low contrast. Use one warm metal accent and one cool inlay family per view; roughness variation should reveal form without glittering across the screen.
- Place repeated relief, weave and filigree where a close view can resolve them. At normal play distance, preserve broad cloth folds, face/weapon separation and a clean outline instead of dense high-frequency pattern.
- Give each action a readable preparation, release and recovery. A source association does not justify a new damage pulse, immunity, terrain obstacle or projectile count. Native gameplay determines the visible effect's timing and range.
- Verify the same actual model/view in neutral light, warm evening light and cool fill, and in silhouette. A generated illustration is a direction target; a successful export or inventory count is not evidence of matching it.

## Library organization

Keep immutable source metadata in this file and `source-index.json`. Keep generated reference images, authored Blender sources, exported GLBs/PBR maps, actual engine captures, and validation reports in separate directories owned by the art/runtime leads. Each asset's manifest should link its source IDs, authoring tool/version, file hash, license/provenance, and whether the image is a concept or an actual render. Record revision-specific limitations alongside the asset. The present source library does not itself create an asset or certify visual quality, physics, animation continuity, or commercial-game parity.
