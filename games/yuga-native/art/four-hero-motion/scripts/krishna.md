# Krishna · character animation script

Krishna is a proposed mobile support hero for YUGA. His hand catches a line of sight before the rest of his body turns. At a distance the player should see a dark-blue face, a single feather above it, a pale flute across the left side and a clean gold disc on the right. The ultimate changes that horizontal silhouette into one long, raised left arm.

The shipped classic roster and its named browser models contain 14 other heroes. No existing Krishna kit or release timing was found there. These names, numbers and poses are proposals for new assets. In the project’s older Mahabharata notes, Krishna-Dwaipayana identifies Vyasa and one usage of Krishna identifies Draupadi. Neither is evidence of an existing Krishna character design. ## Character and props

- Keep the bansuri in the left hand for locomotion, with the mouthpiece nearest the left cheek and enough length for the right fingertips to reach the outer stops. Give the flute a soft, pale edge under arena lighting. Carry it at a left-hip sash loop during the ultimate. Provide hand, lip, hip and right-disc sockets.
- A single chakra rests just outside the right hand when ready. Avoid a disc intersecting the index finger or hiding the face when viewed from behind. Make the retiring edge narrower than the advancing edge. A thumb-sized gap between prop and silhouette helps the player read the release.
- The face is attentive. The head can stay still while the eyes search for an ally, the left thumb adjusts the flute or a small smile fades. Show a soft three-bend rhythm in ankle, waist and head in quiet poses. Straighten the axis for the shelter lift. Animate one feather vane, two sash ends and earrings with separate settling times.
- A Pahari painting shows Krishna calling cattle with a flute and identifies his blue skin. An early Cambodian sculpture shows him lifting Govardhan to shelter villagers and herds during a storm. This brief draws on those specific visual and narrative associations. Its shield, cooldown, chakra projectiles and match effects are original game design. ## Match actions

Author at 30 fps with F0 at the accepted command. Target values are base-rank first-playtest numbers. The server controls eligibility, cover, collision, healing and shields. A returning glint only indicates that the held disc can be shown again. | Primary · **Chakra Flick** | One projectile, 21 damage, 42 m/s, repeat every 0.50 s | F0 sends one disc-shaped projectile in the aim direction. At F7–14 a small rim reforms beside the palm. Cover stops the projectile. The respawning held disc has no return-path damage. |
| Q / slot 0 · **Turning Star** | Existing `line` behavior, 44 damage, 24 m range, 62 m/s, 9 s cooldown | F0 launches along the sightline. The larger disc edge and broken gold trail distinguish Q from primary. It advances straight and ends on the first simulated hit or obstruction. A defender can move sideways or use cover. |
| E / slot 1 · **Reed Refrain** | Existing immediate `heal` for living visible allies and self within 5 m, 42 health, 12 s cooldown | F0 resolves the heal. The musical phrase and hand fingering continue as presentation. One open arc at each successfully healed ally makes the result visible with audio muted. The ability cannot raise a defeated ally. |
| R / slot 2 · **Govardhan Canopy** | Proposed stationary 1.00 s windup. On release, grant one 70-point non-stacking ward for 3 s to each living visible ally within 6 m, including Krishna. 48 s cooldown | The caster’s open ring appears on F0. **F30** left hand reaches its support pose and the server checks allies present at that instant. Allies can enter before release. Cover and leaving the radius deny the grant. The protected actor can then carry the granted shield to its normal expiry or depletion. |

The canopy’s rock strata and falling rain are a world-space visual explanation of protection; they have no projectile collision. For implementation, reuse the ordinary ward’s maximum-shield rule and visibility query. Register a distinct timed release that emits one grant. Sita’s shipped `mithila-sanctuary` additionally maintains a healing zone, which would change this design’s balance. ## Blender action sheet

Use actor-relative left and right. Root at the feet, forward +Z after conversion, scale in metres. Author the whole body, keeping an upper-only `spine03` performance available for instant shots and support actions while running. Only `idle` and `run` loop. `cast` is the generic fallback. ## Contact, sound and review

1. Lock the visual flute to one owner per frame during the R handoff. At F5 evaluate grip and hip socket in world coordinates before switching. Preserve the world transform across the change. Blend back into a left-hand hold upon interruption, including a defeat at F29. Avoid duplicated flute meshes or a long jump between sockets.
2. Watch Reed Refrain from the lip-side view. The flute should touch the lower lip without entering the upper teeth. Frame-step F6–16 with right finger weights visible. The healing recipient can be off camera, so the local actor's short breath cue and an on-screen receipt should also communicate success.
3. Review Govardhan Canopy front-on, in profile and from a real third-person match camera. At F30 the raised left finger, face, flute at hip and right hand should read separately. Keep the canopy's upper edge above the hero and its near underside translucent. A segmented ground outline and a steady ally shield work with sound disabled and reduced motion.
4. Compare continuous run against run plus primary, Q and E. Knees and feet stay in phase. Review a missed Q against a wall so the impact occurs where the server stops the projectile. For R, test an ally entering at F29, one behind cover, death just before release, a duplicated receipt and a reconnect in mid-windup. One activation can grant at most one shield per eligible ally.
5. Deliver neutral-lit portrait, side-on flute contact and the F0/F15/F30/F40 ultimate sequence with and without effects. Mark the coverage as visual blocking or skinned engine playback as appropriate. Consider the new hero playable only after canonical roster registration and a two-client test showing matching grants and interruptions.
