Create one original YUGA: Confluence high-resolution landscape character model sheet, about 3:2 or wider. Fully sculpted, mature adult Indian male ascetic, powerful lean anatomy, stormy matte ash-blue skin with subtle tonal variation, strong long nose, brown eyes, quiet self-command, thin three horizontal white ash marks with one tiny central third eye on the forehead. Thick black matted jata gathered into a tall asymmetrical coiled topknot with one ivory lunar crescent, loose back lengths tied in three heavy sections. Bare chest, two restrained strands of dark rudraksha, worn indigo lower wrap with ochre-saffron overfold crossing the hips and a dark woven side sash, modest fitted dark underlayer under the wrap, bronze ankle cuffs and bare feet. On this character austerity is more important than jewelry. Exactly two human arms per figure. His one monumental trishula has three clean forged steel tines, quiet aged copper bindings and a dark wooden shaft; a small hourglass damaru with two tied beads is lashed below the socket. Avoid other weapons, armor, animal skins, tabards, crowns, text, labels, UI or other people. Reserve mutually distinct zones in a single generous horizontal frame. From the left through the middle three quarters, put a true FRONT full body, true SIDE full body facing left with the hands clear of the hip and the trident omitted for the side view, then a true REAR full body showing wrap closure and locks. These three neutral silhouettes share height/head size, relaxed arms with slight separation, ground line, proportions, palette and costume seam positions. Leave air between each figure and around feet/top of hair. At far right, a large crisp three-quarter face bust above a compact FULL BODY planted pivot action pose holding the SAME trident diagonally up from the ground with both hands, representing a rhythmic ground pulse. Action reads as controlled dance, not a strike on a person. Show one whisper-thin blue-white circular ground arc and keep the face, silhouette and feet clear. Let the sculpture get the visual space; near-orthographic studio perspective, charcoal warm-grey cyclorama, broad soft key, cool neutral fill, gentle metallic edge response, subtle contact shadows, fine skin relief, individually shaped hair locks and woven warp direction, legible at thumbnail size. This is a disciplined three-dimensional production turnaround, not a poster, collage of separate designs, decorative painting or cinematic environment. No lettering, watermarks or measurements.

Orthographic-feeling studio key light, restrained stage of dark neutral warm-grey stone, identical scale, floor line, light direction and physical appearance in all panels; produce a studio board matching the sharp, tactile AAA-quality 3D game style in the provided image. Right panel recovery: 3/4 side profile, feet grounded, both shoulders and open left hand settling lower, shaft upright in his right hand, the damaru beaters swing from inertia and long hair settles behind, the last tiny trace of the ring fades. Make the three silhouettes immediately distinguishable. Respectful dance inspiration, believable gait, no violence toward a person. Physically grounded contact, accurate hands, visible lips and eyes, one crescent on one head in each frame. No words, no letters, no logos, no frame numbers, no duplicate bodies, no collage inserts, no battlefield set, no long-exposure ghost images.
