# R2-01 source and rights

This parameter-only candidate derives from the existing YUGA Rama hero and
finish material. It introduces no image or model asset and copies no external
shader or tool implementation. The owned deterministic PBR textures and costume
are dedicated under CC0-1.0 in `art/hero-rama/NOTICE.md`; MakeHuman-derived anatomy
has its separately retained CC0 provenance. The original meshes, map payloads,
UVs, normal conventions, rig and morphs stay byte-identical.

The script and profiles are original YUGA authoring work. Existing source and
evidence hashes are recorded in `source-manifest.json`. A staged overlay is an
unreviewed diagnostic, not acceptance of final art or proof of a rendering cause.
