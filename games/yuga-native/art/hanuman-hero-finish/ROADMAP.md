# Next Hanuman production rounds

The baseline for comparisons is the finished playable GLB with the matching game, Cycles views and editable studio. A candidate advances when the responsible artist and an independent reviewer can recover the difference from saved source and repeat the in-game check. [Published entry and model](README.md) · [Panel history](design/PANEL.md) · [Rebuild handoff](HANDOFF.md)

| Order | Owner and dependency | Work and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Face in motion | Character sculptor + facial animator, baseline ready | Start from the [Blender blink, focus and recovery study](expressions/README.md). Fit nostril wings, cheek-to-muzzle transition and lip corners in front, profile and three-quarter views. Add exertion and blend the accepted expressions onto moving combat poses. Supply same-camera overlays and a close-up with the eyes open, blinking and settled. Reject visible eyeball breaks, lip separation at rest or added features that obscure the tilak. |
| 2 · Cloth at the knee | Textile artist + rigger, may run alongside face work | Reproject or unwrap the trouser border locally. Capture straight stance, deep vault compression and recovery from both sides with the same cloth swatch. Inspect the border continuity at quarter speed and check the mace and trailing hem in motion. |
| 3 · Hair, shape and performance | Groom artist + rendering engineer, starts from the current model | Compare opaque locks with batched card or strand treatments around cheeks, muzzle and nape. Keep a matched close-up and an in-match shoulder view. Measure the 55/70-pixel detail transition with one and five remote Hanumans. Capture CPU/GPU frame time p50/p95/p99, VRAM and resolution on the target Legion RTX 4070 Laptop with 8 GB dedicated memory. Record driver, power profile, stage, distance and viewport. |
| 4 · Counterplay | Combat designer + playtest lead, current game can be tested immediately | Run recorded human 1v1 and 3v3 sessions. Ask defenders to identify the turn, landing mark and rear danger before impact. Compare correct reads, shield recipients and escape success under local and measured network delay. Tune telegraphs first. Change authoritative timings only with repeatable before/after results on both clients. |
| 5 · Promote and reuse | Release engineer + independent QA, follows accepted art and combat work | Assemble one candidate, validate glTF, reopen its Blender file offline, repeat clip, contact, practice and two-client checks on the extracted ZIP. Render the same camera positions and publish source SHA, receipts, movie and game together. Carry the verification tools and camera setup to the next hero while keeping each hero’s distinct anatomy and motion. |

**Start the next session** with the face animator and textile artist working independently. The rendering engineer can benchmark the shipped baseline in parallel, and the playtest lead can recruit for the current build. The first art review should put the two measured revisions beside the exact published studio shot and identify any regression in the shoulder-camera view. Physical frame pacing and commercial visual parity remain unverified. [Source and camera recipe](render/README.md)
