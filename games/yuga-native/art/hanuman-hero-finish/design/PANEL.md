# Hanuman production panel

**Mandate.** Deliver a playable Hanuman whose face, tail and worn ceremonial kit read at the title, shoulder-camera and team-fight distances. Preserve the 167-joint skeleton, physical grip, five ability clocks and the authority that resolves combat. The four-hero studio preserves the prior production model for comparison. [Before model and scripts](../../four-hero-motion/README.md) · [Portrait direction](PORTRAIT.md)

## Round one · work briefs

Add swept locks with wider roots, tapered tips and a tuft bound to the existing tail chain. | Compare matched three-quarter, front and rear views. | Combat and UI designer | A missed Mountain Pulse target and a successful cast are hard to distinguish in advance. ## Round two · engineering decisions

The first groom candidate passed structural import but introduced 8,532 additional Khronos warnings. New vertices still named secondary joints in channels whose weights were zero. The exporter now clears those indices. Five groom regions also shared a material. Merging their index ranges into one primitive reduced the draw submissions for the added groom from five to one. The region spans remain available for editing. The original skinned-mesh parent warning also appears in the baseline. [Sculpt receipt](../evidence/sculpt.json) · [First sculpt validation](../evidence/gltf-initial-sculpt.json) · [Final Khronos check](../evidence/gltf-validation.json)

The prior Mountain Pulse action remains the source for the original 3D studio film. The revised game adds visual recoil after contact. Its five ability types, input bindings and damage/healing values still come from the Rust authority and its profile. The new target indicator can predict validity from the latest snapshot. The room host evaluates the actual commit. [Tail motion design](TAIL_MOTION.md) · [Gameplay handoff](../GAMEPLAY.md)

## Round three · choose the shipping character

The matched studio photographs use the same pose, lenses and lights for the earlier and revised models. Both amber irises remain visible. The new muzzle is shorter in profile, light separates the swept locks from the cheek, and the shoulder plates pick up the engraved lines. The panel retained the third candidate. The shallow overlap at some cheek and nape hair tips and the relatively smooth central face remain visible artist follow-ups. [Matched studies](../media/images/matched-studies.webp) · [Signed clearance samples from 30 poses](../evidence/review-clearance.json) · [Art roadmap](../ROADMAP.md)

The build preserved 167 joints, all 11 actions and the original contact clocks. The largest added tail-tip movement in Mountain Pulse is 64.35 mm during recovery. The audited head, wrists, feet and gada landmarks had zero change in that motion pass. The official glTF validator found zero errors and one warning, the same skinned-node parent warning as the baseline. [Animation measurements](../evidence/combined-tail-recoil.json) · [Validator output](../evidence/gltf-validation.json)

The release check compared the character embedded in the downloadable game with the studio GLB. The room host sustained a six-player match and a separate duel and released both rooms on exit. Full-detail grooming adds one primitive. A remote Hanuman below 55 projected CSS pixels can skip its 37,584 triangles. The local player retains the full groom. Physical-device frame timing and human competitive balance remain open. ## Reuse and tool selection

- **Anatomy and weights.** Reuse the project's MakeHuman-derived asset and first-party rig, with pinned CC0 source data. This preserves joints, weapon sockets and the old clips for direct deformed-mesh comparison. [Source credits](../../hanuman/NOTICE.md) · [MakeHuman data license](../../../../yuga/art/sources/makehuman/makehuman-assets-license.md)
- **Materials and skinning.** Blender's existing glTF importer provides the artist view. Khronos’ exporter implementation documents metallic/roughness transfer and the armature sampling used throughout the project. The runtime receives a standard glTF 2.0 binary with embedded textures. [PBR exporter](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Blender-IO/blob/04ef1f08ad956545e6b4edd8528a378ad5031346/addons/io_scene_gltf2/blender/exp/material/pbr_metallic_roughness.py) · [Bone sampling](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Blender-IO/blob/04ef1f08ad956545e6b4edd8528a378ad5031346/addons/io_scene_gltf2/blender/exp/animation/sampled/armature/keyframes.py)
- **Secondary movement.** The ozz additive-pose sample and joint-attachment sample offer precedents for isolating local modifications and preserving weapon placement. YUGA applies a project-authored curve change to its own four tail joints. [Additive sample](https://github.com/guillaumeblanc/ozz-animation/blob/744eb9d99f606eda849acb0b1204f7a3dc20bca1/samples/additive/sample_additive.cc) · [Joint attachment](https://github.com/guillaumeblanc/ozz-animation/blob/744eb9d99f606eda849acb0b1204f7a3dc20bca1/samples/attach/sample_attach.cc)
- **Game and release.** Rust/Wasm resolves match state. PlayCanvas uses that state to draw the character and forecast legal targets. Blender and FFmpeg supply editor-ready scenes, actual stills and H.264 review media. The official Khronos validator runs separately after the packed GLB is written. [Confluence source](../../../../yuga-rust/README.md) · [Khronos validator, package revision](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Validator/tree/bcd52cc4ba5f333b2999a58f67cc05ddf28b4fb1)
- **Research library.** The project's pinned catalog also covers MPFB vertex weights, Blender Studio corrective pose tooling, Poly Haven LOD switching and the meshoptimizer fixtures. These are starting points for the follow-up milestones. [Searchable sources and evidence](../../../../../research/game-resource-library/README.md)
