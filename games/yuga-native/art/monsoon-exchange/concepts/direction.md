## Generation brief

**District key art.** Show the entire fictional western Indian monsoon trading quarter from a high southern vantage, with north at the back. Put the narrow shaded dyers’ route and a square indigo stepwell court on the left. Put a raised copper sun instrument and corbelled canopy on the sun court at the right. Connect it to an outer copper arcade and an inner ledger balcony. An offset weavers’ gate divides the centre. The southern ochre caravan yard fans into three route families, with a pale ashlar archive anchoring the northern side. Use red sandstone, worn lime, wet jade-toned public paving, patinated copper, matte indigo and saffron cloth. Repeated modest roof bays, watermarks, gutters, shutters and racking convey a lived-in exchange. Ask for a single continuous architectural image without labels or foreground figures.

**North-up painting.** Use the registration drawing for position, size and connections. Use the first painting for materials. Work in overhead orthographic projection. Preserve the entire southern fork, the west passage and sluice approach, the east arcade and inner short, the central offset and both return loops. Keep streets visible as pale walkable ribbons, put quieter roofs in the infill, cut away covered sections at intersections and leave a paper margin. Use an original architectural-miniature treatment with painterly dyes and brass edges. Omit lettering so the measured edition retains responsibility for labels.

**Production swatch.** Work strictly overhead with even scanner light. Fill a square with dark indigo cotton, a loose repeat of small four-petal resist rosettes, short diagonal hatch clusters and occasional muted ochre centres. Use restrained contrast and fine wear. Avoid a photographic frame, folds, branded motifs and symbols. Produce a single material square suitable for the cloth UVs.
