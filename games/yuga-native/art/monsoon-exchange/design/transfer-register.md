# What Monsoon Exchange keeps from Dust II

A useful piece of Dust II survives a change of scenery when it still gives a player a meaningful choice. From the southern Caravan Yard, a player can take the longer copper road into Sun Court, arrive there from the raised Ledger Balcony, or commit west toward Indigo Cistern. A teammate at Weavers’ Gate can still change destinations. Those relationships form the base of this map. Its dimensions, rooms, ritual sites and surfaces come from a new metre survey. ## Opening pressure and the return to a lit site

Archive Court has an **11.89 s** grounded approach to A by Sun Return and **12.31 s** to B by Cistern Return. The fastest measured arrivals from Caravan Yard are **19.24 s** at A and **19.84 s** at B. The differences of **7.35 s** and **7.53 s** give each defender a plausible interval to choose one position before the opposing center reaches the site center. First sight through a doorway can happen earlier, and five start pads produce five different departures. At A, log which of the two northern mouths players actually use. At B, log whether both western arrivals present useful choices. A short compass distance between two entrances can still hide a long walk around a facing wall. [Route traces and measurement assumptions](../plan/metrics.json) · [Seven entrance records](../plan/survey.json)

A player starting at B can return to A through the archive in **23.86 s** or through the middle in **25.36 s** along the measured routes. A successful grounded quench needs another **3.5 s**. With a fresh 40 s fire, the ideal remaining allowance is **12.64 s** on the rear route or **11.14 s** through the middle. That allowance must cover finding the placed vessel, any delay, contested ground and damage that resets the hold. Both calculations use the site center for the end of the walk. The actual vessel can lie elsewhere inside its disk. Compare those predicted margins with server time at first audible cue and arrival at the vessel in human trials. [Timed routes](../plan/metrics.json) · [Quench and interruption contract](../../../docs/design/ember-rite-5v5/ROUND_AND_TECH.md)

## Bring the artwork into the game

1. **Level engineer.** Match Blender X east, Y north, Z up to glTF X east, Y up, negative Z north. Validate two objective disks and two opposing spawn centers on the exported walk surface. Trace every approach including the low vault and dry strip, then test actor clearance of 0.65 m radius and 1.80 m standing height. Compare the scene witnesses with the measured plan. [Survey and drawing sources](../plan/) · [Independent QA](../qa/)
2. **Gameplay and network.** Bind each duty’s five surveyed pads to the five legal seats. Run the existing 20 Hz rules at the vessel's actual support height. Kindle takes 80 ticks inside the 3 m disk. Quench takes 70 ticks within 1.5 m of the vessel. The 800-tick fire clock and its final-tick quench rule also apply after a side swap. Exercise opposite elevations at close horizontal range, illegal holds behind a wall and reconnect. [Round rules](../../../docs/design/ember-rite-5v5/ROUND_AND_TECH.md)
3. **Hero and environment design.** At the tall gate screen, test Hanuman's flight visual and a 1.8 m receiver separately from his ornamental silhouette. Test Rama’s arrow and Krishna’s return path against a thick wall, a dry ledge and a valid opening at each court. Keep friendly visual ornaments out of the collision layer. The existing Mandala trajectories are a test method. Their endpoints belong to another district. [Matched hero geometry and cast fixtures](../../../docs/design/ember-rite-5v5/atlas/vertical-play/README.md)
4. **Audio, accessibility and playtest.** Ask players to identify site, near/far surface and gate direction with music low and captions on. Test the current sound policy at 18 m for a footstep, 20 m for Hanuman’s leap, 25 m for Kali’s Tandava and 30 m for a kindle onset. A nearby onset does not identify the carrier or announce a successful plant. Collect route times, wrong-way turns and actual choices at A's four entrances and B's three across side-swapped five-versus-five sessions. [Objective audio and listener rules](../../../docs/design/ember-rite-5v5/audio/README.md)
