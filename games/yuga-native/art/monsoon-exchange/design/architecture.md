# A market built around water

Caravans enter the exchange from the south with cloth and copper. An archive at the north records the consignments. Buyers cross an elevated sunlit court to weigh metal. Dyers work in the cooler western courtyard beside a cistern and shallow runnel. Weavers occupy the hinge between the trades. The story explains why each route has a different elevation, light, material and sound. Monsoon Exchange is an invented district shared by both player teams. ## How to read the district on foot

At the start centers the player faces into the market. The lower, enclosed western route promises shade and water. The rising strip of copper bays on the eastern side promises open sky. A first-time player at the center can use those two shapes to choose a site before learning the callouts. A player returning from the north reads the same two landmarks from their backs. Wear on a wall, the underside of an awning and a figure reflected in the water are useful close details. The court openings and the skyline carry the long read. ## Construction layers for the next artist

**Ground and water.** Treat the finished top surface as the datum. Site A and B sit on separate dry floors. At B, show the runnel or storage basin through its parapet profile, surface response and edge material. Collision, visual water, a wet-footstep region and the volume for a grounded quench each need a separate identity. A change to a water shader should leave the metric survey untouched. [Survey](../plan/survey.json) · [YUGA surface sound contract](../../../docs/design/ember-rite-5v5/audio/README.md)

**Walls and thresholds.** The entry record owns mouth position and clear width. Sidewalls, columns, shade and lintels can build up outside that opening. Use longitudinal sections to inspect a rising path under a lintel. A column capital that looks clear in plan can still clip a tall hero or an elevated camera. A pierced wall needs a clear gameplay decision about whether its apertures pass sight, projectiles, both or neither. The current release's physical checks belong to its verification register. [Independent checks](../qa/) · [Hero eye and body dimensions](../../../docs/design/ember-rite-5v5/atlas/vertical-play/README.md)

**Cover, props and craft detail.** Use full-height bodies for the fourteen registered entrance shelters and ornament them by use. Sun Court accepts a press, cask, weight and seal furniture. The dyers' court accepts bale storage, screens, filters and a spout. Record source mesh and scale when using a Mandala prop, and let its new host room name its placement. Cloth edges, beads, lamp brackets and decorative capstones should read as details outside the walking envelope. **Image and Blender reviews.** The floorplan settles coordinate or level disputes. Compare the generated overview with the dimensioned map for prominent landmark, direction of approach and relative mass. Compare the three-dimensional model with the actual threshold, not the painted shadow. The camera should show the district whole and then let the artist inspect Sun Court, Indigo Cistern and Weavers' Gate in their own light. ## Playtest questions

- Starting from each duty, can a new player return to the same site using its silhouette and nearest named gate after the HUD labels are hidden?
- Can a grounded common receiver reach each site from all assigned entrances while a second player uses the other local cover? Use the seat layout, the 0.65 m clearance envelope and an actual 3D contact query.
- At Witness Gate, do players recognize the difference between the adjacent A entrances in both directions? Does moving the camera to the other shoulder change which shelter actually hides the player?
- On Sluice Walk, does the player recognize water before stepping into it, and can they choose dry stone without leaving the route? Repeat with sound muted, captions enabled and the late ritual score playing.
- Does a roofed space change the way a cast is read without changing the published rules for the opponent? Test Hanuman flight clearance, a direct arrow and a returning chakra in game only after the district's collision and authority adapter are present.
