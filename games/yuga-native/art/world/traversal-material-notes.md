# Traversal landing-court material review

The actual 960 × 540 native preview in `/tmp/yuga-traversal-recovery-preview/still-sun-landing-courts.png` showed the new solid landing decks as broad, largely featureless cubes. Thin gold edge details were difficult to see at the captured gameplay distance. This observation concerns that saved preview, not a general performance or art-quality claim.

The next material revision applies `scripts/traversal_stone.gdshader` only to the eight native `SolidLandingDeck` bodies. Original procedural ashlar, shallow closed arch panels, a broad darker plinth and a warm upper band give the wall faces larger readable features. A quiet lighter top identifies the actual landing surface. The decorative arch panels retain stone infill; they do not suggest doors through the solid collision. Vayu lift and Naga tether remain invented gameplay relics, not assertions about canonical mythological powers or a surveyed historical building.

The shader uses local metre coordinates and explicit `deck_height` / `deck_width` uniforms, cached for the two existing deck dimensions. Narrow masonry joints use fragment derivatives and fade with distance. It has no texture reads, fragment loops, animation, alpha, emission or vertex displacement. Existing deck meshes, 238 landmark mesh instances, collision boxes, anchor locations and simulation timings are unchanged. The shared ground material also darkens the existing broad lane paving slightly; route masks and widths are unchanged and no glowing guide line is added.

All shader arithmetic and geometry are authored for this project. Engineering conventions follow the already reviewed official Godot 4.6 spatial shader reference recorded in [rendering-notes.md](rendering-notes.md), at `godotengine/godot-docs@b10b7ed373c2d15555186914b82e167169b5b87b`, `tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/spatial_shader.rst`, SHA-256 `cceceb2b29683254372bf8afa207f2ba0d9b89b05b035fade51af92162d8153e`. No commercial game asset, texture or shader is reused. The current milestone's bounded design/source scope is recorded in [TRAVERSAL_ARMY_SOURCES.md](../../docs/research/TRAVERSAL_ARMY_SOURCES.md).

Fresh headless validation passes 27 integrated world physics/UI checks, including shader assignment and dimensions on all eight decks plus actual landing-surface rays, and 30 lighting resource checks. These checks do not establish rendered visual quality or GPU frame rate. Root-coordinated actual rendering must compile and inspect this material revision before visual acceptance.
