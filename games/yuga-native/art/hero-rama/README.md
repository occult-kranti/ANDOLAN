# Rama hero topology revision

This is a new authored 3D hero candidate. It rebuilds smooth quad-based anatomy from the retained licensed MakeHuman data, then models the costume, swept hair, worked gold, bow and quiver as separate editable surfaces. It is not a concept painting or a claim of commercial-game quality.

The canonical direction is the left figure in `games/yuga/art/references/rama-hanuman-sita.png`: swept dark hair and tied crown, seated dark eyes, exposed chest, asymmetric cobalt silk, gathered ivory dhoti, chased leaf/lotus gold, sapphire settings, a graceful recurve and shoulder quiver. All texture maps in this directory are original deterministic mathematical surfaces. No commercial model, texture or animation has been extracted.

The reference's finished art is a direction. Actual scene renders and native gameplay review determine what this candidate accomplishes.

## Rebuild

Coordinate the Blender/export/render slot with the integration owner, then run from repository root:

```sh
blender -b -t 2 --python games/yuga-native/art/hero-rama/build_hero.py -- --output /workspace/scratch/rama-hero-art/rebuild --preview
```

The command writes `rama-hero.blend` with separate editable anatomy, cloth, hair, jewelry and weapon objects and packed PBR maps; a staged `rama-hero.glb`; a provenance/count report; and three actual Cycles studio images when `--preview` is specified. The source scene is saved before runtime merging and keeps the imported rig and nine existing actions. Generated runtime meshes are `RamaHero` and `RamaBow`. The bow is separate for integration. The reconstruction source retains the original 164 bones and parent/rest conventions.

The canonical gameplay asset remains owned by the integration worker; this generator never writes `assets/heroes/rama-production.glb` or replaces accepted historical evidence.

## Motion interface

- Same 164-joint skeleton, names, rest transforms, imported nine actions and dimensions as the existing reconstruction.
- Bow grip retains wrist.R-local center `(0, 0.105, 0.025)` metres and 0.022 m radius, 0.092 m leather length.
- Neutral string endpoints retain original wrist-frame points generated by `p(0, -0.2, +/-0.575)`; middle remains `p(0, -0.205, 0)` and is weighted to `bowstring`.
- `p(x,y,z) = wrist.R.head + wrist.R.rotation @ ((z,y,-x) + (0,0.105,0.025))` in the source Blender coordinate system.
- No nocked arrow is baked into the main mesh. Runtime motion owns that arrow and action sockets.
- Shoulder cloth blends spine, pelvis and own-side thigh weights. It is not simulated cloth.

## Deliberate implementation differences

The previous model merged a reduced triangulated body with rigid patterned panels and exposed spherical eyes. This build refines the retained continuous anatomical quad surface before deformation, rebuilds small seated eyes with almond rims, uses broad shaped overlapping hair locks, and makes layered garment volumes with thickness and asymmetric curved hems. Chased petals, granulated chains, inset gems, solid vambrace shells and shaped greaves give jewelry actual volume. Fine PBR weave and modeled edges serve different viewing scales.

## Remaining gates

Actual front/face/rear studio review and any resulting correction are required. Gameplay must independently verify anatomy/costume intersections, grip, string contact, continuous movement, lighting, camera readability and imported materials. There are no facial blendshapes, hair strand simulation, cloth physics, LODs, or measured physical-GPU budgets in this packet.

## Review corrections

Actual image iterations rejected the first crown's symmetrical raised rolls and embedded eyebrows, then the flat secondary chest strip and repeated nape curtain. Surface raycasts now seat the eyebrows, native lip polygons carry a palette-matched tint, the scalp follows the actual anatomical head, and overlapping wavy hair masses are unioned and polished into a continuous sculpted surface. The secondary chest strip became a draped chain. A raised rosette and chased scrolls decorate each shoulder cap. The tapered front dhoti has diagonal tension folds; the outer blue wrap has clearance over the gathered trousers.

The candidate is a detailed single-hero prototype. Its high mesh/material cost is not an accepted twelve-player art budget. No authored LODs or physical-GPU performance measurements are included; any engine-generated LODs must be reported separately by integration.

The editable source is independently reopened with `verify_editable.py`. It compares all rest matrices/parents and every retained baseline animation curve with the pinned reconstruction, then evaluates 27 posed mesh-bound samples. `validate_hero.py` independently reads the exported GLB to check embedded texture payloads, required joint/nodes/actions, finite geometry, index bounds, tangent availability, unit normals and normalized four-influence weights. These structural checks do not replace actual visual or motion review.

## Central drape binding correction

The first actual native strafe revealed a bilateral nearest-leg weighting seam in the broad ivory sheet. `rebind_dhoti.py` now supplies a continuous spine05-dominant binding for the central sheet and its two edge meshes: 84–100% pelvis, at most16% combined upper-thigh influence and no knee/lowerleg influence. The main generator imports that same function.

The shipped R7 is a bounded binary weight patch of the accepted R6 geometry. `weight-correction.json` proves that all bytes outside the selected joint/weight records are unchanged, and records the exact source/output hashes. The patched editable source was reopened and verified. A fresh full Blender export may differ in binary ordering/deduplication; exact byte reproducibility is not claimed. Actual R7 motion review owns the remaining deformation/clearance gate.
