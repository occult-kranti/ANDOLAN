# C5 — connected swept sculpt contract

C5 is original editable coarse hair geometry for native YUGA, not a concept
substitute or game asset promotion. The source owner reopened the generated
continuous-swept-hair concept and all four final C4 actual originals before this
construction. C1–C4, their rejections and the canonical hero remain untouched.

C4's three main failures are geometrically distinct: a vertical gather section
stack produces a peg inside the thin fixed tie; near-round rear sections produce
two independent inflated straps; overlapping concentric side volumes produce
helmet panels. Extra grooves would preserve all three problems.

C5 is one connected closed editable cage, with shared edges between scalp,
gather and rear mass. The only reused support is C4's thin anatomy-seated scalp
coverage. Its separate sweeps, gather, tails and voxel union are discarded. The
new outer surface is sculpted directly into an asymmetric low diagonal crest.
A rear-crown patch is extruded obliquely into a horizontally spread, filled bun
with two unequal returning shoulders and a shallow depressed top. Its highest
prepared vertex is 1.890 m, approximately 19 mm above the fixed tie's top. Its
width/depth spread dominates its vertical rise. There is no loop hole, vertical
post, separate connector or new hardware.

The nape edge extends into a single closed layered rear mass. Broad unequal
folds travel across its surface as the entire mass follows an asymmetric S.
Front and back planes have variable approximately 8–20 mm total depth before
the long terminal wedge; they are not circular sections. Staggered lower reaches
and a changing center line supply asymmetry within that shared surface. Fine
strands and decorative grooves remain deferred until the major forms read.

The gold tie stays at glTF Y 1.865–1.871 m, centered near (0, 1.868, −0.040).
Head/face, brows, crown, tie, rest-space rig/reference geometry, material values,
lights, world, frame, four cameras and render settings are protected. Inner
seating may intersect anatomy; visible skin breakthrough and hardware fit are
judged in all four actual views. No blanket zero-contact loop is introduced.

The next gate is one editable Blender save, fresh protected-reference and
camera-fit checks, then W1's four actual 1024×1024 authoring views. Front must
show a low asymmetric sweep and lobed bun without a peg. Profile must show a
continuous oblique scalp-to-gather gesture and flowing rear silhouette. Rear
must read as one layered mass with unequal taper, with no pair of padded
straps. Oblique must preserve the same gesture and readable crown/tie.
These views are authoring clay, not animated game cast evidence.

At most one bounded correction may follow findings from that actual review.
There is no runtime GLB promotion without independent all-view approval. No
commits, roadmap/shared notices or canonical model edits belong to this worker.
Blender source work and raster wait for W1 lane admission and >500 MiB free;
lightweight pure-Python cage preparation is allowed before that admission.
