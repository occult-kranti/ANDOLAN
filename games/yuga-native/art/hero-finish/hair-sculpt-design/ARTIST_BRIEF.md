# Whole-hair sculpt brief

**Target:** a low asymmetric crown sweep grows into a folded knot and two broad falling S-shaped masses. The hair reads as one gathered hairstyle before fine grooves or strand detail. Keep the Rama identity and visible crown, forehead, eyes, brows and ears.

Design proposal only: no new GLB, mesh, generator variation, cache or canonical change. Frozen R4–R6 sources and actual rejections remain intact.

## Why the parameter loop failed

| Attempt | Construction choice | Actual failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R4 | Reduced the principal mass; retained the old knot | Floating knot and separate rear blades. |
| R5 | Added a compact connection | Closed gap, but a perched knot on a stalk. |
| R6 | Widened the gathered root and regrouped locks | Horizontal rear puck, cast bulb and separate scalloped nape. |

The fixed knot and independently constructed cap/locks were a poor design constraint. Wider contact strengthened an extra intermediate form. Repeated tapered tubes produced a toothed silhouette. I treated broader attachment as sufficient source support; the images reject that design assumption. Preservation/contact checks cannot choose convincing proportion or flow.

Use the [original Rama direction](../../../../yuga/art/references/rama-hanuman-sita.png) for its gathered sweep, asymmetry and overlapping fall. The [R6 full originals](../../../docs/evidence/desktop-finish/hair-collected-r6/W1-native-r1/) are the failure reference. No reference pixels or image-derived geometry enter an asset.

## One construction: an artist-authored continuous cage sculpt

Author one principal-hair cage in Blender over locked reference anatomy. Name its scalp, gather, knot and fall regions; shared surface and edge flow carry the design. The old knot's proportion, contour and fold direction may change. Its gold tie remains fixed. Collect the hair through that anchor without another horizontal shelf.

Begin with broad planes: one deliberate side part sweeps from forehead over crown into the gathered root. Bend and fold those same flow strips into the knot. Continue the posterior surface into two slightly asymmetric S-curves, separating mainly near the ends. Shape ear visibility with negative space. Use cage edits and broad sculpt strokes to establish this contour.

Then carve a few long grooves along the established flow, continuing through the gathered region into the knot. Add restrained overlapping accent tips only after the main form works. Avoid another cap/connector/tube assembly, posterior ellipsoid, random noise or extra strands as the composition fix.

## Required reading at the four views

| View | Visual target |
| --- | --- |
| Front | Intentional swept hairline beneath the crown; restrained asymmetric face framing. No plain mini-cap or helmet lobe. |
| Anatomical-left profile | One rising scalp-to-knot contour returning into a downward fall. No stalk, rear bulb, ledge or blade fan. |
| Rear | Knot emerges from gathered hair; long transitions lead into overlapping falls with staggered ends. No knot/puck/bob stack or repeated scallops. |
| Actual cast | Same coherent shape against bright sky, without a perched bun, mushroom cap or isolated points. Crown, face and bow stay clear. |

Before another GLB, produce one silhouette/flow sheet aligned to the four frozen views. Judge outline and interior flow together. After selecting that shape, review one coarse neutral-clay cage before detail or the full eligibility packet. Clay is a labeled design view; the later actual public-cast framebuffer remains mandatory.

## Source boundary

Editable: principal scalp, temple/nape and existing topknot hair components. Preserve eyebrow records, head/face, accepted crown and gold tie, every nonhair surface, rig/binds, animations/morphs, materials/textures and accepted sash UVs. Show a conflict with the fixed tie before altering a protected asset.

Keep an original artist master and reproducible export recipe. Derive three LODs from the approved master; inject only principal-hair arrays into the accepted-UV template, keeping eyebrow/nonhair bytes exact. Reuse existing binding/material infrastructure and declare mask changes. Then repeat contact, recoil/draw, secondary, strict import and fresh actual visual gates. R4–R6 passes do not transfer to a redesigned knot or fall. Counts and closed intersections remain eligibility facts, never artistic acceptance.
