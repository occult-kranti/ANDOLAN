# C6 coarse original block sculpt

The owner read the artist brief and C1–C5 rejection notes, then directly opened all twenty final locked-view originals and the gathered-hair concept. C1/C2 are hanging sheets below a smooth cap; C3 introduces real depth but sparse petals, exposed scalp and two padded falls; C4 closes coverage while retaining a peg, panels and two inflated locks; C5 changes those locks into broad folded plates while the helmet and capped neck remain. Source connectivity, fit and camera counts did not resolve those shapes.

C6 starts with new coarse polygon geometry. No C1–C5 hair vertices are reused. A new 7×11 outer scalp quad net seats directly against rays into the fixed actual head, with a buried inner surface and a deliberately asymmetric diagonal control flow. The three falling regions and folded gather are explicit closed polygon box sculptures stored as final control nodes/faces in `control-nets.json.gz`; nineteen additional individual node edits turn their corners, shoulders and returns in three dimensions. The builder reads those meshes rather than retaining an axial radius/section or loft construction loop. Buried face bridges join them to one editable principal cage, with unapplied subdivision2. Hidden seating intersections are allowed; topology is not visual approval.

The gather has broad shoulders beneath the fixed tie and an unequal oblique folded top, with a lowered central return. The major rear fall travels right then back left, the subordinate crosses toward a shorter right terminal, and a short left nape return breaks the silhouette at a third unequal height. Their rounded depths and long terminal tapers are part of the coarse meshes. No strand detail, groove overlay, surface texture, UV, rig or GLB work is included.

The fixed tie's radial aperture measures 32.075 mm minimum about `(x=0,z=−.040)` in glTF metres, versus C5's artificial 25.5 mm passage restriction. Its vertical extent is 1.865–1.871 m; the fixed head maximum is 1.84855 m. C6 uses approximately 30 mm of control halfwidth through the aperture and folds obliquely above it.
