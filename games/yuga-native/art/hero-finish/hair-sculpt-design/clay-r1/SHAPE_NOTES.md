# One editable whole-hair clay cage

This is a coarse sculpt target following the accepted concept direction, with a **lower folded gather**, not a new runtime asset. The exact concept is preserved in the adjacent `concept-r1` folder. R4–R6 remain rejected and unchanged.

The principal surface is one connected cage. A patch of the fitted upper rear scalp is removed and its existing boundary edges continue through the tied gather into the new low fold. The rear hairline edges continue into two broad asymmetric falling surfaces. These are shared mesh edges, not coincident components, added tubes, an ellipsoid union, or a plug beneath the original knot. The old knot is absent from the clay. There are no strand grooves, UVs, skin weights, textures, LODs, or exported GLBs.

The fold aims to finish near 1.900 m, roughly 20 mm below the original upright knot. Its section passes through the fixed tie inside its existing aperture. One dominant fall overlaps a shorter returning fall; their lower separation is an intentional broad shape. A coarse cage and a non-applied thickness/subdivision setup remain editable. The shell boundary is explicit; it is not yet a production watertight hair asset.

Reference anatomy, brows, accepted crown/tie, and nearby clothing are copied directly from the accepted UV near-LOD parent into locked authoring objects. They are not resculpted or exported. Coordinates are recorded in glTF metres; the Blender scene uses the rigid mapping `(x, y, z) -> (x, -z, y)` without scale changes. The source GLB remains untouched.

Before fine work, review four real clay renders: front, anatomical-left profile, rear, and cast-angle. These are labeled authoring views, not game captures or proof of cast clearance. Check the whole outline: no puck or stalk, no tall perched bun, no repeated nape scallops, no isolated blade fan. The tie, diadem, face and ear openings must remain readable. A connected cage or a passing contact check alone cannot accept the form.

Source checks cover finite cage geometry, shared connectivity/manifold edges, and rest-space distances to reference head/gold. They do not inherit R6 action/contact passes. No animated, secondary-motion, bow-draw, import, material or runtime qualification is claimed. Stop after this one cage and its four-view review; a visible failure is a design hold, not a reason to generate more parameter variants.
