The change from thin sheets to actual volumetric falls is real, but it does not
accept the sculpt. C3 assigned both coverage and silhouette to six sparse
standalone volumes. Three oversized scalp lofts cannot cover the anatomy while
also producing convincing subordinate clumps. Their capped ends are visible
at the temples. A path bent into a loop produced a torus-like silhouette;
short terminal section spacing left padded rather than tapered fall ends.
Fine grooves would decorate these failures without correcting them.

The earlier strict contact failures remain evidence. Inward-facing scalp
contacts can be buried seating. The actual images also show crown/tie
discontinuities, so those hardware intersections are not qualified as harmless.
No animation, deformation, full-body containment or runtime conclusion follows.

## One bounded successor construction

First preserve this exact rejected scene, source cages, derived mesh, helpers,
actual originals and independent diagnostics through W3's recovery map.

The successor uses a continuous thin scalp underlayer with an explicit hairline
and ear cutouts. Its task is coverage; it must sit close to the existing head
and must not become an inflated helmet. A few shallow, broad, tapering sweep
ridges grow along that underlayer and overlap into the gather. Their roots and
trailing edges merge into hair, with no exposed rounded end caps at the temples.

The gather becomes a filled tucked fold, with one broad returning edge and a
shallow depression rather than a through-hole. The fixed tie, head and crown
remain untouched. Posterior coverage continues into one dominant and one
subordinate S fall; both retain cross-sectional depth, then taper in width and
depth over their terminal length instead of ending in short capped lobes.

One successor and the same four actual cameras are the next bounded gate:
continuous intended scalp coverage, directional planes rather than giant petals,
no donut, tapered staggered falls, and readable temple rail/tie. Buried roots
are allowed; visible skin breakthrough and exposed hardware discontinuities
must be judged from images. No detail, UV, rig, export or runtime work is implied.
