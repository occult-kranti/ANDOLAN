The continuous seated scalp closes the conspicuous bare bands of C3. Temple
roots are substantially more continuous, the fixed crown rail remains visible,
and the two falls now narrow over a longer terminal length. These are genuine
bounded improvements, not acceptance of the hair design.

The first C4 take still showed a hollow upright fold and a central upper-fall
split. Its exact scene `a4428ee5…`, executed builder, source cages, derived mesh,
checks and actual images remain preserved. The sole correction replaces the
loop path with a filled root through the fixed tie and moves the upper falls
inward. The scalp and all six sweep cages are byte-identical across takes.

In the final profile/oblique, the gather is now rooted and filled, but reads as
a smooth upright peg or spire with a horizontal ring around it. It does not read
as a compact returning tucked fold. Front retains that peg above two broad cap
lobes. The rear central split closes, but two large inflated overlapping locks
still dominate the back and neck. Their terminal taper does not resolve their
banana-like bulk. Side sweeps read as broad smooth stacked helmet panels;
directional creases alone do not produce the concept's gathered flow.

This is a major-form rejection. More grooves or fine strands would decorate the
mismatch. The protected-reference, manifold and camera-fit checks pass; they do
not overrule the actual images. Buried scalp seating is allowed, and this review
does not manufacture a strict no-contact gate or claim full hardware/deformation
qualification. The rest-space reference rig, crown, tie, head, material values,
lights and cameras were not changed.

## Design constraints for a separate bounded successor task

Preserve the continuous thin seated coverage, hairline/ear visibility and useful
terminal taper. Replace the peg with a broad asymmetric folded return whose
upper edge turns across the gather, with a shallow depression and no hollow
ring. Establish one uninterrupted scalp-to-gather silhouette at the fixed tie
before detail. The actual r2 peg shows that filling a loop with a vertical
section stack solves the hole while preserving the wrong gesture.

Reauthor broad scalp/gather/fall planes together in an editable continuous cage
rather than expecting independent loft sections and voxel union to establish
convincing gesture. Make one dominant rear fall and a distinctly subordinate
return, with depth varying along the S bend and a long taper. The back should
read as gathered directional flow before secondary locks. Do not inflate the
seated underlayer to hide weak gather connections or treat more source parts as
proof of better sculpture. The ongoing game/hero work already has user authorization. These constraints
belong to a separate bounded sculpt task; this C4 construction packet is closed.
