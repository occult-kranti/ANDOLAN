# R6: a collected swept hairstyle, not another connector

The eight fresh R5 originals show the decision clearly. In rear and bright-sky cast, the added bridge closes the literal gap but leaves the unchanged knot perched above a tiny cap. In profile and cast, separate near-horizontal locks project behind the ear. The current baseline has too much helmet-like mass, yet its sweep, knot and hanging locks form one readable hairstyle. The original Rama reference also gathers broad swept hair into the knot and groups the falling locks; it does not balance a knot on a stem.

Build one new candidate from the exact accepted crown/sash-UV trio, retaining original eyebrows and topknot records. Replace the principal scalp and swept locks as a coherent set. Do not append another R5 plug. R4 and R5 remain sealed rejected history.

The foundation will keep its existing fitted head relief. A localized smooth collected envelope at the upper rear will merge into that same foundation, broad across the scalp and narrowing into the existing knot root. Starting control envelope: center (0, 1.822, −0.035) m with radii (0.070, 0.035, 0.057) m before fitted foundation relief. This is a local shape union over the actual unmodified head, not global scalp inflation. It should reach about 75 mm width at the knot's lower-root elevation, rather than R5's roughly 50 mm connector. The knot/tie positions stay exact. Measure the final stored triangles and gold clearance; these control values are not acceptance proof.

Six shallow overlapping crown sweeps will carry the front hairline toward the collected rear. Their ends will enter the gathered mass rather than becoming isolated tips. Keep the diadem, forehead, eyebrows, eyes and ears readable; preserve the existing left/right asymmetry instead of multiplying thin strands. Two modest temple groups frame the face. Seven overlapping posterior groups will fall mainly downward/backward behind the ears, with staggered tapered ends and a small returning curl. Reduce posterior throw relative to R4; avoid its separated horizontal blades and the baseline's broad blunt curtain. Preserve the existing material and head-only rig binding. Upper hair stays alpha zero; posterior tips retain the explicitly declared 0.35 alpha cap, with their contact/stress checks rerun.

Source bounds: existing knot and brows exact; every nonhair accessor, source JSON outside appended hair references, rig, morph, animation, crown, sash UV, texture and material exact. Proposed generated principal hair stays within x±0.13 m, y[1.56, 1.865] m, z[−0.19, 0.15] m. The localized collected foundation must stay below y1.863 m. Any bound or full-triangle head/gold/cloth/hand/bow failure is a hold, not permission to move the original anatomy, crown or knot. Three LODs use the same authored guides at declining resolution; no strand multiplication or new shader.

Actual four-view acceptance is visual. Front: a deliberate swept hairline below the crown, clear eyes/brows/ears, no bare mini-cap. Profile: one continuous scalp-to-knot rise and overlapping locks that fall rather than stick out as blades. Rear: a broad collected root supports the retained knot, without a stalk, isolated plug or black mushroom cap; staggered locks read as groups, not disconnected prongs. Cast: the same rooted shape survives against bright sky, with no gap, perched knot, new gold/head/bow contact or straight blade fan. Inspect full originals against the current canonical baseline with the same cameras, pose and light. Source counts and clearance alone cannot pass this art gate.
