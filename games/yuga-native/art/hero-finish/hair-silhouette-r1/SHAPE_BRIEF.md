# One inactive principal-hair silhouette revision

## Evidence and direction

Viewed the original project reference `games/yuga/art/references/rama-hanuman-sita.png`, especially Rama at left, and all four current S4 front/profile/rear/cast originals. Reference Rama has an off-centre backward sweep, small overlapping lock groups, readable face-framing gaps and tapered irregular nape ends. The existing native front shows a solid cap above the crest; profile/cast show large rounded crown lobes and a uniform dark wall behind the ear. Rear ends form broad blunt fingers. These are shape defects; extra surface noise or sparse wisps cannot fix them. Crown refits are separate and remain sealed.

The reference is existing visual direction only. No image pixels, commercial meshes or generated concept geometry will be extracted. Anatomy, original hair material/texture, rig and previous groom infrastructure retain their existing project/MakeHuman CC0 provenance.

## The single proposed shape

Keep the existing thin skull-fitted foundation, exact original eyebrows and tied topknot. Replace the five oversized crown groups with six shallower swept groups: unequal widths, slightly off-centre paths, lower relief over the cap and tuck-under ends at the knot. Forehead/eyes/brows and crest remain open. Two short side groups turn behind the temples, opening space around the ear instead of crossing the cheek.

Nine nape groups retain a continuous rooted mass but narrow to leaf-like ends, staggered in length and curled backward rather than ending in nine equally thick cylinders. Control the silhouette with the group paths; do not thicken the entire scalp or add filament noise. The existing material is shared, opaque and unchanged. Near/mid/far use the same authored guides at declining resolution; 17 bounded groups is one more than the current 16, with a lower target triangle budget.

The lower nape is the critical contact region. Preserve a visible rear length while narrowing/turning it away from neck and collar. Root/crown/temple alpha stays zero. Only lower nape uses a reduced bounded secondary weight; existing head binding and secondary owner remain. No runtime writer or helper activation is added.

## Acceptance before import

Append-only GLB replacement of the hair primitive; all original binary payloads remain recoverable, all nonhair references/materials/skins/animations/morphs remain exact. Retained brows/topknot arrays and indices must be exact. New UV/TBN/indices/positions must be finite, unit/orthogonal, nondegenerate and match one existing head joint. Three LODs must preserve the same guide shape and declining triangle counts.

Test full triangles against scalp/face/eyes/ears/brows and original crown, then against source-rest neck and collar. Replay source geometry on retained actual imported bone matrices and the exact 348-sample bow-draw hand tape; include the known measured-step recoil failure rather than omitting it. Bounded secondary stress is separate from nominal posed contact. A failed gate is retained and corrected before this candidate is eligible for private import. Headless/source tests do not prove the desired appearance.

## Render decision

After W1 finishes the smooth-crown comparison, use separate sealed children with only this hair replacement and the original crown. Compare full original front/profile/rear/cast frames at the same camera/light/pose. Look for a swept layered silhouette rather than helmet lobes, tapered asymmetric ends, clear face/crest and no detached wire strands, scalp gaps or draw clipping. Reject a shorter sterile cap, exaggerated volume, plastic rope shapes or loss of the tied-hair identity. Smooth crown and hair stay separate until each is reviewed, then need a new composite contact/visual check.

## Parent revision after the crown decision

The original brief above preceded crown promotion. Following root's accepted four-view smooth-crown R2 review, R3 and later hair attempts derive the exact accepted three-LOD smooth-crown trio, rather than the historical band. The eventual pair therefore holds smooth R2 crown constant and compares original hair with this new hair. Full head skin, brows/topknot and crown bytes remain unchanged. Historical-band compatibility is retained only as a separate diagnostic. This supersedes the earlier proposed original-band render pairing.

The new nape's maximum secondary alpha is explicitly reduced from the old groom's 0.78 to 0.35. This is a candidate behavior change, not inherited motion acceptance. Actual R2-parent measured-step recoil and separate opposing secondary-offset stresses are required before import eligibility.
