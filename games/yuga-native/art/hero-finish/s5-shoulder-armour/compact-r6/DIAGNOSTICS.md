# Compact shoulder diagnostics

This directory is an inactive successor to the sealed `../candidate-r4/` packet.
Its local candidate numbers identify compact revisions, not promoted hero assets.
The original52-file receipt remains exact. No canonical GLB, rig, material,
controller, package notice or runtime caller is modified here.

The reference calls for a substantial anatomical-left gold/cobalt shoulder.
The current hero has a shallow original cap, partly obscured by the sash.
The proposal replaces that exact old cap; layering a new rigid plate over it
introduced avoidable collisions and duplicate silhouette.

`full-smooth-*.json` are complete triangle comparisons against all18 original
primitive groups, excluding only the exactly superseded rigid50 cap in gold,
darkgold and sapphire. Each chosen LOD uses its actual source geometry. The
348-sample tape is retained direct-API evaluation under motion8c377; it is not a
continuous native modifier/render test. The additional37 samples use the exact
accepted smooth-crown R2 import and actual named-bone poses for all five casts.
The face mesh is neutral in these CPU geometry checks; no facial-pixel claim is
made. Secondary displacement is not applied in those nominal reports. The separate
`secondary-stress-r5.json` now evaluates120 bounded admitted-material fixtures.
Skin is not an admitted secondary material and never receives a cloth stress offset.

Near/mid compact-r3 source minimum skin gaps across the348 draw samples are
1.787/1.886mm. The nocked-arrow capsule/AABB lower bounds are174.070/174.151mm.
These measurements are historical and do not transfer to a revised shape.
The failed idle/recoil tape overrides any broad acceptance inferred from them.

The compact-r5 design uses the already-clear cap envelope for both layers:
gold occupies envelope v0–.78; cobalt occupies v.68–1 with a6mm radial layer step
for its3mm wall and separation. It removes the downward arm wrap, keeping one
ordinary rigid49 binding and no new bone writer. Its final source checks,
isolated replacement assembly, actual import and matched native pictures remain
separate gates. No claim of improved game graphics follows from this document.

`replace_left_cap.py` was executed for the historical smooth-crown assemblies.
The first assembly's new normal conversion failed its orthogonality check;
`replacement-r5/` and its failed verifier are retained. The corrected inverse-
transpose conversion produces `replacement-r6/`, and `compose_accepted_uv.py`
produces `replacement-r7/` on the accepted sash-UV parent without shape changes.
The exact stored proofs and all3 Khronos results pass; import and pixels remain open.

original BIN as a dead prefix, removes only original rigid50 cap triangles,
