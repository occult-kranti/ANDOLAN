reference guided form only; no commercial mesh, extracted model or external
texture is included. See the preserved `../NOTICE.md` for the original sculpture
and dependency attribution. Its old action-failure disposition applies to that
sealed predecessor, not an automatic verdict on any successor.

replacement-r7 retains the accepted smooth-crown R2 plus sash-UV R2 source body,

sculpture to existing material primitives. All original upstream notices remain
applicable; this file grants no additional rights in those resources or the
reference. No new texture or material shader is introduced.
