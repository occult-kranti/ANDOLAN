This is the second verified-batch workstream requested on 17 October 2026. The
[batch ledger](MYTHIC_BATCHES.md) maps ten bounded packets to the larger
[production backlog](ROADMAP_BACKLOG.json). Both batches passed their bounded reviews. Publication at `yuga-traversal-armies-2026-10-17-mythic` remains the final step; this milestone does not accept the entire production game.

## Play the new practice abilities

Open **Explore Confluence**, press **F3**, choose an invocation and select **Use
invocation**. **X** is the default cast key; change it through **F2 → Controls**.
The pause menu also opens the selector. Existing saved key assignments survive
the added action: if X was already assigned, the new action takes a free key.
The HUD and selector display the actual current binding.

Rama currently channels all fourteen invocations. Select Rama, Ravana, Arjuna,
Duryodhana, Hanuman, Karna, Bhima, Meghnad, Sita, Draupadi, Subhadra, Hidimbi,
Chitrangada or Ulupi to practice their native troop payload. These are fourteen
abilities through one avatar, not fourteen completed production hero models.

Cast while grounded on the street. Preparation locks movement and weapon fire;
the accepted army-clock release restores control. Every invocation shares a
12-second cooldown, including after changing selection. Pause, focus loss and
overview suspend the simulation. Travel cancels an active invocation while
preserving its cooldown. Restart restores the practice round and clears owned
effects, statuses and temporary guards.

Damage, healing, shields, slows and summoned guards act on living lane troops.
Hero health/death, structure spell damage and native online matches remain open.
The selector describes the actual supported payload and warning shape. Its
**ART DIRECTION** text describes the more ambitious creative brief separately.
Use its saved **Reduced effects** and **Reduced motion** options to reduce visual
ornament; critical warning footprints remain. The pause-menu volume controls
the new cues alongside existing sound.

See [practice rules](ULTIMATE_PRACTICE.md), [effects](ULTIMATE_EFFECTS.md) and
[selector behavior](INVOCATION_UX.md) for exact implementation boundaries.

## What the two batches change

The first five packets establish fourteen creative hero/ultimate directions,
an editable reconstruction of Rama and corrected surfaces, an upper-body bow
carry, original world ornaments/lighting, and explicit version contracts. The
[first acceptance review](evidence/mythic-batch1/REVIEW.md) retains actual images,
motion evidence, rejected attempts and unresolved anatomy/contact/art issues.

The second five add a native entity presentation bridge, an executed 3D physics
comparison, the fourteen troop invocation lifecycles, distinct original effect
constructions and short synthesized cues, and the selector/input/return flow.
The [entity bridge](NATIVE_ENTITY_BRIDGE.md) validates ownership, aim and lifetime;
it has no sockets or damage authority. The [physics decision](PHYSICS_AUTHORITY_DECISION.md)
recommends one headless Godot authority for the next native online prototype
after measuring real differences from a TypeScript query reference.

Native practice content is explicitly revision 2. Browser/proposal content and
input/event/entity/replay wire versions retain their separate versions. Old
native content descriptors reject without partially replacing accepted state.

## Quality boundary and next production work

The effects have distinct, bounded silhouettes, but are still sparse geometric
studies. Ulupi's braid reads largely as a luminous ring at distance; fine warning
lines need stronger distant readability, and Karna/Bhima need greater visual
separation. The hero's face, hair, cloth and weapon contact require further
authored work. A concept painting is not evidence of those improvements in play.

The next production gates remain continuous hero motion/contact, an adopted
native authority/controller profile, two actual native clients with prediction
and reconciliation, full match/hero combat state, and authored production
character/environment/effect work. The backlog retains unmet dependencies.
Software Vulkan captures establish pixels and timing in the recorded scene;
they do not establish consumer GPU performance, Windows execution, human
balance or listening quality, or Deadlock/Overwatch graphics parity.
