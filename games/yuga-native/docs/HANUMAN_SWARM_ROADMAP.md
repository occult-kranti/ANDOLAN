# Hanuman production roadmap

The 25 November 2026 checkpoint is [`12b56789` on `game/yuga-hanuman-trial`](https://github.com/occult-kranti/ANDOLAN/tree/12b5678915e688ee99baa8170bb35de30c7affb0). It contains an offline Godot trial, an editable rig and eleven clips, profile-driven combat, and a separate Blender look with the generated cloth mapped to the model. Five prototype passes are complete. The next production gates address facial performance and fur, contact in motion, camera readability, delivery and measured performance. The playable GLB and the Blender comparison scene have separate materials and acceptance evidence. [Start with the handoff](HANUMAN_HANDOFF.md) · [Five-pass decision record](research/hanuman-nov25/PANEL.md) · [Final motion and effects review](research/hanuman-nov25/independent-rounds-4-5.md) · [Blender look and receipts](../art/hanuman/lookdev/README.md)

Run two panels of two people in each production round. **Panel A** pairs the character artist and technical animator. **Panel B** pairs the combat presentation designer and independent verifier. The author makes one bounded change. The peer opens the exact artifact, tries to disprove its acceptance claim, and takes the next edit after an explicit file handoff. Roles alternate within each panel. The lead owns shared interfaces, the render queue, integration and published evidence. Both panels can inspect, test and prepare work together. Queue Blender, Godot imports and software rendering to keep heavy work from competing for the same CPU.

The temple court work supplies a new high-detail keyframe for H1. The paired skull and palm studies now have measured rest symmetry and a belt-hand pose. Continue the face from the eyelid, muzzle and chin planes in the portrait and profile. Test the rigid cuirass with shoulder lift and the tail tuft during a fast turn in H2. Compare the cloth weave and calf silhouette at gameplay distance before export. Budget the dense groom, fitted cloth, light rig and stone independently before granting any production asset promotion. Each author hands over the source path and hash, tool and version, affected interfaces, exact command, original evidence, failure found, change made, and remaining limit. The peer verifies the pin, reruns a case that could fail, and adds the revised artifact or documents the acceptance limit. The lead checks the normal entry path and ships the smallest bundle that another panel can open and reproduce.

Keep rig roles, socket roles, ability phases and material slots in separate manifests as compatibility grows. A team can then reuse a named action while another team changes only the material for a specific camera. The current Hanuman GLB contains fourteen image entries with ten unique byte payloads. A future packaging pass can test shared storage for the equal payloads after checking color space, normal decoding and material slot behavior. The current capture identifies the fourteen extracted files by their original bytes.

The team can use the installed OpenCode Game Studios `art-bible`, `team-combat` and `perf-profile` references with the repo’s YUGA hero, motion and evidence skills. For browser systems, verify local module behavior first and then use documented, version-pinned examples.
