## Remaining acceptance work

**Artist/animator** owns the staged Blender file, body performance, grip, cloth and tail, and proposes camera-ready exports. **Runtime designer/verifier** owns the Godot actor, ability cues, test driver and receipt. Both inspect the artifact at each round. The author gives their peer the exact candidate and health/contact case. Swap file ownership explicitly if the peer takes the next edit. Keep one owner on the shared profile and run costly exports, imports and renders in sequence. ## Reuse and limits

- **Rig and art.** Start from the R5 skeleton, contact markers, action names and base meshes. Reuse already-attributed materials and the existing court kit where they suit the scene. A new costume or hero keeps its own silhouette, hand-to-prop relation, and performance. [Art and provenance](../art/hanuman/README.md)
- **Geometry, timed texture and shadows.** Use the accepted warning boundaries for exact shape, moving geometry for direction and elevation, and the reusable floor atlas for eligible marks after impact. Keep the shadow footprint independent of any combat zone. Compare on the limestone first. Multiple floors, steps and a pose-projected proxy need their own receiver and occlusion evidence. [Rendering techniques and measurement protocol](research/HANUMAN_INDIE_RENDER_TECHNIQUES.md)
- **Performance.** Record unchanged routes at 1280 × 800 on a physical Steam Deck or declared target device before selecting a shipping preset. Log the rendering backend, power and render settings, frame-time distribution, draw calls, texture uploads and visible errors. The current Blender triangle comparison and Forward+ software captures establish their own view and device scope. [Published technique evidence](research/hanuman-indie-runtime/README.md)
- **Next product milestone.** Consider an original Kishkindha encounter after the court clips and terrain contract are accepted. Browser startup and multiplayer authority are separate backlog work. Run hosted WSS, two actual clients, a full match and reconnect before closing an online gate.
