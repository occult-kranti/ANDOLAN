# YUGA — Triad relays and siege practice

The native Confluence arena is now 140 × 260 m with longer three-lane routes, eight landable decks, ten tether anchors and animated spear soldiers, bow guards and banner commanders. Paired waves fight, threaten cores and award once-only player last-hit gold. Reset starts a new local practice round. Cannon carts reinforce every third wave and royal chariots every fifth, subject to paired spawn clearance/capacity. Their committed ground blasts have visible anticipation and original sounds.

**New relay loop:** Reed, Lotus and Sky relays can be captured by heroes and soldiers. Opposing teams contest control; holding a relay supplies up to three arrows and six troop health every five seconds, with range, height and line-of-sight checks. Original stone/brass stations and angular paving distinguish these objectives from the revised segmented siege warnings.

Extract the entire Linux or Windows ZIP. Run `launch.sh` or `launch.bat`, then choose **Explore Confluence**. Linux execution is reviewed separately from Windows archive validation. The default Forward+ renderer requires Vulkan; `--rendering-method gl_compatibility` is a reduced-effects fallback.

**Try a relay:** Explore Confluence → Escape → Visit Lotus relay. Enter the marked zone and hold for eight seconds alone, or four with a soldier. Enemy ownership must be neutralized first. Capture and supply stop on pause, overview, round end and during the siege drill.

**Try the vehicles immediately:** Explore Confluence → Escape → Siege practice. F5 restores ordinary waves. The drill uses the same troop combat rules; siege blasts currently damage troops, not the hero.

Default controls: WASD move, mouse aim, left click bow, Space jump/rise, Ctrl descend, Shift sprint, Q pulse, E dash, R reload, F toggle flight, G tether/release, F1 overview, F2 help/remap, N daylight, Escape pause, F5 reset. Settings retain sound, sensitivity and custom bindings. Flight and tether need energy and a clear target.
