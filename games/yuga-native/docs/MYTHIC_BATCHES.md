Open **Explore Confluence → F3**, choose a legend and select **Use invocation**. Aim while grounded on the street and press **X**; use F2 to remap. Saved conflicting assignments are preserved, so the displayed binding is authoritative. All invocations share a 12-second recovery, including after changing selection. The current Rama avatar channels every invocation; selecting a legend does not change the model.

The native rules affect living lane troops. They include capped healing, expiring shields/slows and two temporary guards within existing army caps. They do not implement hero health/death, structure spell damage or native online combat. Hanuman projects a landing payload without teleporting Rama. Read [practice rules](ULTIMATE_PRACTICE.md), [effect contracts](ULTIMATE_EFFECTS.md), [selector UX](INVOCATION_UX.md), [entity bridge](NATIVE_ENTITY_BRIDGE.md) and [physics decision](PHYSICS_AUTHORITY_DECISION.md) for exact boundaries.

Native-practice **content revision 2** pins the changed gameplay sources and rejects older native descriptors. Browser/proposal content remain revision 1, as do input/event/entity/replay envelope versions. The bridge and physics experiment prepare future authority work; they do not change the browser's protocol 2, 3v3/480-second game or deliver the proposed native 6v6 match.

## Ownership and next production work

Use exclusive files and agreed contracts for each worker. Independent source/code/tests may run in parallel; serialize imports, asset replacement, shared source freezes and heavy rendering. Report after contract review, implementation/counterexamples, and independent test/render review; add a correction round only for a concrete finding. Retain exact hashes, commands, failures and limitations.

After the bounded release, prioritize continuous hero motion/contact and authored face/hair/cloth, clearer distant/overlapping warnings and stronger motif separation, then adopt the native authority/controller profile and test two actual clients with prediction/reconciliation. Full hero health/death, match rules, roster production and operated services still follow their [production dependencies](ROADMAP_BACKLOG.json). A concept image, geometric effect, headless pass or offline core result does not establish Deadlock/Overwatch/Dota 2 quality or a complete competitive game.
