## User-supplied claims and adaptation boundary

The descriptions of *Black Myth: Wukong* NG+8, a named boss's specific phases, a no-damage run, automatic stacking, 2.5× explosions and full invulnerability frames were provided by the user. No linked footage, game files or developer specification for those claims was inspected for this packet, so they are **unverified here**. Do not present them as measured game mechanics, reuse proprietary game assets, or assume they belong in YUGA. The first YUGA rule deliberately defines its own bounded area, timings and counters.

The brief's “moksha” language, “Vayu Mantra” and a named pose are suggestions rather than sourced ritual instructions. The presentation direction uses guardians returning to light and avoids asserting a specific scripture, chanting or doctrinal outcome. The sacred source traditions warrant review with knowledgeable practitioners and artists rather than one asserted, definitive telling.

The translation uses its author's historical terminology and transliteration. This document uses “Vanara” as a broad reference to the named communities in the tale and treats the depicted YUGA guardians as fictional designs. Hanuman's powers, color palette, accessibility choices, numerical effects and the fictional Silt-Crowned Warden are authored game decisions.
