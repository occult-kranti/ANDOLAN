# Ember Rite audio · Ash at the Waterline

At Ghat Mandapa a defender hears a wick catch on the far side of the bell screen. They can challenge the hold, circle through Dry Bank, or wait. Only when the fire is lit do both teams hear the first plucked string. For the next forty seconds the music tells everyone how much time has passed. A splash, a quiver or a returning disc still belongs to the few people close enough to hear it.

## What a round sounds like

| Round phase at 20 Hz | Who gets the information | Sound, score and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare, proposed 20 s / 400 ticks | Each team knows its role and briefing. Allies know their carrier. | Let a briefing tone die away before movement opens. The current Rust experiment starts at Approach. |
| Approach, 100 s / 2,000 ticks | The acting player gets their own cues. Nearby listeners can hear an eligible movement. A hidden carrier's identity stays with allies. | Let the dry or wet foot contact lead the mix. The rehearsal emits a contact per 1.65 m actually travelled. A local drop or recovery has its own sound. An opponent's footsteps alone do not identify the vessel. |
| Kindle, 4 s / 80 consecutive eligible ticks | The carrier gets hold feedback. A listener within the 30 m drill radius can hear a disclosed start. Cancellation and its cause belong to the actor. | Use a wick scrape at the start. If released, stop the actor's hold sound. A remote listener who heard the onset has to wait or investigate. An authorized drop, a failed hold and a confirmed ignition have different meanings. |
| Burn, 40 s / 800 ticks | Confirmed site and fire time become public. | Start the plucked drone. At elapsed ticks 200, 400 and 600 bring in low drum, answering hand drum and reed. The reed enters with ten seconds left. The exact time stays visible. A practice handoff freezes the solo exercise until the defender takes control and starts the retake clock. |
| Quench, 3.5 s / 70 consecutive eligible ticks, within 1.5 m of the placed vessel | Defender gets progress and cancellation. A disclosed water-pour onset is local, with an 18 m drill radius. | A brief pour may be a genuine tap and release. A replacement defender begins a fresh hold. Positive shield or health damage breaks a hold. On the last eligible burn tick, completion precedes expiry after lethal and elimination checks. |
| Resolve and reset | Both teams get the verified reason and score. | Quench closes with breath and descending plucks. A completed burn earns the rising horn ending. Approach expiry and elimination have different results. Retire the old round's voices, fields and pending releases. |

Each side can pick the same hero. Sun/Moon identity persists through the regulation duty swap after 12 rounds. First to 13 wins regulation. A 12–12 score opens paired overtime with a swap after each round. A two-round lead at the end of a pair wins. Three tied pairs finish at 15–15. The fire lasts **40 seconds in overtime**, with the same final-tick rules and score phase. Eliminating the last attacker after ignition leaves the defenders' quench decision alive. ## A score that leaves the doorway audible

*Ash at the Waterline* sets a 96 BPM pulse through its plucks and drum attacks. Each mono loop runs for sixteen beats, exactly ten seconds. The plucked drone takes its color from tanpura. A pressure bend on the low drum and a closed answering stroke suggest mridangam and tabla articulations. Leave space after the reed's ascent for a hero warning. Shehnai and shankh are separate double-reed and conch references for future performance. The available score is original numerical synthesis. The four loops, stereo mix and two result stings are rendered FLACs. The reference mix measures −21.5 LUFS integrated and −7.3 dBTP. Those are file measurements. Loudness and intelligibility in a crowded fight still need ears on the target device. ## Mixer, captions and ten listening seats

**The local mix.** The rehearsal uses equal-power stereo panning from the listener's facing, distance attenuation, low-pass filtering through cover and one short reflection for a shared building footprint. Footsteps reach 18 m, the Hanuman leap audition 20 m and the Kali dance audition 25 m. A step outside its radius produces neither an audible event nor a caption. Priority cues duck music. A hero audition in Explore has no match effect. Riot's 2.06 notes describe a useful headphone comparison on selected footsteps, reloads and Deathmatch respawns. For a future 3D pass, compare optional HRTF against this stereo baseline on the same disclosed positions, with mono and captions available in both. [Rehearsal mixer](../atlas/rehearsal/audio.mjs) · [Riot Games, VALORANT patch 2.06](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-2-06/)

**The listener's controls.** Sound starts on an explicit gesture. Master, Effects, Music, mono and captions can be changed independently in the rehearsal. Caption eligibility comes from the same audible observation, including coarse bearing and muffling. A muted player can still distinguish “Kindle started” from “Site B kindled”. In the hosted design, subtitle the speaker when known, preserve the segmented timer and relation shapes, and give speech its own level when performances enter matches. MDN documents user-gesture resumption. Microsoft's guidelines cover speaker and directional labels, captioning important sounds, separate controls and mono output. [Web Audio best practices](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Audio_API/Best_practices) · [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 104](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/104) · [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 105](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/105)

**The proposed host contract.** After each movement and damage tick, the host resolves the ritual and each listener's audibility, including floor, cover and permitted identification. Send public site, phase, start tick and deadline through the reliable round-state stream. Number transient cue events within a round generation and filter them for each seat before transport. A listener hearing an unseen source receives the permitted bearing, distance band and muffling, with actor identity and exact coordinates omitted. Give a hit or heal receipt only to its eligible recipients. A feint may create footsteps or a flutter, while the objective alone can issue a completed-kindling or quench message. Separate player streams from spectator views. [Game rules and current adapter boundary](../ROUND_AND_TECH.md)

On reconnect, take a fresh snapshot, discard old transient IDs and start only current layers at `elapsed_burn_seconds % 10`. A late listener at 23 s enters the first three loops at offset 3 s. A resolved snapshot restores the displayed result silently. The server clock owns victory and held progress while Web Audio schedules playback. The solo drill can freeze its local clock on pause or focus loss. A hosted player's return must catch up to the continuing round.
