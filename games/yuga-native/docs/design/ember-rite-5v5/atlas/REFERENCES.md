# Map research for Ember Rite

The panel selected maps with published accounts of a design change and its consequences. The selection covers two CS:GO reworks, three VALORANT maps, and several revisions of Deadlock’s *Cursed Apple*. The examples answer three different questions. Can a player read an entrance? Is a shortcut worth revealing a rotation? Does a strong off-site position still draw a team back to the objective?

## Competitive maps

| Reference and edition | What the source records | Design decision to test in YUGA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dust II, Valve’s CS:GO rework | At A, Valve removed dark background doorways and drainpipes that obscured corner peeks. At B it admitted daylight into the tunnels, widened Window, moved the car and simplified scaffolding. [Valve walkthrough and comparison views](https://www.counter-strike.net/dust2) | Give the ember carrier a plain backdrop from both entrances. Put the district’s rich carving above heads or below the playing surface. From both camera shoulders, compare a still silhouette, a moving hero and an empty doorway at the same distance. Include dusk and reduced effects. |
| Inferno, Valve’s CS:GO rework | At B, widening the last approach and providing cover before the site made moving together easier. Valve also observed that grenades became more effective. At A, a clearer path let players use utility before exposing themselves to a defender. [Valve walkthrough, Bombsites A and B](https://www.counter-strike.net/inferno) | Give a second teammate room to pass a stopped carrier. Put the second approach’s decision point upstream of the narrow entry. Test two attackers entering abreast, then defend with Hanuman’s single Sena field. Record whether one placement denies both legal approaches or just one. |
| Ascent, Riot level designers, 20 July 2020 | The team started with three lanes and an adjustable connection. They refined openings, engagement distances and angles in repeated greybox playtests. An elevated landmark distinguished A and a drained canal distinguished low B. The team kept most rich detail outside the band containing human silhouettes. [The Birth of Ascent](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/the-birth-of-ascent/) | Give every site a recognizable horizon and a separate local ground mark. A distant landmark can indicate the district while a vessel socket identifies the actual interaction point. Ask first-time players to face each site from a junction and then reach the legal socket with the minimap closed. |
| Split, Riot level designers, 17 June 2020 | An early central tower let defenders see attackers’ approach. It pulled too much combat away from the sites. Riot developed separate site-facing towers, connected them back into the sites, and reduced the number of ways to enter mid. [The Creation of Split, “The Build”](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/the-creation-of-split/) | A gallery can reveal one crossing. One perch observing both sites’ interaction points would make committing to a defensive rotation too cheap. Trace both shoulder views, then stage a feint at one site and an ignition at the other. Record where a defender had to move to get reliable information. |
| Bind, Riot’s map overview and design retrospective | Bind’s two sites have direct approaches, no conventional mid and a pair of one-way teleporters. The design team identifies removal of mid as the constraint that motivated the teleporter experiment. [Official map overview, Bind](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/maps/) · [Design retrospective](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/the-creation-of-split/) | Mandala’s own proposed bidirectional folds give up information in exchange for time. Test a local entrance/exit cue, a visual and caption equivalent, and a fully usable walking bypass. Log who could infer the rotation and when, with the folds enabled and disabled. |
| Cursed Apple, Valve gameplay updates, 22 May and 30 October 2026 | Valve removed two outward-facing jump pads in mid in May. In October it enlarged the Mid-Boss area and added cover, and lowered the tunnel movement bonus from 70% to 50%. The notes document the changes without a measured account of their effects. [May update](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1422450/announcements/detail/670617878982034053) · [October update](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1422450/announcements/detail/680765564125186046) | Treat getting somewhere faster and surviving the arrival as independent tuning decisions. Compare a runner’s dry walking rotation and optional shortcut with identical heroes. Then test whether the receiver can contest the landing and whether the runner has a retreat. Keep the ordinary route viable with every special connector disabled. |
| Cursed Apple, community map description, revision 244462, 24 November 2026 | The wiki describes three named lanes and four distinct districts connected by buildings, alleys and tunnels. The Theater has several entrances, a roof opening and a tunnel below reached from the canal. This is a community description of a changing game. [Pinned revision, “Districts”](https://deadlock.wiki/index.php?title=Cursed_Apple&oldid=244462#Districts) | Treat jungle, gallery and waterside rooms as named places with recognizable entrances. A vertical room should let players identify the upper and lower exits by sight and make a short, unambiguous call to a teammate. Have players call a level and exit with the HUD hidden, then run the reciprocal route using ordinary movement. |

The panel also inspected Valve’s paired stills of Inferno’s B approach and Dust II’s A-site ramp. In the Inferno pair the approach wall changes from a row of dark arched recesses to a flatter facade. In the Dust II pair a low pale wall separates the playable ramp from a much more ornate building behind it. These observations inform YUGA’s silhouette and false-doorway checks. The photographs and commercial map geometry remain with their publishers. [Inferno comparisons](https://www.counter-strike.net/inferno) · [Dust II comparisons](https://www.counter-strike.net/dust2)

## Architecture and story

| Reference | Spatial observation | Direction for original environments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Group of Monuments at Hampi | On the Tungabhadra, temple complexes, suburban bazaars and water works form linked parts of a settlement. The account describes wide streets lined by pillared mandapas, stepped tanks and a channel network. [Statement of Outstanding Universal Value](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/241/) | Give the market, embankment and court a civic relationship. A carrier can follow a clearly visible delivery route from water to market, and a player leaving the court can orient from the arcade silhouette. Place narrow decorative posts outside the intended walking width. |
| UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Rani-ki-Vav, Patan, Gujarat | The stepped corridor descends past pillared pavilions toward a tank and a well. The vertical sequence gives a visitor a way to read depth. [Property description and brief synthesis](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/922/) | Use repeated landings, a visible dry return and a clear water edge for fictional river courts. A floor marking at each landing should still indicate the way back if particles, reflections and sound are switched off. Hampi and Patan are separate references for form. |

## What to validate next

A floor plan can show a stair, a fork or two sites connected by a route. Movement time, usable cover at a hero’s eye height, and the success of a feint depend on the actual blockout and the other team. Run the same approach, feint and retake in both directions before fixing decorative geometry. For each iteration keep the ineffective hold or confusing turn alongside the revised plan, so the next designer can see what changed. [The Level Design Book, Layout](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/layout.md) · [Blockout](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/blockout.md)
