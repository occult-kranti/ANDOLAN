Controls and drill rules

Focus the plan to walk with WASD or the arrow keys, or use the direction pad. Hold E or Interact at a site or fold. G drops the vessel. Hold Interact near it to pick it up. A hold stops on release. P pauses.

The guide measures the route from your selected start at full walking speed. Ramp grade is represented on the plan. Fixed cover and masonry stop the marker. At Measure Well the central sill stays dry between the two recessed basins. The clock starts with the first move or hold and pauses while this window is hidden.

Kindle anywhere on the 3 m disk. The solo handoff lets you try a defender’s retake of that exact vessel. The Quench exercise instead places a practice vessel 2 m north of the chosen site centre. This is an offline drill without opponents.
