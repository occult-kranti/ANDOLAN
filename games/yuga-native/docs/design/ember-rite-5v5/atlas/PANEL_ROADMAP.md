# Ember Rite · map and hero production handoff

Updated 26 November 2026. The release has two complementary ways to play. In the solo map rehearsal a player can carry, kindle or quench in any of four districts. In the Confluence knockout rooms up to ten people can play Hanuman or Rama with their revised kits. The next integration brings the actual vessel, playspace and duty swap to that same room host. ## Three design rounds

| Round | What the specialists compared | Decision and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Research and diagnosis** | A source researcher read dated site descriptions, excavation reports, translations and a historical itinerary. The district designer and cartographer checked the four baseline routes. Combat design compared each playable hero’s contact and resource opportunities. | Give every new structure a local purpose and a distinct silhouette. Keep a dry route when a special crossing is held. Reduce the nearly ten-second Palace Terrace setup advantage through route geometry. Preserve the heroes’ existing strengths and improve an escort decision for Hanuman and a movement decision for Rama. [Source passages](RESEARCH_ROUNDS.md) · [Reference map studies](REFERENCES.md) · [Hero arithmetic](../HERO_BALANCE.md) |
| **2 · Author and integrate** | District and technical art built four registered landmarks, then produced the plan, section, thresholds and route strip from the same parameters. The traversal specialist linked all four maps to an offline, keyboard/touch rehearsal. Combat put both additions in the Rust authority. The Observatory team connected the live and saved dashboards to the same release data. | Two temple–pyramid pairs turn the player in opposite directions. The human-readable signs, inverse return, physical bypass and transition time are authored together. Each other district gets its own architectural route decision. Solo drill and online skirmish are separately identified wherever players launch either one. [Atlas and collision exports](README.md) · [Runtime and controls](../../../../../yuga-rust/README.md) |
| **3 · Challenge the result** | Independent route analysis closed mid and both spawns, tested dry access to pairs of actual doors, and charged the cost of losing a nearly completed quench. Blender checked the imported models, slopes and passage clearance. Browser tests exercised carrying, interrupted holds, return-facing and control reset. The combat panel tested overlapping casts, full quivers and blocked movement. | Add Flood Bench after a mid closure severed Kurukshetra’s retake. Turn two Mandala approaches around a solid pavilion wing. Project walking-height masonry into the drill and test the centre and shoulders in both directions. Write distinct return cues for the folds. The solo practice ally begins wounded so Hanuman's new support interaction can be tried directly. ## What the geography buys a team

| District | New choice | Observation to collect in a ten-seat match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mandala Rift** | Take the Sluice for a short 11.4 s site switch, announce a folded temple crossing to nearby players, or use the longer dry side passage. Count notches and read the named exit before committing. | Can a defender tell which mouth opened without seeing who carried the ember? Can a player on the crest see both placements from one position? |
| **Lanka** | Trade exposure on the 21.9 s Wind Arch retake against 29.6 s by dry shelter with mid closed, down from 35.4 s. At clear walking speed the sheltered option now leaves about 6.9 s after the pour. | Count how many defenders still choose the exposed arch when they already know the site. Ask what the gallery's counted bays told them about the opposition. |
| **Ayodhya** | Reach the other site through the audible wash in 24.5 s or use the normal 28.0 s dry route. If Wash Steps is separately denied, Measure Well gives a 30.2 s dry retake. | Record which route a player actually heard, which site they inferred, and whether the running water hides a legal quench cue. |
| **Kurukshetra** | At Palace Terrace defenders now arrive 3.3 s before attackers. When Gauge Bridge is denied, the 27.2 s Flood Bench walk leaves about 9.3 s after an immediate quench. Wind Field still gives defenders roughly 6.0 s to set up. | Does the defender who arrives first need to commit to one threshold? Do bearers ever use the longer bank after taking information at mid? |

These are travel-time budgets from room centres, with ordinary movement at 5 m/s and shallow water at 85% speed. A runner still needs time to find a lip placement, clear a corner and react to the opponent. Every side/site has a pair of independently reachable dry threshold centres farther apart than the 6 m diameter of a single Sena. This geometric result leaves freedom to favor a particular hero at one door. It makes no prediction about real win rates. ## Game plan and next owners

| Order | Owner and implementation | Review condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Mandala in the room host** | World and Rust engineers bring the dry floors, cover, objective disks, and both playable hero capsules into one authoritative district. Add pickup/drop, held interact, interruption by accepted damage, replication, and a fresh state after side swap. | Starting from both sides, place the vessel at the centre and four edges of each site. Two distinct entrants can recover or quench it. Two rendered clients agree on position, tick, result and the next round after a disconnect. |
| **2 · Traversal and camera** | Attach the registered pavilion and its passage, then the shallow ford and two signed folds to the networked district. Keep reserve dry routes open. Test Rama’s bow camera and Hanuman’s tail and shoulders at each bend. | The exit pose composes to identity on return. Blocked exits and interrupted channels restore the correct floor. Test both shoulders, muted cues, reduced motion and the steepest Lanka ramp on actual target controllers. |
| **3 · Teamplay and a meaningful recap** | Log one event ID per verified change of possession, interruption or completed objective. Record escort healing actually delivered and arrows replenished against eligible opportunities. | Have both sides explain the decision at a fork. A reconnect, friendly tap or repeated partial pour cannot multiply round credit. The recap can recognize a useful escort when the team loses. |
| **4 · Human competition** | Run the existing side-swapped Mandala playtest. Keep draft, map, side, repeated team identity, exposure and unknown choices in the analysis. Repeat the failed scenario after moving geometry or adding an under-served hero tool. | Publish sample sizes and uncertainty. A hero may remain preferred in a context if the opponent has a viable answer. Advance the next district after both sites support a contested retake and players can explain the alternatives. [Staged playtest](ENCOUNTERS.md#playtest-sequence) · [Role-by-role plan](../HERO_BALANCE.md) |
