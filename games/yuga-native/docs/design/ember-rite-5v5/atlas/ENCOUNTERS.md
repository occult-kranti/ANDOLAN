# Who carries the light?

Use the vertical script for a ten-player observer run with named hero pairings, alternate door assignments, mobile setup, airtime counters and edge-of-disk pours. It includes thresholds and a recording form. [Round director’s script](vertical-play/encounters.md)

Four seconds in the open is a long time to ask of a teammate. The carrier chooses a patch of floor at the sanctum. The other four decide where to show themselves, which approach to hold, and which way out to leave open. Once the vessel is alight, defenders face the same problem. Anyone can pour the water, and someone else has to make the time. The script calls for ordinary movement and can be rehearsed with duplicate Hanuman and Rama picks once a playable graybox exists. Its clock and interaction rules come from the standalone Rust experiment. [Round contract](../ROUND_AND_TECH.md) · [Rust rules](../../../../../yuga-rust/src/ritual.rs)

## One Mandala round, with two ways out

Ten human seats, ordinary movement, third-person cameras. Sun attacks this round. On the drawn centerlines at a constant 5 m/s, Spice Gate → Reed Steps → Dry Bank → A takes about 37 s. Spice Gate → Cloth Yard → South Pyramid → B takes about 34 s. The script leaves time to check the last corner. Real capsule collision and other players will change those times. Times below run from the start of the approach unless marked “fire +”. [Mandala geometry](maps/mandala.json)

| Time | Bearers | Keepers and what they know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Briefing | The carrier and escort call Dry Bank toward A. Two teammates plan a visible push toward B. The fifth volunteers to watch the Sluice. The carrier says, “If the bank closes, I’m coming back to you.” | Two volunteer for A, two for B, one for mid. Each anchor names the second route back into their site. A ping can communicate each assignment. |
| 0–40 s | The B pair actually enter a visible approach. They refrain from calling B open until one can see the route. At A the escort checks the near side of the bell screen while the carrier waits. | A B player reports “Two at Bazaar.” The mid player can help B and give up the inside A angle, or hold and risk leaving the pair in a two-on-two. Seeing two echoes without sight of the vessel leaves the carrier unknown. |
| 44–47 s | The carrier starts at the A lip. At 46 s one accepted shield hit resets the kindle. The escort sees the interrupt angle and calls it. | An A anchor knows which lip was tried. The B anchor still has to decide whether to leave. There is no global ignition announcement on an interrupted attempt. |
| **Hold A** | A teammate occupies the interrupt angle. The carrier restarts at 48 s on the far lip, remains grounded, and finishes at 52 s. Everyone now sees “A · 40 s”. | One defender approaches the vessel from the opposite entrance while another looks behind the screen. At fire +12 s the first taps quench and releases. At +15 s a second defender starts from zero. An uninterrupted finish at +18.5 s wins. A hit or a release cancels it. If defenders keep the surviving attackers out but then run down the clock, the fire still wins. |
| **Leave for B** | At 47 s the escort says, “I’ll hold the bank. Bring it back through mid.” The carrier leaves with the vessel. The B pair can retain an entrance or come back to escort. They have 53 s left. The drawn A → Sluice → B route costs about 11 s in clear space, plus 4 s to kindle. An occupied Sluice changes that decision. | The A anchor can chase and abandon the lip or wait for another attempt. The mid keeper can rejoin the B anchor by a dry route. If the carrier falls, recovering the vessel costs another player the trip to the last confirmed ground. A team that waits out the 100 s approach gives the keepers the round. |

The fixed pre-ignition clock makes a false push cost time for the team that stages it. Defenders can call “two seen, carrier unknown” and retain the second site. An escort can reopen an angle or recover the vessel, and an anchor can interrupt a live hold. Both actions can matter in a lost round. Riot's early Split tower drew play away from the sites. For Mandala, let the Sluice player see a crossing and move to verify where it led. If the same perch reveals both sanctums, turn one entrance or screen its last few metres with a return wall. ### Two hero interactions to stage

- **Hanuman against the far-lip quench.** Place Vanara Sena at the center of the court after ignition. Its 3 m circle and the site disk have the same nominal radius. With a center plant, try knocking out the caster during windup or holding outside the field until it expires. With a lip plant, try approaching from just outside the field while staying within 1.5 m of the vessel. The result depends on body size, cover and the actual field edge. Repeat with fields staggered by two Hanumans. Record whether defenders can find a legal hold before the 40 s fire ends. [Sena dimensions and timing](../../../../../yuga-rust/assets/vanara-sena.json)
- **Rama beside the carrier.** Stage a Guardian Circle at an entrance to protect a teammate crossing to cover the kindle. Then stage it over the carrier. An accepted hit to shield must reset the hold even when the carrier’s health appears full. Ask the carrier to explain the interruption from the cue alone. Standing on an ally’s ward should never become the only way to complete a site. [Rama kit](../../../../assets/design/rama-ability-kit.json) · [Interaction interruption rules](../ROUND_AND_TECH.md)

**Try the new strengths in the skirmish first.** Put a wounded escort beside Hanuman at Sena impact, then have a second escort enter after the impact. Only the first should gain health. For Rama, spend an arrow and step into an open crossing, then repeat facing nearby cover. Count the resource change and distance actually travelled. In the future ten-seat objective round, pair the escort with a carrier at one doorway and give the opposing team the independent second entrance. Record the carrier’s call and the defender’s route choice. [Mechanics and counters](../HERO_BALANCE.md) · [Door spacing by side](BALANCE_MATH.md)

## Feedback and recognition

- A carrier gets hold progress, the word **Interrupted**, and a short local break in the rising cue when damage lands. Confirmed ignition and quench each get a distinct shape, label and sound. Offer map pings and captions with the same recipient and spatial limits as the corresponding cue. Spectator overlays stay out of player views.
- In the recap, credit a recovery when the same player later completes ignition without losing possession. Credit a player’s first verified damage interrupt against a live opposing channel once per round. Lead with the actual result and reason. A rehearsal observer can annotate an escort’s sightline on replay. Automatic credit needs a server event ID, seat, tick and round reset. Repeated taps, friendly pings and reconnect replays earn nothing. Start with recognition only. [Progression design](../MODES_AND_MAPS.md)

## Playtest sequence

The counts below set decisions for an initial production test. No participants or runtime data are claimed here.

| Pass and owner | Procedure | What would change the next build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Paper, level designer** | Give ten first-time readers one role-view blueprint each. Five start on either side. With no narration, ask them to point to A, B, a second dry entrance and the route to the marked vessel within 30 s. Repeat with the opposite side and grayscale after recording the first answers. | If fewer than 8 of 10 get all four on first exposure, change the confusing callout, silhouette or crossing. Report both wrong answers and elapsed time. Repeat with fresh readers. |
| **2 · Collision and cues, world + accessibility** | In the graybox, mark center and four compass-edge ignitions per site. Walk from two independent dry entries for each of the ten markers. Record the third-person view at the last turn with the wall on the left and on the right. Repeat with muted audio, reduced effects and a different team outline. Try to interact from the wrong elevation. | One stranded vessel, through-floor channel, earlier hidden-information cue in captions, or mandatory mobility ability holds the map. Record position, camera and source revision. Correct the floor, prop or cue, then rerun the failing placement and its neighbor. |
| **3 · Free-choice rounds, game design** | Once authority and ten rendered seats are ready, start with Mandala. Three separate groups of ten play 16 rounds each on the same revision. In fresh eight-round practice blocks, exchange team duties and keep the same hero pools. Give no preferred site. Classify declared destination, first dry/wet/special approach, whether it changed, exact ignite point, winner and reason. Ask one player on each side what triggered the final decision. | Flag a site selected in fewer than one in five declared attacks, or any repeated case where a single stationary angle covers both independent entries and the live vessel. Replay the rounds before moving cover. Record no-plant rounds and unknown intentions separately. The 48 rounds share teams and do not establish a population win rate. |
| **4 · Retake and iteration, encounter director** | On both sites, stage six equal-size retakes from each dry entry, half near-lip and half far-lip. Run the same pairings on the revised geometry and compare recognition time, first legal hold and interruptions. Ask both sides whether they could identify a different choice. | If all six attempts at an entrance fail before anyone can even start a legal hold, review that camera, cover and approach in the recording. Retain the earlier blueprint and the change that improved it. Move to the three other districts only after Mandala has two usable entries per site and the same information reaches a muted player. |

For each round keep one line with map revision, team on attack, hero pools, intended site or unknown, approach, actual site, ignition and interruption ticks, result reason and one sentence from the debrief. Group observations by team and side. A useful criticism identifies what the player believed at the fork, what information they actually had and which alternative the level let them take.
