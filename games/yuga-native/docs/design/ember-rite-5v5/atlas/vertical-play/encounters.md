# Running vertical 5v5 rounds on Mandala Rift

Use one carrier, one close escort, two route players and one reserve on each team. The attacker seats are **A1** Hanuman (escort), **A2** Rama (carrier), **A3** Arjuna (ranged watch), **A4** Krishna (space control) and **A5** a second Rama (reserve). Defender seats are **D1** Hanuman (challenge), **D2** Rama (water), **D3** Arjuna (observer), **D4** Krishna (re-entry) and **D5** a second Rama (reserve). Repeat a block with duties reversed and rotate the carrier and water role among players. The second Rama is a private-test roster choice. Arjuna’s and Krishna’s new projectile profiles and Hanuman’s sustained flight are study actions. Existing Rama and Hanuman actions also have separate Rust implementations. These scripts specify a moderated ten-seat test when the match adapter supports the scenarios. [Hero status by ability](hero-abilities.md) · [Round rules](../../ROUND_AND_TECH.md)

Coordinates below are `(east, north)` in metres. Heights give the elevation of the character’s feet above the named support. In an engine, world Y is up. Put the feet on the surveyed ground before every channel. A threshold coordinate marks a crossing, while the hide coordinates are offset from the cover solids and are safe places to stand in the surveyed posture. Ghat Mandapa A is at `(0,0)` and Bazaar Sanctuary B at `(40,0)`. Each ignition disk has radius 3 m and a clear 4.5 m apron. Keep both a round clock and a recording tick. The simulator runs at 20 Hz. A runner’s reported intentions and an observer’s visible evidence are different log fields. [Registered map](../maps/mandala.json) · [Source for geometry and directional witnesses](architecture.py)

## 1. The last second of preparation

**Question.** Can five players stage a meaningful opening without disclosing the enemy’s setup or releasing an early action?

1. Start a 400-tick, 20 s preparation at Spice Gate `(0,-112)` and Water Gate `(0,112)`. A1 climbs Spice Loft `(5.2,-114.5)`, floor +2.4 m. A3 travels through Cloth Yard to the optional Cloth Loft `(52,-69.2)`, +3.2 m. D1 and D3 may use the mirrored Water Loft `(-5.2,114.5)`, +2.4 m, and Scale Loft `(52,69.2)`, +3.2 m. Each ladder is on the west of its deck with eastward ground egress. A2/A4 and D2/D4 rehearse a split from ordinary ground. A5/D5 watch their side’s legal barrier and clock. If a player misses a distant loft before the signal, record their actual position and open the round on time.
2. On the runner’s final legal setup step, test a ground foot and an elevated body against north `−40.45` for attack and `+40.45` for defence. The fields extend above the playable air envelope. At tick 399 try an attack, fold, ritual and flight as separate resets. Compare both teams’ views of the scenario with the attempted input omitted. The defender can hear the shared public countdown. A remote enemy’s private position, selected action or prep VFX must not give away a landing or target.
3. At tick 400 release the barriers once while retaining legal positions. Require a fresh action in the live phase for every rejected one. Run a late movement packet carrying a prep-era sequence and one fresh live move. Preserve the new packet’s legal movement and the old packet’s rejection. A2 calls “going via temple”; D3 replies to their own team “north ready, carrier unknown.” Opponents should learn the actual destination from contested territory.

**Pass.** All four legal decks can be staged from their home side via ordinary ground and ladder or scored as missed against the clock. Both blocked boundary approaches, airborne restrictions and private information behave consistently. Log the first legal ground arrival at a site per side and staging choice. The older 10 s proposal has been superseded in this study. [Preparation and movement model](physics.py) · [Riot barrier evidence](research.md#a-round-can-start-before-combat-does)

The ordinary ground-route model gives the fast inner openings after twenty seconds of staging. A and B respectively take **11.098 s and 16.020 s** of live time for attack, **10.272 s and 14.576 s** for defence. Holding preparation at its old ten seconds would leave attack with 16.587/21.509 s and defence with 14.825/19.128 s. Do not use these inner-route estimates for the outer pad starts. With west ladder ascent included, Cloth Loft takes 17.70 s from its spawn and Scale Loft 16.60 s. A late detour can use up that margin. ## 2. A at the bell terrace

**Question.** Can the carrier commit at one lip while the opponent still has a distinct route to the actual vessel?

1. After release, A2/A1 follow South Temple into **a-temple_s** at `(-3.85,-10)`. A4 takes the Dry Bank door **a-bank** at `(-11,3.2)`. A3 watches from Sluice without seeing through its gearbox. A5 remains on the dry rotation. D1 holds the Bell screen’s south-door pocket, D3 watches a separate northern approach, D2 stages a retake via **a-mid** and D4/D5 reserve B. The standing Bell screen hide is at `(-6.162,-0.74)` and its open peek is `(-5.38,-0.74)`. The Trace table needs a crouch at `(6.21,-6.868)`. If the threat comes from the ring, the documented alternate hide is `(3.08,-8.46)`. The bank approach also has the Gauge locker. Give the defenders only sightings from their current facing.
2. A1 makes the air choice outside the disk while A2 stays low. For the flight treatment allow one `vayu_flight` thrust, a 2.4 s window, at most 12 m of horizontal path and +4 m feet above launch. Watch the launch position, ground shadow and landing. Repeat with A1 grounded and using the ordinary turn. If collision under an overhang ends the climb, do not award any hidden extra altitude. D1 can stay behind the standing screen. D3 can hold where the return is visible. D2 keeps the second entrance rather than chasing the flyer.
3. A2 starts a visible four-second kindle at `(2.8,0)` while standing still. Give A1’s Sena one legal 0.6 s arrival on a wounded ally within 3 m, then repeat with a wall between actor and effect. A2 can receive a bounded heal and can still be interrupted by incoming damage. Additional escorts cannot shorten four seconds. At the control run, interrupt the carrier on tick 79 of 80 and confirm zero completed ignitions.
4. With an accepted ignite, place grounded D2 at `(4.0,0)` on arrival. The horizontal separation from the marked vessel is 1.2 m. D2 is outside the ignition disk and can still quench if reach is clear. A defender at `(4.31,0)` is 1.51 m away and cannot begin. Test 69 ticks then release, and 70 uninterrupted ticks; record progress and winner. Repeat on the opposite lip with a new legal placement and use the actual coordinates in each record.

**Pass.** Both ground routes admit the carrier. The chosen high path produces no remote ignite. Every observed interrupt, edge-of-disk quench and receiver receipt is attributable to a legal actor and tick. The Bell screen counter is a sightline from a specified entrance, and an observer taking an elevated angle gets a fresh visibility test. ## 3. B behind the weighbeam

**Question.** Can a team cross a threatened angle and switch route after seeing which counter the opponent chose?

1. A2/A4 take South Pyramid to **b-pyramid_s** at `(39,-10)`. A3 watches the Sluice route, A1/A5 approach the alternative. D3 chooses Stamp screen at **b-pyramid_n**, D1 chooses the low Bale bench at the south door, D2 waits to quench at **b-mid**, D4/D5 guard the cross-site option. Start the low hide at `(45.236,-6.14)` and peek at `(43.98,-6.14)`. For the replay have D1 choose the standing Receipt shutter hide at `(32.19,-6.279)`. Do not give either defender both places in one run.
2. Script an Arjuna `gandiva_line` down a legal, unobstructed approach in the study. The 0.40 s telegraph is eight ticks. At 48 m/s a 12 m shot then needs 0.25 s of travel. Leave a defender enough information to step behind the solid before release. The hard cover hit must precede any target beyond it. Repeat while moving the aim over the soft Twine rack. Its visual fringe cannot stop the projectile. Check both shoulders with effects reduced. The 30 m range is shorter than the 40 m between the two site centres.
3. A4 tries `sudarshana_return` from an exposed mark. Its 0.25 s tell precedes an outward path up to 12 m at 16 m/s, then up to 12 m back at 20 m/s to the original anchor. For a full unobstructed excursion the continuous travel plus tell totals 1.60 s before tick rounding. D1 can leave the return corridor or use Receipt shutter. On separate trials put a defender across both legs, place a hard obstacle in one leg and move the caster after departure. A target may receive one contact across both legs. A solid impact ends the path. The return must not home to the caster’s new position or reveal an unseen opponent.
4. Place the vessel at `(40,-2.5)`; A5 holds the **b-mid** alternative and stops looking at the fall of the chakra to observe a real doorway. Use one ordinary grounded water holder from either north or mid. Retain an entirely non-flight retake in the same block. In the replay D1 calls “bench exposed from above” and shifts to Receipt shutter without receiving information about the hidden carrier.

**Pass.** Standing and crouched options produce different observed outcomes at the same south entrance, the north and mid counter-routes remain reachable, each blocked shape resolves correctly, and a grounded defender can take an uncontested legal 70-tick hold. ## 4. The false call and the return to A

A3 intentionally shows a legal silhouette at Sluice `(20,0)`, crest +1.4 m, while A2/A4 commit at B. D3 may report one visible player, direction and time only. “The carrier planted A” is an incorrect call until D3 has the corresponding evidence. Repeat with identical prep and an actual A commit so an experienced defender cannot classify a false call from the script order. Compare with a separate free-choice run. The straight 40 m between lantern rings gives an eight-second lower bound at 5 m/s on a flat line. The dry route via Sluice actually takes **11.359 s** centre to centre. With mid and both starts closed the dry model takes **24.303 s**. During the 40 s burn, reserving a 3.5 s quench leaves **25.141 s** with mid open or **12.197 s** with mid closed, before fights, reacquisition or a late interruption. A hold broken after 69 ticks costs an additional 3.45 s. These are route-model budgets. Measure ten-player arrival times separately. Run two site-swapped versions. In version 1, require D2 to walk the long dry way, D4 to protect a second door, and D1 to call a plausible landing without following it. In version 2, D2 takes mid; D4 must call the actual placed vessel, D3 can follow an airborne silhouette up to the first occluding solid, and D5 times the hold from the first legal tick. Force one late interrupt per version, then replay without it. If a crossing, solid or facing changes the predicted winner, keep the counterexample with both route and hold times.

## 5. Human trials and decision rules

| Block | Assignment | Record | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prep disclosure | Two independent ten-player groups. Each group runs four setups, swapping attack and defence halfway. Randomly assign one player per side to stay low, one to climb and one to attempt a forbidden input before the barrier. | VOD from one client per side, timestamps, own/other observer event payload, start position, first legal movement and action, route to A or B. | Any enemy-only setup information or saved forbidden input blocks competitive use. Missed high starts count as misses instead of extending the phase. |
| Seven entrances | Use the three ground protocols above for each of the four A mouths and three B mouths. Swap the observing side and run each pocket in the documented posture, then stand, then raise the observer. | Who could see whom, shoulder, posture, number of clear body samples and whether an ability was needed for access. Record moves into and out of cover separately. | One unreachable hide, an apparently solid curtain, a false safe label or a route requiring a specific hero triggers a geometry or labeling change. |
| Cues and counter actions | Shuffle the grounded escort, one committed flight, straight arrow and returning disc. Give defenders an open and a hard-cover choice. Use balanced order with sound present/muted and normal/reduced effects on both sides. | At least two independent players per scenario give their first recognized cue, inferred destination, actual defensive action and impact outcome. Sample fresh players separately from practiced players. | Reject a hidden-position telegraph, a single disc registering two hits on the same target, or an aerial channel. Missed reads require reviewing both a failure and successful counter from matched geometry. |
| Continuous play | Three groups of ten play four rounds on each side. Keep voluntary callouts and site choice, one independent observer per group. | Log 24 rounds with attempt/cast IDs, first arrival, site and exact point, supported/airborne state, interrupted and completed channel ticks, player-chosen route, win reason and number of eligible support events. | Correct broken reach, information leaks and inaccessible alternates immediately. Treat pick and site preferences as hypotheses. Twenty-four correlated rounds cannot establish win rate or a stable meta. |

For each round keep local build and source hashes, group and anonymised seat, side, player-experience band, start pad or floor, visibility setting, map clock, runner position and support, intended site if voluntarily declared, origin/release/impact/ground/ritual ticks, observed target and actual round result. A moderator can reproduce the failing action from these fields. Do not award progression for logging a plan, tapping an objective or repeating a cancelled action.
