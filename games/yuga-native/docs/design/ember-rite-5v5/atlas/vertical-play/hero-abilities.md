# Heroes above the ritual courts

A player at Ghat Mandapa should be able to tell three things before looking at the HUD. Where will Hanuman come down? Which side of the pillar will Krishna's disc pass? Can Rama see the target from his bow hand? These questions govern the vertical hero study. Hanuman and Rama can already play Confluence private matches. The ten Ember Rite hero concepts share a proposed competitive rig. Rama is an additional playable reference and Shiva is a motion-study appendix. The vertical testbed and map rehearsal are separate from the private-room build. For **any pitched shot**, range is total 3D path length. With a straight normalized aim at pitch `θ`, the clear outward height change is `r sin θ` and the horizontal reach is `r cos θ`. At 30°, Rama's 40 m studying ray can rise 20 m while covering 34.64 m horizontally. Arjuna's can rise 15 m and cover 25.98 m. Krishna’s 12 m cast can rise 6 m and cover 10.39 m before retracing. The geometric upward ceilings relative to the release socket are respectively 40, 30 and 12 m with a vertical aim. Walls, roofs, world bounds and intervening bodies normally shorten those lines. They are range budgets and do not confer flight, target acquisition, or clearance over a roof. At 20 Hz, a full-speed Rama arrow advances 3 m per step, Arjuna 2.4 m, the disc 0.8 m outward and 1 m returning. Sweep each segment against the expanded collider to catch a narrow wall. **Per-cast accounting.** Use (round, actor, cast, target, effect) for once-only receipts. Each different ally may receive its own legal support effect. A capped pool accepts only missing health. Shields use the greater strength and eligible expiry, never the sum. Identical slow fields use the strongest effect for their overlap; they cannot extend each other by bouncing contacts. A missed return or blocked flight closes that effect. Death, disconnect and round change cancel its pending contacts. Retain the consumed cast identity until its generation retires so a reconnect cannot replay a paid effect. Cooldowns start at accepted cast and prevent replayed inputs from purchasing extra effects. A teammate passing behind cover, a flier who cannot fit headroom, or a returning disc that reaches a wall must produce a readable miss/blocked cue. ## Research that changed the design

Blizzard's Pharah provides distinct controls for upward lift, hovering and horizontal dash, which led to separate rise, duration and distance budgets for Hanuman. Hanzo’s bow draw and wall climb distinguish a firing tell from movement and informed Arjuna’s separate preparation and release. Riot’s official Sivir data describes a crossblade that can deal damage each way. YUGA preserves the returning threat while using a per-cast target ledger for one payment across both legs. Valve's 16 September 2026 update discusses Lash's height-sensitive Ground Strike and Vindicta's Flight. Its example motivates the visible landing and local height cap, without importing those games' damage or cooldowns into YUGA. ## Round 3 · What a second body can change

The Rust ritual evaluates living, connected actors after damage. Before ignition, eliminating the last available attacker ends the round for defenders. After ignition the fire survives the final attacker. Eliminating all defenders ends the round for attackers. If both teams disappear on one tick, defenders win before ignition and attackers win after ignition. A carrier who dies or disconnects over the river drops the vessel at the last supported ground when the round can continue. A living, connected, grounded attacker with a valid held interaction can recover it within 1.5 m. An eligible carrier must hold 80 ticks at one site; a defender must hold 70 ticks in reach of the placed vessel. Damage, releasing the interaction, losing grounding, invalid reach or changing the acting seat breaks the channel. Two defenders holding together advance one tick of progress per tick. On takeover, the new defender starts at one tick. An untouched completion can count on the final eligible timer tick after lethal/elimination checks. [Authoritative state machine](../../../../../../yuga-rust/src/ritual.rs) · [Tie, interruption and takeover cases](../../../../../../yuga-rust/tests/ritual.rs)

For a late quench, place the vessel 2.8 m east of the centre. At 4.0 m east a grounded defender is 1.2 m from the vessel and outside the disk. At 4.4 m east the same defender is 1.6 m away and cannot quench. Krishna and Kali can interrupt only through real, timely contact. A disc that returns to a defender already paid on the outward leg cannot interrupt them again. In the escort play, healing after an already damaging tick cannot restore lost channel progress. The allied flyer may hold a separate approach, while a same-footprint body is neither walkable high ground nor a second planter. Verify actual airborne release, sightline, ground support and channel eligibility on the same tick before connecting the map lab to the host.

Recheck the numerical contracts with `python3 hero_rules.py` and `python3 -m unittest -v test_hero_rules` in this directory.
