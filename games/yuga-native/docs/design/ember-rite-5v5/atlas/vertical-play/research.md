# Why Mandala lets a player leave the ground

A ground carrier at Ghat Mandapa needs to know which doorway a flyer can see, how to cross under that sightline, and whether there is still time to kindle. These are three separate design questions. The study gives players a mobile setup period, vertical options with visible commitments and multiple grounded entries to both sites. Candidate rules and imported comparisons are dated 26 November 2026. [Encounter scripts](encounters.md) · [Existing round specification](../../ROUND_AND_TECH.md)

## Round 1 · Mechanics the panel could verify

### A round can start before combat does

Riot’s beginner guide describes buying and preparing before the round. Its closed-beta 0.50 notes move attacker barriers forward at A Main and B Main on Split, move the defender barrier back at A Ramps and make the backs of spawn barriers opaque. These are documented layout changes on 12 May 2020. In 1.07, Riot explicitly let Viper place Toxic Screen through spawn barriers during the buy phase and rejoin her team before the drop. That is an agent-specific exception. It does not establish a general entitlement to fire, place effects or see enemy setup before the start. [Riot beginner’s guide, 2 August 2024](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/announcements/beginners-guide/) · [Riot 0.50, map updates](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-0-50/) · [Riot 1.07, Viper, 1 September 2020](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-1-07/)

**Mandala decision.** Players have a reason to move and discuss the first commitment during the 20-second study preparation. Each side can choose a high or low start inside its own territory. Before release a team can change its own setup. Opponents receive no live enemy positions, silhouettes, aim or cast signs. An input rejected during preparation earns no attack on the opening tick. Time the first *reachable door*, not a mirrored distance between spawn centres. [Round-opening drill](encounters.md#1-the-last-second-of-preparation)

## Round 3 · What changed after cross-review

**A hiding place needs a bearing and posture.** The Split playtest gave us a reason to question whether adding high ground counts as a new strategic choice by itself. We compared the two pockets at every physical mouth against the registered observer. At Bazaar north a crouched player behind the Cart chest is hidden from the sampled incoming ground sightline, but six of nine body samples show when that witness is raised 4 m. The nearby Stamp screen shows zero from that same witness. At Bazaar mid the Market register exposes six samples and the Measure post exposes one, even though both support standing cover from the ground. We retained those asymmetries, named the specific threat and added a paired high/low trial. This gives an airborne escort information an opponent can contest. It leaves human camera and aim as separate measurements. [Seven doors and site witness table](encounters.md#seven-route-checks-for-the-observer) · [Geometry evidence](architecture.json)

**Ignition and quenching have different spatial rules.** The first roster draft accidentally made both actions require the player to stand inside the 3 m site disk. The original Rust state machine measures the quench from the *placed vessel*. The hero panel corrected its shared rule, and we exercised the physics gate directly. At A a grounded kindle at `(2.8,0)` succeeded. A grounded defender at `(4.0,0)`, 1.2 m from the vessel but outside the original disk, qualified. A defender 1.51 m away did not, and a defender with feet raised 0.1 m did not. The B point `(40,−2.5)` also qualified for ignition. These checks concern interaction eligibility, independent of a subsequent opponent interrupt. [Physics interaction model](physics.py) · [Corrected common hero rule](heroes.json) · [Original round authority](../../../../../../yuga-rust/src/ritual.rs)

**Shooting over cover needs a visible cost.** The physics witnesses give the proposed Gandiva line an eight-tick tell; a standing shot can hit the Ghat screen while a raised release can clear it and contact the exposed receiver. In the chakra test, the outward strike first meets a shield, turns back to its frozen anchor and can then catch a different, late crosser. A low disc meets the Bale bench at tick 7 of its scenario. At the South Temple fold a disc stops against the physical face instead of appearing at a distant exit. In the pavilion, an upward Kodanda arrow meets the cornice and a separate flight approaches the same ceiling at tick 13. The encounter director kept a real grounded alternative for each case. Human players still need to demonstrate that they can read the cues, make the counter and complete the objective under pressure.
