# Ember Rite · architecture and vertical hero play

A grounded hero entering **Bazaar Sanctuary** through North Pyramid can disappear behind either the Cart chest or the Stamp screen. The chest requires a crouch. Raise the observer by 4 m and six of nine sampled body points become visible behind the chest. The screen still conceals all nine from that bearing. Choosing where to land, send the disc or escort the vessel now changes which of those two positions is useful. [Walk the entrance and inspect the casts](https://occult-kranti.github.io/ANDOLAN/production/ember-rite/vertical-play.html)

This fieldbook covers **four districts, eight sites, 27 physical entrances and 54 entrance-specific shelter assignments**. Its detailed vertical and aerial study is Mandala Rift. There are 53 registered art subjects, four measured hero models, profiles for all ten proposed Ember heroes plus Rama and a Shiva motion-study appendix. The playable browser page is a solo ground rehearsal with inspectable fixed-tick records. Hanuman and Rama are the two playable kits in the existing private Confluence skirmish. Integrating this map study with competitive multiplayer is a separate milestone. ## Reading the scale

Plan coordinates are east and north in metres. In the survey triples, elevation is **third**. In engine and glTF triples, **Y is up**. The fold signs give destination headings in the local frame. A route’s catalogue width is the nominal width perpendicular to its centreline. Oblique entries and projecting furniture can move the usable two-person passage away from the exact mouth centre. [Survey data and traffic probes](architecture.json)

| Measurement | Value used here | What it controls |
|---|---:|---|
| Standing feet to eye / top | 1.60 / 1.80 m | A low counter may hide the torso while exposing the eye or crown. |
| Crouched feet to eye / top | 1.15 / 1.32 m | A 1.55 m table can certify a crouch from one bearing. A 1.00–1.05 m counter stays partial cover. |
| Body radius / conservative path clearance | 0.45 / 0.65 m | The independent ground browser and testbed use a 0.45 m body. The cover layout checks the wider Rust movement clearance. |
| Hanuman / Rama equipped render height | 2.0790 / 1.9195 m | These are rest-pose measures of skinned art. The receiver, camera and aim socket are measured separately. |
| Krishna / Shiva equipped study height | 2.0476 / 2.0431 m | Ornament and hair contribute to visual height. Neither model grants a taller competitive receiver. |
| Quarter-turn Pavilion | 10 × 8 × 9 m | Its walk-through bay is 5 m wide. The first soffit is at 4.23 m, leaving 2.43 m for the feet of a 1.80 m standing body. |
| Site centre / working apron | radius 3.0 / 4.5 m | One living grounded carrier kindles on the inner disk in 4 s. From ignition the 40 s clock and the 3.5 s pour apply at the actual vessel. |

The riverbank at +1.8 m and the Sluice at +1.4 m change the effective height of an observer before any ability fires. In the register the base and top of the mesh are world elevations. In the ability table the bow hand, poised disc and flight cap are relative to the hero’s supporting floor unless specified otherwise. The art extent of an awning, plume or roof is never enough to make it a foothold. [Art registration and special cases](element-register.md) · [Hero dimension sources](hero-abilities.md)

## Cover, clearance and entry

Ghat Mandapa’s Dry Bank, South Temple and North Temple approaches are each 7 m wide. Its Sluice approach is 5 m. Bazaar Sanctuary’s two Pyramid approaches are 6 m, with a 5 m approach from the west Sluice. Both courts measure 22 × 20 m. A player at either central disk can challenge an entering hero from a different angle, so the plans give separate reverse hides and peeks. The flood routes to each of the 14 incoming positions clear an occupied alternate and preserve the working apron. Hard solids block projectiles. Reeds and hanging twine supply art cues and receive no solid or full-hide credit. [Mandala signed witnesses](architecture.json) · [Encounter observer assignments](encounters.md)

The other sites retain their district geometry. A Lanka player enters the elevated Signal Court differently from the lower Ashoka Garden. Ayodhya’s Assembly Court stands 4 m above district datum while its Ferry Quay is at 0 m. Kurukshetra keeps a west approach into Palace Terrace and a wet Ford into Wind Field. Each entrance in these three districts has two certified standing positions behind separate pieces with an unobstructed lateral peek. Its witness stands just inside the room. Mandala has additional reverse and overhead observations because its registered 3D floor was independently probed. [Heights, approaches and positions at all eight sites](door-register.md)

The deepest Mandala hide certifies the entire 0.45 m receiver from the named ground eye. All 16 vertices of a circumscribed prism lie in the convex perspective shadow of the same opaque cover. A player can move up to 0.67 m from that hide to the original shoulder mark before peeking. The three other districts use four enclosing plan corners on level ground with a sufficient solid height. [28 directional whole-body certificates](mandala-body-cover.json)

At the nearby shoulder mark, cast sight segments from the incoming eye to nine points distributed across three heights and the two shoulders and centre. Each first opaque intersection must be the designated cover. Recompute from the court centre for a retake, then raise the incoming eye by 4 m and record visible samples. A peek needs a walkable path and a clear eye ray. Aim still originates at the actual release socket. Two allies with 0.65 m clearance can cross each of the seven mouths on centres 1.36 m apart. At Bazaar North Pyramid the pair needs a 1.20 m lateral shift, and at Bazaar Sluice it needs a 0.20 m shift. The drawing gives the actual two centres. ## Preparation, climbing and stacking

For the 20-second study preparation, each team may walk, take its home ladder and choose a forward loft within its own boundary. At tick 399 both curtains remain opaque and closed. At tick 400 the round accepts fresh live inputs. Set up a low escort at the curtain and a high observer on the loft to learn what each player gives up. A climb reserves its access, top entry and exit as appropriate so a teammate already occupying an endpoint can hold it. The four lofts have independent, usable egress. Stacking five roles in one area is legal where supported feet fit. A hero cannot stand on a teammate’s head, chain bodies into a ladder, fly the carrier into a free plant or speed a pour by layering interact inputs. ## Flight, arrows, chakras and team plays

Hanuman’s proposed **Maruti Ribbon** trades one charge and a 16 s cooldown for up to 2.4 s of thrust, a 12 m horizontal-path budget and 4 m of foot elevation above his launch support. He must land to rearm. Architecture can cut that ascent short. In the pavilion trial his feet reach 2.4298 m before the soffit stops him. On dry land, a high arrival also changes the enemy’s view of his landing and may break an ally’s short-range heal. [Hanuman physics and landing](physics.md) · [Kit totals, counters and visual poses](hero-abilities.md)

Rama’s **Kodanda** provides the live baseline at 60 m/s, 40 m range and 0.22 m projectile radius. The vertical lab sweeps it through 3D. Arjuna’s proposed **Gandiva Line** gives a visible 0.40 s wind-up, then travels at 48 m/s up to 30 m. Krishna’s **Sudarshana Return** travels out up to 12 m at 16 m/s and back to its recorded release point at 20 m/s. A single cast can catch a different late crosser on return. Cover, shields, a fold face and the pavilion cornice have explicit contact outcomes. Kindling still requires a grounded carrier at the site. In the 19th fixture a wall forms after the disc passes outbound and stops its return on tick 23. The line between the two Mandala centres is 40 m, leaving 34 m between the nearest edges of their 3 m disks. Distance and occlusion both matter on a cross-site shot. [Site sections and casts](https://occult-kranti.github.io/ANDOLAN/production/ember-rite/vertical-play.html#flight) · [Full numerical profiles](heroes.json)

Four named plays distinguish simultaneous positioning from additive power. **Two at Bazaar** lets Kali answer movement forced by Krishna’s returning disc. **Gate Rotation** lets Rama move to a new release angle beside Ravana’s temporary wall and optionally gives Ghatotkacha another supported approach. **Lift and Steward** layers Durga’s ward, Hanuman’s landing protection and Draupadi’s tether. Strongest-shield wins, so 28 and 48 yield 48. **Elder Screen** lets Shakuni mask one entrance while a pre-climbed Arjuna or Karna looks at a different shoulder. Each script states who spends a charge, the available response window, what breaks the chain and whether the objective is still legal.
