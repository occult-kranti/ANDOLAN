# Ayodhya · The Sarayu assembly quarter

Two river wards once signaled to each other with a pair of lamps. A dark wick might mean the far bank has lost power, or that its landing has washed away. One faction wants to relight the ferry signal. The other wants someone to check the landing first. From the high Assembly Court a team can see the roof of the ferry shed. It has to descend through the streets to reach the water. [Round script and endings](../ENCOUNTERS.md)

## A street circuit above a shallow wash

The outer circuit serves the potters, cloth porters and ferry crews. Four doorways face the inner Ledger Court. Use solid returns on its walls so the carrier can choose a destination there without one observer seeing both sanctums. The western terrace sits 4 m above the ferry quay. The measure beam over the court points toward the low landing, while the canopy at Assembly Court breaks the opposite skyline.

The quickest site switch cuts across the wash, from Ledger Court to Wash Steps. ## The high road through Measure Well

With Wash Steps closed to both teams, the fastest dry A–B route runs through Ledger Court → Sluice Gate → Measure Well → Ferry Landing. It costs **30.2 s**, down from **33.6 s** around the previous blocked-wash layout. Reserving the quench leaves **6.3 s** to find the lip, win the entrance or recover from a false read. Both the 28.0 s inner path and 24.5 s wade remain viable when Wash Steps is open. Have one defender call “east landing” while a second holds the wash. Swap the location of the planted vessel between the two lips and repeat.

At (35, 9) an 8 × 12 m stepwell study puts a level, 5 m-wide east–west causeway between stepped water markers. Three receding levels on the flanks make the descent recognizable. ## Site furniture, cues and a retake rehearsal

- **Learn the slope by sight.** Use a fan-shaped roof at the upper assembly entrance, one horizontal measure beam at mid and paired mooring drums at the low site. Mount ruled ceramic tiles and three deep landings above Gauge Steps. A chipped tile pattern can still indicate the dry side with reflections off. Keep the lamp housings dark during combat and reserve the answering ferry lamp for the resolution shot.
- **Reuse and sight test.** Take linked YUGA roof-band, lantern and foundation modules into the blockout. Mark the 19 m **Gauge descent** and the short **Wash exit** probes in collision-debug view. Run Rama and Hanuman toward each from both camera shoulders and check the planter's outline at dusk, in grayscale and with leaves and particles disabled. [YUGA Blender collections](../../../../../art/reuse-library/README.md)
- **Blind rehearsal.** On a B ignition, have a pair start at A. One uses the covered Sluice ramp while the other takes the wash. Ask the anchor to call only the crossing they actually observed. Repeat with the audio muted, then with the ford closed. If defenders lose the dry route after a single interruption, measure where the time went. If the gaze from Ledger sees both objective interactions, extend its east return and repeat the feint. Record both side assignments and the actual vessel point.
