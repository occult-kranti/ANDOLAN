# Mandala Rift

A copper wheel sits above the market with one cup suspended halfway to the top. People at the ghat still draw water by hand. At Ghat Mandapa the bearers can restart the lift from the intake. At Bazaar Sanctuary they can feed the market regulator instead. The keepers see a cracked casing at the Sluice and have good reason to close either circuit. This civic dispute is the setting for a five-against-five Ember Rite round. ## What the team chooses

The Sluice is a raised crossing between the sites. A team holding it can change its destination late, but the ramp turns below the lip on both sides. An observer has to leave the crest to check the vessel. A runner who takes the outside approach gives up that flexibility in return for a protected path to one site. A folded doorway buys time at the price of announcing two possible places for an opponent to look.

The drawing keeps the two sites, team start positions, and four fold mouths from the [earlier Mandala plan](../../../mandala-rift-5v5/README.md). North is positive Z. Attack begins at Spice Gate in the south and defence at Water Gate in the north. Players exchange duties at halftime. The new bank, central Sluice, angled entrances and market yards give those old landmarks distinct jobs. The proposed 136 × 256 m envelope stays the same.

## Two retakes to rehearse

**Ghat Mandapa · A.** The 22 × 20 m stone floor puts a flat 3 m ignition disk between a tall ribbed bell screen to the southwest and a low lectern to the northeast. Attackers at the west and south entries can split the defender’s attention. Practice lighting at the southern and northern lips, exactly 3 m from centre. Each time one defender descends through North Temple while another comes up Dry Bank. Have the first point to the actual vessel before starting the pour. A player who stares at the bell or the nominal centre can lose an otherwise recoverable round. The 16 m plan probe across the bell screen is intended to be blocked at body height. Test both shoulders from each end and check for a view through the ribs.

**Bazaar Sanctuary · B.** The 22 × 20 m floor has two deliberately different pieces of cover. The tall dye cabinet lets a player break sight to part of the ring. The waist-high weigh counter screens the runner’s legs while keeping their shoulders visible against plain plaster. Stage the vessel at the east lip, then the west lip. One defender comes via North Pyramid, a second descends from the Sluice. The first can interrupt if there is a legitimate view, or ask the second to clear the back of the cabinet. The 11 m probe from the south interior toward the north lip should be open. If the weighbeam, awning or a camera clipping through the cabinet contradicts that cue, move the offending art and replay.

At both sites the drawing leaves a further 1.5 m apron around the full disk clear of solids. Ignition takes four seconds. It fixes the vessel at the finishing position. Defenders then have forty seconds in which to reach within 1.5 m of that spot and complete a 3.5 s pour. A fully dry retake from the other site's centre leaves roughly 25.1 s after reserving the route and the pour, before reaction, opposition or a last-lip adjustment. A dropped vessel needs ordinary ground access along the same routes. [Ember Rite interaction contract](../../ROUND_AND_TECH.md)

## River, dry bank and fold doors

- **Reed Steps to Jar Steps.** The outside channel through Reed Ford sits 0.3 m below datum. Its banks hug the jungle wall. Water sounds and a local ripple make timing legible to a nearby opponent. The Dry Bank route climbs to +1.8 m and exposes a runner against a plain parapet. It offers the direct western turn into A. A team in the ford can leave partway round the loop by climbing the middle escape to Dry Bank. Use a visible bed, shallow ramps and lowered planting beds while keeping both heads and aim indicators clear of reeds.
- **Sluice.** The crest sits at +1.4 m on an 8 × 12 m landing. Lowering it from +2.4 m and narrowing its east–west footprint gives the market-side ramp 5 m of horizontal run between the flat room edges. A 1.4 m rise over 5 m gives a 28% grade directly across the connecting plane. Sample the exported floor at intervals of at most 0.25 m along the bent corridor centreline. Over a 2 m run, the steepest market-side grade falls from **54.8% to 25.6%**. On the ghat side it falls from **53.7% to 25.0%**. The gearbox now sits inside the northwest landing edge. Compare foot placement and the shoulder camera at that lip in the hero blockout. Set opaque masonry returns at both descent bends and check whether either shoulder can inspect *both* candidate ignition disks from a single crest position. [Exported floor geometry](../massing/mandala.glb)
- **Temple folds.** Each temple and its same-side pyramid share two paths. The 5.5 m conventional passage takes about 7.3 s door to door. The candidate fold takes 1.5 s, including its visible windup. One V notch marks the southern pair, two horizontal bars mark the northern pair. Each entrance spells out its destination. Captions and lit destination signs mirror the spatial cue at the same local range. The membranes are opaque. Keep the traveler on the source floor if the exit is occupied or the windup is interrupted. Rehearse a blocked exit with the carrier, a nearby teammate and an opponent on the walking bypass. The proposed authority must confirm the last grounded position before it can place the vessel. [Fold contract](../../../mandala-rift-5v5/IMPLEMENTATION.md)

## Turning the southern threshold

The Quarter-turn Pavilion sits over South Pyramid at (28, −32). Four receding roof tiers occupy a 10 × 8 m footprint and rise 9 m above the market floor. Inside the 12 × 18 m approach court, the walking arcade and Cloth Yard meet at the south mouth. The north mouth opens onto the curve to Bazaar Sanctuary. The central passage is 5 m wide. With both studies registered, the closest of the three walking centrelines stays 1.47 m from the pavilion masonry through the first 2.12 m of standing height. Keep the roof outline legible from Cloth Yard and the V notch legible at the mouth. The Chola temple group supplies a comparison for the receding upper form. The civic passage, dimensions and rotated thresholds belong to the game. [Pavilion study](../massing/mandala-architecture.glb) · [Architectural source M1](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/250/) · [Source applications](../RESEARCH_ROUNDS.md)

From South Temple to South Pyramid the fold turns the player’s facing 90° clockwise. North Temple to North Pyramid turns it 90° anticlockwise. The return uses the inverse turn. With +X east and +Z north, (x, z) becomes (z, −x) on the clockwise crossing. A north-facing carrier therefore arrives facing east and recovers the original heading by folding straight back. On the chart a bearing carried back by the ordinary arcade retains its eastward orientation. That disagreement is the map’s spatial puzzle. The opaque mouth and destination label allow the carrier to prepare for it before committing.

Give a first-time player a dry delivery and a folded delivery to the same site. Ask for the exit direction before each cross. Then ask the defending listener which two doors they could reasonably contest. Count the correct destination, facing and cue recipient separately. Teaching the turn earns route familiarity without increasing movement speed or revealing an enemy. The online skirmish still needs verified entry, destination clearance and heading replication.

## Art pass at shoulder height

At Hampi, a great bazaar street, pillared halls, waterworks and stepped tanks are parts of a settlement. That gives the fictional ghat, lifting gear and marketplace a shared purpose. The deep ribs above A and the square weighbeam over B read as different silhouettes even in grayscale. Keep architectural pattern above the silhouettes or in the stone skirting. ## First blockout decision

Begin with both fold pairs closed and the dry routes walkable. At each site send a Hanuman and Rama pair from different dry entrances, plant once on each far lip, then exchange sides for the retake. Stage Vanara Sena at the final turn on one approach and see whether the other pair can still reach a valid entry or challenge the field owner. Include a missed approach in the capture and record where the player believed it led. Open one fold pair afterward and repeat the same entry with the nearby opponent muted and captioned. If a single held angle controls both entries and the vessel, adjust the entrance return or cover before adding ornament.
