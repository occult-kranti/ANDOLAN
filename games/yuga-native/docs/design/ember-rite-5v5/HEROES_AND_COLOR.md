# Ember Rite · hero, role and color design

The ten characters below are **proposals inspired by the supplied brief**, not ten implemented Rust heroes. Each player may select an eligible hero on either Sun or Moon; the attack and defence roles swap without changing that hero. The current Rust skirmish has Hanuman and Rama only. Existing Hanuman ability names below are reference points; its current rules remain those in the [live kit handoff](../vanara-sena-2026/README.md) until a separately tested change is made. Rama remains playable and can be considered as an eleventh roster candidate rather than being silently removed.

The intended kit template is **two tactical abilities, one signature and one ultimate**. The supplied idea makes tactical charges buyable; for the first private prototype, compare fixed free charges with an optional **in-match** earn/spend economy before choosing. Real-money purchases must not change gameplay power. The concepts in this document have no accepted damage, costs, charges, collision, animation or cooldown numbers. Use placeholders for private design review; implement only after ability-by-ability server contracts, counterplay, human testing and accessibility. Names and weapon silhouettes should be original YUGA designs rather than copies of other games' branded weapons.

## Color and team readability

The original brief's 60/30/10 proportions are a **composition exercise**, not a physical law or a required costume template. The inspected [Britannica rasa overview](https://www.britannica.com/art/rasa) describes aesthetic experience and several emotions, not a universal formula assigning one emotion or moral character to a hex color. Terms in the user's draft such as “Maya Rasa” and “Shakti Rasa,” and claims such as saffron making a figure 20% larger, are not established by this source. Treat all color associations below as YUGA design intent, not canonical scripture or measured player psychology.

For every player, combine a configurable **ally triangle** and **opponent bracket** with labels, outline/pattern and accessible palette choices. A sample cool ally/warm opponent pairing may help, but red/green alone cannot convey allegiance; skin tone, dark clothing, fixed faction colors, name or a hero's palette never change visibility, damage, hitbox or morality. Support and attack cues get distinct shapes and captions in addition to colors. Avoid a dark costume giving automatic stealth or a larger artistic model silently changing collision.

The table's ratios are computed on **opaque, flat sRGB swatches**, against dark `#151B24` and pale stone `#D9C8A3`. They show why no hue works on every background; they are not measurements of textured/transparent game frames or proof of accessibility. W3C guidance calls for non-color distinctions and, where applicable to UI, 3:1 contrast for essential graphical indicators and 4.5:1 for ordinary text. Test actual composited frames, displays, outlines, color-vision settings, day/night and both teams.

## Role and ability concepts

Each row proposes a signature, two tacticals and an ultimate. Parentheses state counterplay and cooperation; none changes the vessel rule or grants an automatic plant, information through cover, invulnerable quench or guaranteed kill.

| Hero and role | Signature | Tactical 1 / tactical 2 | Ultimate and team value / counters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Arjuna · marksman/duelist** | **Gandiva Line:** a committed precise shot along a visible aim line. | **Side Step:** short collision-limited reposition / **Shared Sight:** a visible ping on an opponent actually seen by the server. | **Sky Arc:** telegraphed arrow sequence; teammates can rotate while enemies break line of sight or pressure the committed archer. It cannot ignite a site remotely. |
| **Hanuman · initiator/support** | **Windward Vault:** collision-limited space-taking movement. | **Protective Fan:** directional ally protection / **Vanara Sena:** one visible, temporary ground field. | **Mountain Pulse:** a marked impact and ally-support opportunity; opponents leave or take cover. Preserve live timings and counters when studying adaptation; no extra autonomous army. |
| **Kali · entry duelist** | **Crescent Advance:** a short committed strike after a readable windup. | **Ash Veil:** short vision blocker affecting both teams / **Break Step:** brief lateral movement with a clear endpoint. | **Dance of Thresholds:** a telegraphed sequence of bounded arcs; allies follow an opening, while enemies back away, use cover or interrupt the wielder. It does not make a site impossible to quench. |
| **Krishna · controller/support** | **Guiding Note:** a small, visible ally-support pulse. | **Maya Screen:** a two-sided sight blocker / **Echo Feint:** a clearly bounded decoy that cannot reveal hidden enemies or fake a confirmed server event. | **Still Horizon:** a clearly telegraphed, short control field with an escape route; its exact status effect needs testing and must not freeze a whole match or override objective rules. |

Healing and assistance earn clear feedback; being near the objective grants no hidden power. |

## Story references and presentation gates

Arjuna's archery, Hanuman's mountain-bearing service, Krishna's devotional traditions, Kali and Durga's multiple representations, Ravana's role in the Ramayana, and Draupadi, Karna, Shakuni and Ghatotkacha's Mahabharata connections are a **starting research map**, not an exhaustive characterization. [Sources and limitations](SOURCES_AND_ROADMAP.md) name the accessible references actually inspected. Existing [museum objects](../../research/art-history-panel-2026/README.md) and [character art](../../../art/dharmic-heroes-key-visual-2026/README.md) can inform poses and material studies; the generated picture is not a rig or a final character.

Before an ability or hero can enter a match, document a control, tell, hit/visibility rule, team interaction, interrupt/counter, audio and reduced-effects equivalent, plus death/disconnect/round-reset behavior. For four- or multi-arm silhouettes, separately review actual arm attachment, weapon contact, animation blending and targetable bounds. User-supplied heights and palette percentages are art proposals, not physics or fairness measurements.
