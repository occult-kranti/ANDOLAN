# Heroes at the threshold

Hanuman is the pick to try when a wounded vessel escort and an opponent meet at one doorway. Rama is the pick to try when a team needs to hold a second angle during a rotation. Both heroes can play either side. Their new actions run in the Rust Confluence skirmish and private rooms. The four Ember Rite districts supply rehearsals for the proposed objective match. Human pick rates, win rates and preferred lineups for those districts are still unknown. [Playable build](../../../../yuga-rust/README.md) · [District atlas](atlas/README.md)

## Round 1 · Find the missed opportunity

Hanuman's Vanara Sena already punished opponents who stood in a small circle. Bringing the formation to a hurt teammate crossing that same circle gave the escort no immediate recovery. Rama could move up to 4.5 m while holding his aim with Measured Step, yet arrived with exactly the ammunition he had before committing. These openings reward different decisions. Keep Hanuman's forward shield as the first choice for incoming pressure and Rama's Guardian Circle as the first choice for a sustained stationary hold. [Hanuman's five base actions](../../../assets/design/hanuman-ability-kit.json) · [Sena field data](../../../../yuga-rust/assets/vanara-sena.json) · [Rama's base kit](../../../assets/design/rama-ability-kit.json)

The Vanara host assembling around its leaders in Griffith's *Ramayana* translation gave the team a basis for coordinated movement. The medicinal mountain in the Met's Rajasthan painting gives Hanuman a recognizable image of aid. The five echoes, once-only recovery and archer's quiver return are YUGA mechanics. In a round debrief, ask the escort which teammate they meant to help and what made the result legible. This tests the shared-goal and competence rationale in the research against actual player experience. ## Round 2 · Change the abilities

| Rust/PlayCanvas action | Previous result | New result | Limits the opponent or player can use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hanuman · V · Vanara Sena | 12 enemy damage on arrival plus 8/s for 2 s. Zero ally recovery. | At tick 12, each wounded teammate with clear sight within the 3 m disk recovers up to 12 HP. The 40 subsequent field ticks still apply only the recurring enemy damage. | The caster is excluded. Cover, elevation, the 10 m placement range, the 0.6 s windup and the 20 s cooldown all apply. A late-arriving teammate misses the recovery. Classifying the target as an ally follows the caster's current team. |
| Rama · E · Measured Step | A step of up to 4.5 m held the aim direction and returned zero arrows. | At the final movement tick, actual horizontal travel of at least 3 m returns one spent arrow. The ordinary 12-arrow quiver, aim direction, cast recovery and 5 s cooldown still apply. | A wall can shorten a legal step below the threshold. An interrupted movement or a full quiver yields zero. A returned arrow still needs an accepted draw, a clear flight and a hit. |

For a 300 HP teammate, a full Sena recovery is 4% of maximum health. Four eligible companions in a 5v5 could receive 48 HP together on one landing. A teammate at 296 receives four. With one Hanuman the ceiling is 12/20 = 0.6 HP/s per continuously eligible teammate, averaged over recasts. A field cannot cover two door centres more than 6 m apart. Crossing 3 m from the centre at the movement model's 5 m/s costs 0.6 s on level open ground. A player moving at impact could take roughly 12 + 0.6 × 8 = 16.8 damage there, compared with 28 if they stay for both seconds. Shields, walls, latency and collisions change the observed result. [Authority and exact contact rules](../../../../yuga-rust/src/combat.rs) · [Measured site entrances](atlas/BALANCE_MATH.md)

Rama needs at least two thirds of the unobstructed step distance to recover an arrow. Repeated successful steps can return at most 1/5 = 0.2 arrows/s while the quiver has space. A normal Kodanda hit is 28 damage before mitigation. Committing E gives at most one *chance* to produce that shot or allows Rama to save his 1.6 s reload for a covered corner. At 5 m/s the same 4.5 m takes 0.9 s of level walking. A clear Step reaches its endpoint on tick 7, or 0.35 s after acceptance, for a modeled saving of 0.55 s over that short segment. No hero action changes the standalone Ember Rite channel clock. Ignite takes 4 s, quench takes 3.5 s, and accepted positive shield or health damage interrupts a channel. A 2 s Sena field can force a later hold. A Guardian Circle can protect a survivor but cannot by itself complete the interaction. ## Round 3 · Verify before moving the meta

In the default drill, the Sun escort begins at 90/120 HP. Aim V just east of the escort. A cast around (3.9, −51.5) reaches the companion and misses all three guards. The escort reaches 102, the drill credits the technique once, and Hanuman stays at 275. For Rama, spend a Kodanda shot, step in the open and check the quiver. Repeat near the wall and compare the distance before counting an opportunity. The HUD description, practice coach and impact feedback read the kit or its authoritative events. [Drill and wall regressions](../../../../yuga-rust/tests/vanara_sena.rs) · [Rama regression](../../../../yuga-rust/src/rama.rs) · [Practice-coach check](../../../../yuga-rust/tools/test_practice_coach.mjs)

The ten-seat regression mirrors each hero's scenario across Sun and Moon. It checks the exact radius boundary, a receiver just outside, a nearly full teammate, one impact visible to another seat, 28 damage over the full enemy exposure and the caster's own health. In a second case, two landings reach a teammate at 296/300. Both casters receive two HP of credit and the teammate finishes at 300. Another companion takes lethal damage on that same tick and receives zero. Rama's scenario spends an actual arrow, rejects a duplicate step and compares the seat's quiver and event stream with a remote roster view. The separate wall and interruption cases exercise both kits on the real Confluence collision data. [Private 5v5 regression](../../../../yuga-rust/src/battle.rs)

The default Rust suite passed 101 tests. Two optional tests, the live Godot comparison and the twenty-minute simulated match, passed in a separate release run. At the arena edge 3.00 m of actual movement returned an arrow and 2.99 m returned zero. [Commands, results and source hashes](evidence/hero-balance-tests.json)

The first human session should log separate matches, teams, sites, sides and player experience. For Sena count legal impacts with a wounded visible teammate, recipients actually aided, and distinct opposing entries that remained usable. For Step count completed casts with a spent arrow, their measured distance, the credited arrow, and whether it was later fired before reloading. Count outcome once per cast or eligible teammate, record the denominator and report sample size. Ask each player to identify the second route and the enemy's tell. Preserve situational favorites until side-swapped matches give a reason to change the options available to the other roles. [Panel research and limits](atlas/RESEARCH_ROUNDS.md)
