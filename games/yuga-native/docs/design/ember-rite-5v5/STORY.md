# Ember Rite · story and cinematic script

## The fictional setting

In a speculative 2080, a network of ancient-looking sanctuaries appears as a **Confluence** of remembered places. A luminous energy source called the Ember is destabilizing the network. Its architects believed it could be used to heal damaged cities; their incomplete records also describe a dangerous way to concentrate it into a weapon. The Ember and its technology are inventions for YUGA, not claims about a real yajna, Agni, a historical reactor or an authentic scripture.

Two fictional coalitions disagree about how to respond. The **Deva Covenant** wants to contain the Ember until its effects are understood. The **Asura Concord** argues that controlled renewal could save communities now; within it, Shakuni secretly seeks to manipulate the network for personal power. Their members are not a uniform moral category, and people may disagree within either coalition. Krishna urges restraint and shared responsibility; Shakuni treats the uncertainty as an opportunity. Neither is presented as a historical quotation.

The playable figures are YUGA's fictional **echoes** of legendary figures, with visual and narrative interpretations to be reviewed against sources and living traditions. They can be selected on either player team. Their inclusion does not make every deity or community a combat faction, and a team does not become a different people when roles swap at halftime.

The weapon named in the inspiration, Brahmastra, can be an unresolved story reference rather than a guaranteed post-match spectacle. In a round, the carrier brings an ember vessel to a sanctum and channels it. A successful fire stabilizes or redirects one local node for that round; an extinguish prevents that transfer. The round result does not by itself destroy the world or change the rules of later matches.

## Optional round and result lines

These are sample original voice directions, not recordings. Provide captions and a volume control; do not depend on spoken lines alone.

- Round start: “Ember secured. Choose your route.”
- Carrier dropped, allies only: “The vessel is down.” Do not reveal its location to unseen opponents through voice or minimap.
- Ignition begins when observable: “A sanctum is being kindled.” The warning's range and visibility require server design.
- Fire active: “The Ember is awake.” Show the selected site and timer to both sides.
- Successful quench: “The light is contained.”
- Match ends: “The Confluence remembers your choice.” Show the actual score, result and round reason without implying a global world change.

## Seasons · proposed, not live

1. **The Fracture:** introduce the two coalitions, the two-site objective and the first roster proposals.
2. **The Dice Game:** spotlight Shakuni, already introduced in the initial story, and investigate the altered records. A Dice Hall map is a future proposal, not an existing map or a duplicate new-hero launch.
3. **The Forest Exile:** open a forest/river chapter and evaluate Bhima as an additional hero, subject to asset and balance gates.
4. **The Return:** revisit the Ember's origin. A cosmic Krishna visual is a proposed story/cosmetic treatment, not an automatic new competitive character or an unearned gameplay power.

If community wins ever influence story, measure opt-in eligible matches over a declared season window, account for attack/defence exposure and abuse, publish the calculation and tie/outage fallback, and use the result only to select between pre-authored narrative variants. Preserve the same rules, maps and access for all players. A raw 51% faction tally is not implemented and would not by itself prove a fair or reliable world state.
