# Action combinations and opponent responses

These examples use the current Confluence profiles without adding combo damage or forced outcomes. A tick is 50 ms and measured from the first accepted action. Targets can move, cover can block effects, cooldowns still apply and authority receipts decide the result. The examples are timing exercises for a player test, not a balance finding. [Derived timings and profile hashes](baseline.json) · [Detailed ability pass](HANUMAN_RAMA.md)

| Sequence | Earliest nominal timeline | Opponent response and review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hanuman vault → rear sweep | E starts at tick 0. In clear travel it lands by tick 13 and unlocks at tick 17. If C starts at tick 17, its rear burst occurs at tick 26. | The target can move in front of the new facing or leave the 5 m rear sector. A wall may shorten the E landing, so test the real landed position and avoid a precomputed dust location. |
| Hanuman rear sweep → Mountain Pulse | C bursts at tick 9 and its field lasts through tick 49. After body unlock at tick 17, start X. Its impact occurs at tick 40, leaving 9 ticks before the earlier field ends. | This can overlap existing damage rules when the target actually remains eligible for both. Opponents can leave either fixed footprint or move behind relevant cover. Confirm two independent cast IDs and unchanged sector origin. |
| Hanuman shield → gada | Q grants at tick 7 and unlocks at tick 12. LMB begun then reaches impact at tick 17. The original shield lasts up to 60 ticks after its grant. | A defender can move behind Hanuman or outside his 3.5 m fan. An ally outside the forward shield fan or behind cover gets no grant. Inspect the two different shapes where the support and strike overlap. |
| Rama step → Piercing Dawn | In clear movement E releases at tick 2 and completes its nominal step at tick 7. Q begun at tick 7 releases its arrow at tick 20. | Cover can shorten the step or stop the arrow. The target can change lane during the Q draw. Confirm the bow muzzle follows the new actor position and the shot uses the newly accepted aim. |
| Rama Guardian Circle → Dawn Volley | C grants at tick 11 and unlocks at tick 17. X begun then fires arrows at ticks 36, 38 and 40. The original circle remains active through tick 101. | Opponents can take cover or move between volley lanes. Healing requires Rama to meet the field's live recipient conditions. An already granted ward can remain after leaving until it is depleted or the field expires. Show each projectile and the independent fixed circle without covering the health bar. |
| Rama primary → Piercing Dawn | The primary releases at tick 4 and unlocks at tick 8. Q begun then releases at tick 21. Both projectiles continue according to their own collision tests. | The defender can block either arrow independently. A target can move during the longer Q draw. Never merge the primary's one-hit projectile into the piercing projectile's six-target limit. |
| Hanuman shield with Rama circle | Two teammates can overlap their support footprints. Each caster still uses its own accepted target, visibility test and shield ownership. | Test both cast orders, a pre-existing stronger shield, actual damage and each expiry. Confirm that one shield's cleanup does not incorrectly restore or erase the other's value. The current code does not establish a stacking bonus. |
| Hanuman Pulse with Rama volley | Hanuman's fixed disk impacts at its tick 23. Rama's three arrows use his own tick 19/21/23 offsets after his independently accepted X. | Players can exit the mark, cross the gaps in the lines, or use cover. Do not synchronize the two starting ticks implicitly. The target and each arrow keep separate receipts and counterplay. |

For proposed Shiva and Krishna, test their interaction only after both abilities have real server identifiers. One candidate pairs a visible Stillwater Coil perimeter with the one-time Govardhan protection grant. A defender can leave the slow field, and an ally must be alive, visible and inside the canopy radius at its release. No healing, extra slow or shield refresh is implied by overlapping visuals.
