This handoff records a five-pass review by combat, physics, animation, world and art-history owners, with a source librarian and integration lead. Work began from `d1cad241`. Hanuman and Rama's five-action Confluence match and the separate 14-hero classic sparring roster have different rules. The panel's proposed Shiva and Krishna kits use existing animated development rigs. ## Results to use

- The current Hanuman authority clears vault height on interruption. Its area effects compare each fighter's pre-tick feet elevation and body height with the ability's vertical band. Rama's separate projectile and ward elevation policy remains open. [Physics evidence](../../research/physics-panel-2026/README.md)
- The camera solver tests the desired view against the same 16 building and deck boxes. It retracts near a wall and can widen its lens within a cap. Its framing flag covers the body-axis head and feet, and animated weapons and tail still need visual checks. [Camera helper](../../../../yuga-rust/web/camera-clearance.ts) · [Regression](../../../../yuga-rust/tools/test_camera_clearance.mjs)
- Mountain Pulse's in-game visual uses a reduced CC0 rock with its PBR texture maps. Its trajectory and brief impact accents follow the existing cast snapshot. The projectile is presentation and carries no collision or damage. [Rock credits](../../../../yuga-rust/web/art/mountain-rock-NOTICE.txt) · [Cue test](../../../../yuga-rust/tools/test_mountain_cue.mjs)

The [four illustrated boards](../../../art/ability-keyframes-2026/README.md) are concept art, with corrected weapon continuity and provenance.

The [world panel](../../research/world-panel-2026/README.md) retains its candidate, paired captures and unpromoted placement decisions.

This handoff's publication receipt was added in a subsequent documentation-only commit. [Publication receipt](evidence/publication.json)

The final locked Rust suite passed 73 tests with two existing tests ignored. The packaged browser practice passed 39 checks, including all five coach techniques and reset. Separate practice, lane and both-team duel probes checked 20 successful casts, eight denied casts and 5,376 repeated spatial samples. The package's Mountain Pulse report checks flight, impact sparks and cleanup. [Rust test log](evidence/rust-tests.txt) · [Browser report](evidence/browser-practice.json) · [Practice capture](evidence/browser-practice.png) · [Ability probes](evidence/hanuman-release.json) · [Mountain cue](evidence/mountain-cue.json)

These automated browser tests used software rendering. They do not prove physical-GPU performance, public-host capacity or human competitive balance.

The packaged camera replay also passed six staged-transport cases: Sun and Moon at desktop, portrait and wide viewports. Each uses a real 240-step Wasm movement snapshot near the building, checks camera near-plane clearance and body-axis framing, and independently reprojects pointer-to-ground aiming. The close Sun-side camera leaves the hero very large on screen, so competitive framing still needs design review. [Camera report](evidence/camera-browser.json) · [Sun capture](evidence/camera-sun.png) · [Moon capture](evidence/camera-moon.png) · [Reproduction script](../../../../yuga-rust/tools/test_camera_browser.mjs)

The new concept art, isolated Blender renders and prototype map do not establish a finished commercial-quality game. A production acceptance still requires target-device frame pacing, human competitive playtests, live network conditions, collision-aware world art and artist review of the complete animated models.
