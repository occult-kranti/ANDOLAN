Line numbers refer to the exact pinned electronic editions, not universal
verse numbering. The original epic traditions are literary sources; these
English translations are mediated witnesses, not critical Sanskrit editions.

Edition uncertainty matters to the design sheet. Griffith's [note 787,
66171–66178](https://github.com/GITenberg/The-R-m-yan-of-V-lm-ki-translated-into-English-verse_24869/blob/b48a7a226e8a20bed61131382aa5b8bf2206e039/24869-0.txt#L66171)
explicitly reports omissions of descriptive passages in Book V; note 894,
66570–66575, describes two further cantos not reproduced there. His evaluative
judgments are the translator's. A negative search in this edition cannot prove
that a motif is absent from all Ramayana traditions. Kamban, Tulsidas, regional
performance/costume sources and Sanskrit critical editions were not read in
this pass. Record an artist's selected tradition before calling a costume
traditional; the briefs below deliberately identify their costume choices as
original.

## Six implementable briefs

All acceptance criteria below are **proposed checks**, not measured results.
Use the current fixed camera and a declared 1280 × 960 capture setting for
comparison; include normal gameplay distance and 20/40 m distance references
where relevant. Those distances are review fixtures, not already proven LOD
thresholds. Work from the existing editable reconstruction and its equivalence
limits. These briefs do not authorize replacing the accepted runtime asset
without a new review.

| Brief / owner | Source → authored choice | Readability requirement | Artist deliverable and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MY-01 · Rama's held bow silhouette** — character artist; R2 identity/reference constraints, R3 costume/bow production | R1-bow → preserve a clear long-bow arc, visible middle grip, off-arm negative space and one asymmetrical drape. Retain indigo/gold as the project's palette. Choose forehead/hair/ornament forms in an **original costume** sheet rather than attributing them to this passage. | Bow separates Rama from bow guards through scale, arc and held posture; shoulders, drawing elbow and hand remain visible through cloth. No tiny trim should carry identity. | Front/side/back orthographic sheet, black silhouettes at the three distances, costume layers/material zones, and an edit list against current mesh/rig. Review grayscale day/night silhouettes with bow guards: reviewers must locate hero, grip and drawing arm without a HUD. R2 consumes identity/reference constraints only and keeps unrelated bow/costume geometry fixed; any approved silhouette/drape edit belongs to R3. Face identity needs its own close-up gate; text does not supply an anatomical model. |
| **MY-02 · Controlled draw, visible release** — animator R4 + ability artist R5 | R1-bow's steady handling → calm planted support, bow-hand continuity, rising drawing elbow, release then a short recovery. Keep native Kodanda, Piercing Dawn, Measured Step, Guardian Circle and Dawn Volley as authored mechanics. Use one restrained stroke along the arrow path; lotus ward and solar line work remain original graphic choices. | Anticipation, release, contact and expiry must have different visual states. A cloth snap, bow recoil or light flare must follow its actual event. Three paced ultimate shots must read as three; do not cite M1's simultaneous five-shot demonstration as their source. | Contact sheet plus uninterrupted start→draw→release→recover and move→draw→cancel footage, including day/night ward expiry and cover-limited step. Match the existing kit's 20 Hz release receipts, not an invented animation timer. Inspect hand/string contact, shoulder continuity, feet, canceled casts and zero pre-release impact. Keep ward perimeter visible against both water and pale paving; record event/frame alignment. This is an animation/effect proposal, not new powers. |
| **MY-03 · Five-role army silhouettes** — soldier/vehicle artist; world/minions R6 | R1-city's guards and M1-arms' weapon distinctions → one shared original civic military cloth/material family, with role carried by weapon/upper-body contour. The last three contour choices and cannon technology are game inventions. | Separate role from team: the same outline should identify the same role on Sun/Moon. Keep commander's ornament below hero salience. Background banners must not duplicate unit pennants or objectives. | Five-role × two-team silhouette/material sheet and short idle/march/windup/release/recovery/hit/death loops; vehicles also need siege deploy/fire/reload. Add a collision envelope overlay for each. In mixed day/night 20/40 m frames, reviewers identify role before seeing labels; failures record which pair was confused. Check vehicle facing/impact danger and foot/wheel contact. Do not label teams Pandava/Kaurava, good/evil or infer troop ethnicity from the passages. |

A1's well/step forms, if used, get a separate historical-reference label. | Leave foot silhouettes, ward boundary and stepping edges visible. Ambient particles do not mimic arrows or poison damage. Plants in decorative regions must not imply cover unless collision/visibility rules agree. | Wet/dry bank modules, three reed-density variants and water/material breakdown; hero + troop crossing capture, Guardian perimeter across shore, and bright/dark water comparison. Show walkable border and depth rule on the plan. Reject invisible ledges, submerged target silhouettes and opaque foreground reeds. | **MY-06 · Courtyard light and quiet woven band** — prop/material/lighting artist; R6 | R1-lamps → few human-scale lamp groups and warm entrance pools; M1-weave → a narrow original light/dark paving band away from the main firing lane.
