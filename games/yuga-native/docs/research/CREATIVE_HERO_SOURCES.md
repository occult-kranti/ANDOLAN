## Original authored decisions

The mirrored-arrow eclipse, ten-crown iris, horizon needle, bronze crescent gate, mountain procession, reversed sunwheel, fault drum, storm scroll, living court, fivefold assembly, twin banner procession, walking grove pair, crossing oath and braided water-serpent basin are **new visual inventions**. Their colours, choreography, geometry, budgets, counters and timing proposals are game-design decisions, not textual claims about canonical powers.

Sita, Draupadi, Subhadra, Hidimbi, Chitrangada and Ulupi receive individual silhouettes, faces, material language, physical stance and complete primary/Q/E/R briefs. Subhadra's commander/spear role, Chitrangada's spear-duellist role and every numerical restorative/shield/slow/summon rule remain adaptations. Hidimbi is an individual guardian rather than a generic monster skin; Ulupi's water association grants no water immunity. Teams and strength do not depend on religion, caste, language, skin tone or epic allegiance.

Only the three already timed browser Rs preserve an existing preparation contract. Eleven other R preview windups need a separate authority migration before competitive adoption. More elaborate art cannot add projectiles, summon count, wall penetration, melee hits, immunity, terrain or damage ticks. All catalogue demonstrations explicitly apply no gameplay mutation.

## Tool and reuse decisions

Retain Godot 4.6.3, Blender and glTF. The [reusable-system study](REUSABLE_GAME_SYSTEMS.md), [character audit](CHARACTER_REUSE_AUDIT.md) and [reference import](REFERENCE_CHARACTER_IMPORT.md) distinguish permitted tool/motion studies from proprietary assets. No new dependency or third-party art is adopted. Earlier REA/Ghidra work exercised an original C fixture, not a recovered commercial character pipeline.

Use existing permitted anatomy/rig studies only with their recorded notices and an owned contact/retarget review. This catalogue does not replace production Rama or promote rejected carry motion. Concept prompts are inputs: an image is not a mesh, rig, animation or measured outcome. No “4D/5D” file format is asserted; the deliverables are animated 3D assets and rendered time sequences.

## Counterarguments still requiring evidence

- Spectacle can hide aim. Preserve clear centres, low boundaries, team shapes and reduced-effects fallback; inspect overlapping casts in motion.
- Shared baseline kinds still limit mechanical variety. Unique forms are not fourteen newly implemented mechanics; rules need separate simulation/network/balance gates.
- Demonstration windups can misteach browser timing. Label the preview and expose the retained baseline; never silently delay live authority.
- Budgets are provisional. Four simultaneous previews do not qualify a twelve-player fight on consumer hardware.
- More concepts do not repair hands, feet, faces or cloth. The next production proof is one complete continuously animated hero and encounter.
