# Blender sources for the Hanuman reconstruction

The reconstruction uses the editable Blender master and the generated Hanuman keyframe. The large visible gaps are the hero’s weight and stance, shoulder-carried gada, fuller head and jaw, folded cloth, fur breakup and dusk temple depth. Model these as editable 3D surfaces and judge a new Cycles render against the target. Keep shared skeleton, skin and playable GLB changes on the separate in-game acceptance path. ## Anatomy and gesture practice

Ayal Pinkus’s Practice Drawing This provides a model index, rotatable reference views and configurable construction studies. The index credits the skull to martinjario, the ribcage to Andy Johnson and Hadi Halimi, and the static hand to thunthu. The site declares its contents copyrighted unless otherwise noted. The study below uses the public viewers to learn how forms change across angles. Save original YUGA sketches and Blender views as evidence. Obtain an explicit asset license before integrating any third-party geometry or imagery. [Site and copyright notice](https://practicedrawingthis.com/) · [Model index and credits](https://practicedrawingthis.com/cgi-bin/3d-models-index.cgi)

Chromium opened the skull and ribcage viewers. Their canvas changed when dragged. In Dynamic Hand, Chromium rendered the mesh and set index-finger curl to 60 degrees and thumb opposition to −25 degrees. In Dynamic Torso, it rendered the mesh and set hip pitch to 12 degrees and hip yaw to −18 degrees. The gesture primer page title is available, but its lesson media could not be verified in the cloud. ## Form, symmetry and composition follow-up

- Rotate the construction box and cylinder in the browser, then sketch the same views with an original Blender shoulder block and gada shaft. Check consistent shoulder-plane convergence and the projected ellipse of the shaft and bracer. Chromium rendered and rotated both viewers and the organic forms viewer. The warm-up article also lists stacked boxes. [Rotating boxes](https://practicedrawingthis.com/3d/model-construct-box-rotation.html) · [Cylinder](https://practicedrawingthis.com/3d/model-cylinder.html) · [Organic forms](https://practicedrawingthis.com/3d/model-various-organic-forms.html) · [Primitive warm-ups](https://practicedrawingthis.com/cgi-bin/carousel.cgi?section=online-tools&episode=rotating-primitive-forms-warm-ups)
- Establish the sash’s fold origins at the waist and show its twist in a three-quarter clay render. The twisted-box reference returned its HTML, but the Chromium request returned 503. Use a simple twist drawn on the local asset before adding fine folds. Pinkus’s composition article discusses balancing tight and loose detail in a single image. Apply that to Hanuman with a readable face and grip, broader treatment of the court, and clean openings around the raised elbow, gada and tail. Save a scaled-down original composition and annotate the obscured landmarks. [Twisted box](https://practicedrawingthis.com/3d/model-twisted-box.html) · [Composition article](https://practicedrawingthis.com/cgi-bin/carousel.cgi?section=newsletter-archive&episode=blog-2022-02-20-how-to-use-the-concept-of-balance-yin-and-yang-in-your-art-compositions-and-creative-processes-)
- Compare rest anatomy across the armature-local `X = 0` plane. Blender’s Mirror modifier acts in object-local axes around the origin or a chosen mirror object. Use it while constructing a new paired part. Sculpt Mirror reflects brush strokes on local axes. Mesh Symmetrize copies data from a chosen side, including UVs, color attributes and vertex weights. Try it on a copy if a repair needs retopology, and inspect normals, seam placement and changed weights before applying it to a rigged piece. The checker samples the head, torso and lower legs of the body mesh with at least 100 vertices beyond the 1 mm midline band on each side. It measures both directions with a 0.1 mm 95th-percentile limit and a 0.5 mm maximum. Pose, gaze, swept hair, costume overlaps and the gada are reviewed for their intended asymmetry. [Mirror modifier](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.3/modeling/modifiers/generate/mirror.html) · [Sculpt symmetry](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.3/sculpt_paint/sculpting/tool_settings/symmetry.html) · [Mesh Symmetrize](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.3/modeling/meshes/editing/mesh/symmetrize.html) · [Keyframe verification](../../tools/hanuman-match/check_match.py)

In Blender 4.3.2, a four-vertex half panel produced two mirrored faces and two welded seam vertices. Applying a one-sided offset and running directional Mesh Symmetrize restored the reflected geometry. An isolated Hanuman copy with a 9 mm one-sided cheek edit failed the anatomical check, while the rest of its keyframe checks passed.

## Optional artist extension

LoopTools 4.7.7 from Blender Extensions requires Blender 4.2 or later and declares GPL-2.0-or-later. In an isolated Blender 4.3.2 profile, the `mesh.looptools_relax` smoke fixture moved all 24 uneven vertices by more than 1 cm and preserved 24 vertices and 24 edges. Blender also resolved its bundled `EXPORT_SCENE_OT_gltf` operator in that profile. This validates the tool setup and edge-loop operation. The automated Hanuman reconstruction uses the normal factory-startup profile. [Blender Extensions listing](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/looptools/)

Use a fresh artist profile directory to repeat the setup. Run Blender with these variables only for artist sessions using the extension. The command adds a local repository and enables that one reviewed version.

```sh
profile=$(mktemp -d /tmp/yuga-looptools.XXXXXX)
zip="$profile/add-on-looptools-v4.7.7.zip"
sha=ff1ca3b3fff73094379da8b1fa2c1acbc9d88d26b7dfc73bb9de5941a6b50108
curl --fail --silent --show-error --location -o "$zip" \
  "https://extensions.blender.org/download/sha256:$sha/add-on-looptools-v4.7.7.zip"
printf '%s  %s\n' "$sha" "$zip" | sha256sum --check
mkdir -p "$profile/config" "$profile/extensions"
export BLENDER_USER_CONFIG="$profile/config"
export BLENDER_USER_EXTENSIONS="$profile/extensions"
blender --background --command extension repo-add yuga_tools \
  --name 'YUGA Optional Artist Tools' --directory "$profile/extensions/yuga_tools"
blender --background --command extension install-file "$zip" -r yuga_tools --enable
blender --background --python-exit-code 1 --python-expr \
  "import bpy; print(bpy.ops.mesh.looptools_relax.get_rna_type().identifier)"
```

## Render and export decisions

- Save good scene-linear material values and explicitly record the display view transform. Keep AgX for the matched shot. A claim about an ACES tutorial or color pipeline requires a specific accessible source and OCIO configuration.
- Review the lit PNG against the source image at the same aspect ratio and from the same planned full-figure view. Use masks or depth and normal inspection to diagnose material, occlusion and color. Keep a second camera render for surfaces the target image cannot show.
- In a playable export, check named materials and tangent-space normal inputs, joint count, transforms, weights, action playback, contact and level of detail. Bake unsupported Blender shading or curves separately if a new model is promoted. A detailed Blender scene with sculpt, groom and court is a separate editable deliverable.

### Engine handoff checks

Record the export format, engine/importer version, unit scale, tangent and normal settings, and animation sampling for any target under test. Use the same original weighted mesh, a measured scale marker, one neutral material and one clip to validate a new importer configuration before exporting the full asset. Compare the imported facing, skin deformations, lip and eye shading, original clip duration, hand/sole contact and material response. Unity and Unreal were reviewed through official documentation. Their editor imports have not been run here.

The cloud Blender 4.3.2 bundled exporters emitted a binary FBX revision `7400` and an uncompressed GLB version `2` from a simple local fixture. This checks that the exporter operators run on this machine. Epic names FBX 2020.2 in its Skeletal Mesh documentation, so a destination-engine import is necessary to qualify a skeletal asset.
