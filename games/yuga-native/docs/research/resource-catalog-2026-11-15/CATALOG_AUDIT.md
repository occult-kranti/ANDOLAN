# Audit of the supplied hero-shooter catalog

The supplied text is useful as a discovery index. It is not a reproducible acquisition manifest: several entries lack exact repository owners, package versions, source URLs or license files, and its two sections disagree about some activity dates/counts. We do not treat its stars, maintenance labels, commercial-use conclusions or claims of exhaustive absence as verified facts.

## Claims deliberately left unresolved

No fresh star-count ranking, exhaustive repository census, account-specific Fab entitlement, Quixel price guarantee, Lyra full-package licensing conclusion, anti-cheat vendor agreement assessment, or current status of every unnamed GAS/MCP plugin is asserted. No purchased game archive was opened or extracted, and no commercial game assets were added. This study concerns technical fit and documented license conditions, not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion.

The root audit fetched Epic's official EULA, Content License, Lyra overview, Fab summary, Creative Commons terms and GitHub licensing guidance. The Lyra Fab short-link returned an inaccessible redirect. GNU FAQ/selling pages timed out through the web tool; their text was not used as fresh evidence. Specialist source ledgers record their own exact successful and failed retrievals. Browser “current” documentation can change; archive the exact governing text and item receipt when acquiring a candidate.
