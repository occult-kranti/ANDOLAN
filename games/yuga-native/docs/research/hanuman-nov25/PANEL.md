The quality target is evaluated from the actual model and game camera. Concept direction, numeric geometry checks and Blender stills each answer a different question. Physical GPU profiling, artist-led refinement and human play sessions are separate acceptance work.

The third Blender revision established the first playable trial. The independent reviewer confirmed that the front waist panels remain continuous in the reviewed pose and both soles meet the studio plinth. The rear sash intersected its clasp. The fourth round moved the sash over the shell and measured 16.16 mm of minimum clearance across its 589 sampled crossing vertices. The fifth round softened the mouth, improved the tail transition and supplies the final playable asset. The packed Blender scene reopens with its textures, camera and eleven clips. Expression, fabric contact across moves and measured levels of detail remain below the production target. [Art files and measured details](../../../art/hanuman/README.md)

The first native route exposed an input-driver error. An absolute pointer move while the mouse was captured turned the character almost 90 degrees, so the frontal and rear strikes missed. The corrected route used mouse button edges without repositioning, aimed the vault with relative input and reused the courtyard lighting and stone finish. [First run receipt](native-r1-driver-receipt.json)

The corrected run used 16 input steps, reached each of the five impact events and exited through the title with code 0. It recorded 34 mace damage, 48 self-shield, a collision-limited vault and return to the floor, a 28-damage rear strike followed by burn exposure, and three 65-damage Mountain Pulse receipts. The separate authority fixture also tested allied healing and interruption. Eleven original R3 screenshots accompany the download. Nearby training targets can still crowd the camera and the normal launcher uses Rama’s portrait. [Actual route and events](native-r2/receipt.json) · [Rig tests](native-r2/rig.json) · [Combat tests](native-r2/combat.json) · [Independent review](independent-review.md)

The final R5 model uses 167 bones, 11 clips and four responsive tail links. Independent review found all four links animated in every clip. The overlay fades over seven 60 Hz ticks on entry to the authored sweep, reaches zero by impact, and resets on the actual trial lifecycle. The 7.5-second studio reel samples authored motion in place. The 7-second native reel uses scripted input against the trial with an advancing physics clock. Both videos are silent.
