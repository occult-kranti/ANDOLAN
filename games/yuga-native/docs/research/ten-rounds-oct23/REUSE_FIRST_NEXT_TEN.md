## Hero sharing and full-map adoption rules

**Share infrastructure and source references, not finished-hero labels.** The accepted Rama GLBs, animation library, LOD binder, input/state/event plumbing, palette ownership and intake tests are the donor. Arjuna is the first compatible archer scaffold; Karna and Meghnad can reuse that pipeline only after their own proportions, face, bow, silhouette, grips and choreography pass. The current Godot runtime library has **23 clips**; importing the GLB into Blender yields only its **nine embedded legacy actions**. Do not claim the Blender scaffold inherited all 23.

Sita, Draupadi, Subhadra, Chitrangada and Ulupi require their own approved anatomy, face, garment and weapon choreography. Bhima/Duryodhana heavy proportions and mace mechanics, Hidimbi's design, Hanuman's tail/mace/airborne body, and Ravana's weapon/crown need separate art and compatibility decisions. The current codex gives Ravana ten crown facets within one head outline and Ulupi humanoid legs/face; changing those is a design revision. A new rig/rest pose needs its own versioned animation contract; do not loosen Rama's exact 164-bone or single-live-rig checks to make incompatible anatomy pass. Family intake must reject unknown sockets and incompatible binds.

**Adopt modular architecture district by district.** Reuse `assets/environment/lanka_courtyard.glb`, `assets/world/{architecture.glb,nature.glb}`, existing water/ground/roof/frontage/lighting helpers and the tested source loader. Preserve the source group's actual geometry and licence; use named rigid placements and an explicit collision/navigation contract. Ancient coastal/Lanka, swamp/jungle and modern civic districts need distinct silhouettes, circulation and sightlines, not the same podium copied everywhere. Source instancing is encouraged; every district still needs actual route, combat-camera, day/night, resource-cost and ordinary-distance review. One accepted kit or one element's intake tests does not qualify the assembled map.
