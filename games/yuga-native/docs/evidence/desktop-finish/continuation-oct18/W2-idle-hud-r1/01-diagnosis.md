# Idle traversal HUD

The actual default-camera world frame in `../W1-actual-r1/world-landmarks-r1/candidate/00-landmarks-fullworld.png` shows a large lower-right traversal panel while Rama is grounded with full energy and both actions ready. It repeats ground/zero altitude, the energy percentage and optional tutorial advice. It occupies useful world space despite no active traversal decision.

The bounded correction keeps explicit current/max energy, the bar, both saved action keys and the 36m range. Only the genuinely idle, full, ready, grounded actor with no aimed anchor, targeting failure, drill or terminal outcome gets shorter copy. Flight, tether, partial energy, cooldown, targeting feedback and scenario instructions retain their original branches. No claim that Help fully replaces traversal guidance.
