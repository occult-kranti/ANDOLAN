# W2 independent diadem review — LIMITED

Viewed all eight full original1280×960 PNGs, before reading any sibling artistic verdict: baseline/candidate rest-front, rest-profile, rest-rear and cast-three-quarter. Source/camera/pose/material matching is owned by [W1 pair evidence](W1-diadem-r1/receipt.json); this is the visible artistic judgement only. No image or asset modification.

The refit is a useful correction to the floating rail, strongest in profile. In [baseline profile](W1-diadem-r1/baseline/rest-profile.png), the rear half makes an open gold loop with background visible inside it, detached from the hair contour. In [candidate profile](W1-diadem-r1/candidate/rest-profile.png), that loop closes onto the hair and reads as a worn headband. The [candidate rear](W1-diadem-r1/candidate/rest-rear.png) likewise brings the lower rail down toward the central hair mass and reduces the lifted end hooks; the upper bun ring remains distinct.

The blue front stone, gold crest leaves and central hanging ornament retain their silhouette and position in both front and three-quarter views. The candidate does not visually crush the crest, move it into the forehead, or introduce an obvious open break through its centre. No obvious rail/skin penetration is visible in these four stills; hair occlusion and movement clearance are not proved.

The remaining artistic limit is the lower rail’s bend quality. In [candidate front](W1-diadem-r1/candidate/rest-front.png), the short temple turns are visibly more angular, especially the viewer-left dogleg near the outer eyebrow level. In [candidate cast three-quarter](W1-diadem-r1/candidate/cast-three-quarter.png), the horizontal line from crest toward the visible ear has a shallow dip and upward kink where it crosses the large hair lobes. It reads more like a wire following individual bumps than a deliberately formed continuous metal circlet. This is small at whole-character scale but visible in these intended close views. The rear is comparatively calm; I do not see a severe repeated zigzag or gross new clipping there.

**Verdict: LIMITED.** The attachment problem is visibly improved and front identity is preserved. I would keep this as a better fit candidate, but reserve final close-up jewellery acceptance until the two temple transitions and side dip can form a smoother intentional arc without reopening the old rear gap. That is a narrow silhouette refinement, not a request for more ornament or a different crest. It is not full-hero quality acceptance, animation clearance, distant-LOD review or commercial-art parity.
