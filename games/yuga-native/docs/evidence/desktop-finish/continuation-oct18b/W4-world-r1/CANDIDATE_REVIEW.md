# Candidate1: shallow-film colour, actual review pending

The current released-PCK night original at [W1 archived night original](../W1-actual-r1/water-baseline-r1/raw/00-night-swamp.png) was directly opened. Both swamp lobes read as broad soft green smudges without slab detail. The dry crossing remains legible. At full pool coverage the original shader discards all previously computed paving colour, even though the declared surface is only a 12 mm cosmetic film over flat collision ground.

The isolated candidate changes only the swamp body-colour blend weight from 1.0 to 0.18. Thus 82% of the existing damp-stone colour signal remains at the centre; at the dry border the original colour is exact. Existing pool outline, wet-stone tint, wave normal, roughness, specular, animation and coast branch remain unchanged. This is opaque colour compositing, not actual transparency, refraction, depth or a new movement mechanic.

The finite perturbation oracle demonstrates the old centre erasing slab-colour variation and the new centre retaining it. Those 16 source/math checks are not shader compilation, visual acceptance or performance measurements. W1 owns current-PCK preflight/actual rendering. The three planned still guards are night swamp, same-camera day swamp, and corrected coast day; a short matched animation sequence must preserve the bed pattern and existing dry strip before admission.

The first coast-day take is invalid: its camera was inside the block 5 bounds. It and its sidecar remain with W1. The corrected offshore camera (-94,4.6,-38)→(-80,-.15,-38) passes a conservative recorded-bounds test against 1132 visible meshes, including 24 water-grid segments; that offline test still requires actual framing review. Failed intermediate offline bounds proposals remain recorded.

Root owns a separate shared ground-normal experiment. It is not included in this candidate. Canonical rendering, geometry, authority and content sources remain unchanged. No new asset, light, texture, collider or material allocation is introduced.
