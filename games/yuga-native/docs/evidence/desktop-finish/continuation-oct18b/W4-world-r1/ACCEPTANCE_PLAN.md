# Bounded acceptance contract (before current images)

This is a review plan, not a selected shader remedy or accepted visual result.

- Baseline/current candidate use the same complete scene, camera, phase, viewport, simulation schedule, active water material parameters and common paving include. Record water's actual world transform/bounds and projected coverage; the coast is outside the playable envelope and swamp remains cosmetic wet ground.
- A candidate may alter only the demonstrated water reflectance/normal/roughness behavior. No alpha/depth/screen sampling, extra light, geometry, collider, movement or route changes. Root owns ground/common material work separately.
- Compare current coast day and swamp night at the fixed cameras. Dry crossing and opaque border must retain the same paving controls; no new bright line may imply an objective, depth step or blocker. Reeds, building silhouettes, hero/gameplay state and light budgets remain unchanged.
- Choose the affected fixed camera for 32 consecutive frames at a fixed 30 Hz capture clock. Record exact sample frames and water animation parameters; prove shared TIME schedule if claiming cross-run temporal equality. Otherwise report the timing limitation rather than comparing unrelated shimmer phases as a material result.
- Visual admission requires an independently visible improvement at normal review scale, preserved bank/dry-route readability, and no new moving sparkle or reflection discontinuity in the finite sequence. Native shader compilation and relevant existing world-lighting cases are required. Source pins and actual node/collider/resource controls protect already accepted resource sharing.
- No FPS, physical-GPU, long-cycle water, full night traversal or finished-world quality claim follows from this narrow gate.
