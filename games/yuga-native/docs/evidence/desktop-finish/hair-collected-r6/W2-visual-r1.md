# R6 author visual review: REJECT, no promotion

I independently opened all eight full original PNGs from the frozen current-runtime pair: four baseline and four candidate. The anatomical-left profile is the new yaw180 view; no old opposite-side R5 image was substituted. Durable originals match the exact raw files opened.

**rest-front** — The slimmer side mass exposes the face, crown and ears cleanly. The high, plain hairline is still cap-like; a successful front alone does not establish the hairstyle.

**rest-profile** — The anatomical-left view shows a broad rounded swelling below the retained knot and short rear locks that divide into pointed independent blades. The baseline has more continuous down/back flow, despite excessive bulk.

**rest-rear** — The knot, horizontal rounded puck beneath it, diadem band and repeated scalloped nape form stacked layers. There is no sky gap, but the hair does not read as one gathered form. The baseline curtain is heavy yet more cohesive.

**cast-three-quarter** — Bright sky exposes a rounded bulb behind/below the knot and a small fan of isolated pointed ends. The front sweep does not visibly continue through that swelling into the knot. Literal attachment is fixed; natural collection and flow are not.

The bounded improvements are the slimmer front and closed attachment. They do not pass whole-hairstyle cohesion. The current baseline remains bulky and helmet-like, but its upper mass and falling lengths read together more convincingly. I agree with lead and W1 rejection. No canonical promotion is recommended.
