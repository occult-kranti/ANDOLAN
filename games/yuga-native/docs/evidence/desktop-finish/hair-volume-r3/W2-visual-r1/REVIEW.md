# C3 independent four-view artistic review

**REJECT at the coarse-shape gate.** I directly opened all four original PNGs and the approved concept sheet. The closed volumes are a real improvement over the earlier ribbon construction, but the hairstyle still reads as a few oversized sculpted petals rather than a continuous gathered flow. This is a major-shape failure, before fine strands, UVs, rigging or runtime export.

- **Front:** the broad sweep has volume and the crest stays legible. Long exposed scalp channels remain beside and between the sparse sweeps; hanging temple lobes end abruptly. The target has a connected scalp-rooted collected mass with smaller overlapping transitions.
- **Anatomical-left profile:** depth is improved, but a wide bare band separates the upper sweep from the temple lobe. The fold remains an upright hollow loop. The rear fall is a thick paddle with an abrupt flat or stepped end, without the concept's flowing taper.
- **Rear:** the diamond gap and thin crossed ribbons are gone. Two bulky overlapping lobes now dominate, ending in blunt stacked blocks. Their broad central seam and small number of masses do not read as staggered, asymmetric S falls. The fold at the tie still resembles a hollow donut rather than a compact sideways collected turn.
- **Cast-angle authoring:** the large scalp opening is especially clear beneath the upper sweep. A separate petal at the temple and the heavy rear lobe repeat the same disconnected grouping; the upright loop remains prominent. This is a static authoring angle, not an animated cast test.

## Visible hardware and locked reference

The face, eyes, eyebrows, earrings and frontal crest remain readable in these views. The front crown rail is recognizable and substantially continuous. In profile and cast angle it crosses the temple hair lobe, and the tie appears as a white bar entering and emerging around the top loop. These pixels do not establish clean crown/tie clearance or resolve every triangle contact retained in the source diagnostic. I do not claim a newly visible eye or face breakthrough, and I do not relabel the known gold intersections as harmless scalp seating.

The prior diagnostic's inward scalp contacts must not be confused with the **visible uncovered scalp channels** here: one concerns buried surface seating, the other missing hair coverage between major masses. Both can exist together. No additional metrics or geometry changes were run for this review.
