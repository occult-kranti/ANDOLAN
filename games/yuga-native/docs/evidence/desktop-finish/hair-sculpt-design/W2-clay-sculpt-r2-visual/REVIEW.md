The authoring method did not resolve the problem: small displacements of a ray-fitted dome leave a dome, while open C-sections plus inward Solidify produce a wider sheet. The inherited horizontal fold rings preserve the upright-cap reading. Passing topology/rest/projection facts cannot accept those forms.
