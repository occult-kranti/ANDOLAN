# R5 author review: REVISE, no promotion

I independently opened all eight complete original PNGs from the fresh current-runtime baseline and R5 candidate, at rest-front, rest-profile, rest-rear and cast-three-quarter. These are the current matched pair, not reused R4 images.

R5 closes the literal gap under the unchanged knot. The rear and bright-sky cast now show a dark physical connection. That is a bounded improvement over R4, but the connection reads as a narrow stalk below a large perched knot. The upper crown remains too small to collect naturally into that knot. The profile and cast also expose separated, stiff, near-horizontal blade locks behind the ear. The lower cutaway is severe; the remaining hair reads as independent pieces rather than a continuous swept hairstyle.

The baseline is bulky and helmet-like, but its upper mass and longer overlapping locks hold together more convincingly. R5 clears the face, eyebrows, ears and crown, and reduces silhouette mass; those gains do not outweigh the disconnected visual hierarchy. I agree with the independent lead and W1 rejection. Source contacts, actual import preservation and exact matched controls do not establish aesthetic acceptance.

The next priority is one coherent scalp-rooted collected mass beneath the existing knot, with overlapping tapered lock groups that carry the sweep into it and fall down/back behind the ears. A second narrow plug would repeat the failure. Preserve the knot/tie, gold crown, face, rig, material and accepted UVs. No additional material or nape-only experiment is justified by this review.

R5 remains inactive and excluded from the release. R4 rejection and the complete R5 source candidate are preserved. Any R6 must be a new isolated packet and pass these same four actual views before promotion.
