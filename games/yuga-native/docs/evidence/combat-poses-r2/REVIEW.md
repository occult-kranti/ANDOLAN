# Initial duel actor render review

Eight actual 960 × 960 Godot Forward+ studio captures. The report confirms successful capture of the stated asset/library hashes; **visual acceptance failed**.

Root and the independent direction reviewer observed small detached bow jewels floating beside the Ravana Sentinel and a crown sitting above the hair. These defects were returned to the artist for correction. Skin/cloth gloss and the draw-hand/string relationship also need closer review.

The ready and draw poses differ visibly, but these stills do not prove continuous motion, physical contact, real-time performance or production-quality character art. Source revisions after this review must be captured again.
