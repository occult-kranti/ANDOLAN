# Initial integrated duel review

Twelve actual 960 × 540 Godot Forward+ samples, captured with unchanged runtime sources and fresh imported models. See combat-film-render.json for exact times and hashes. This is a keyframe preview, not the continuous movie.

The guard, locked warning corridor, five-bolt wave, health receipts and captions are visible. Root review returned these concrete issues: overly flat brown lighting and shadow stippling; small distant actors; caption overlap with Rama's feet in the draw shot; apparent sole/floor separation after evasion; detached sentinel ornaments and crown fit already identified by the close-pose review. The impact camera has a very fast transition requiring revision and continuous-motion inspection.

This preview is retained as an earlier review, not accepted final art. Subsequent lighting, model, camera and attachment changes require a new capture.
