# Final traversal capture review

The completed capture contains 180 consecutive rendered frames, 360 measured physics ticks, a six-second 1280 × 720 H.264 video and five additional review stills. The video is silent. The native game has the separately tested original soundtrack and seven event cues; no fabricated soundtrack synchronization is asserted here.

The hero's spawn and initial camera were staged before the take. Actual Godot Input actions then engaged the tether, released it, and activated ascent/lateral flight. No player transform was rewritten during the take. All 180 recorded player/rig states remained finite; maximum speed was 18 m/s and minimum energy about 19.4. Additional overview, landing, army and night views were staged after the take. The army still advanced its public simulation by a separately recorded 20.5833 seconds until a real queued attack appeared; those ticks are not spliced into the video.

Root and specialist reviewers inspected sampled original frames and stills. The revised dark minimap legend and compact contextual hint are readable and leave the central hero clear. The three routes, landable deck surfaces and gold tether rings remain distinguishable. Spear/shield, bow/quiver and crown/banner roles and rust/teal teams are readable in the engagement still. Stone courses, broad deck trim, lanterns and day/night color changes are visible. No gross detached limbs or torso/weapon intersections were evident in the inspected samples.

This is bounded prototype acceptance. Continuous foot contact, sophisticated archer grips, cloth/hair motion, detailed faces and commercial art quality are not established. Banner speckling, simple segmented forms/materials, distant objective contrast and sky-heavy framing remain visible art work. The generated concept plate is a future visual target, not a substitute for this captured build. The full roster and native online matches remain separate roadmap work.

The renderer was Forward+ on llvmpipe software Vulkan. Capture and staging took 187,548 ms. During the input take the maximum recorded workload was 931 draw calls, 2,352 rendered objects and 587,431 primitives. The movie's 30 FPS is simulated playback time; these numbers do not establish consumer-GPU frame rate. Live graphics profiling and human playtesting remain required.

All 214 captured source files were independently compared with commit `0124fd4d63384a988223eb76ceb72b2510659ac3`. The video and retained images have byte hashes in their reports. All 180 original PNGs were verified before encoding; only selected PNGs are retained in this archive. `capture-summary.json` records the source commit, adapter and workload. Portable Linux OS input testing is recorded separately and does not rely on this scripted take.
