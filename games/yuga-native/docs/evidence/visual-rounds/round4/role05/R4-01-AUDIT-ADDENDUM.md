# R4-01 independent evidence audit addendum

The original rejected-candidate packet remains unchanged. Independent R4-01 review identified two limitations that constrain reuse.

The executed `continuity-r3a.json`, `continuity-r4.json` and both existing-suite reports declare the historical raw hero SHA `de61dc4c470920eab6f3265bc5467da7f9fb33d6fae2129a9353e7ca6d24d3f5`. `seal.py` later collected `source-pins.json` from the then-current checkout, recording R2 raw `140f8ff…351a` and imported SCN `90d5e4c…68a9`. These are seal-time observations, not an execution-time raw/import binding receipt. The execution reports do not hash their loaded imported SCN. Therefore the old packet does **not** establish that those executed measurements used the exact approved R2 imported resource, and it cannot establish current R7 behavior. The claim in the original reproduction section that exact complete source/hash identity is embedded in every output was too broad; simpler acceleration/event reports omit hero identity, and none binds the actual import at execution. The original reports and rejected candidate decisions are retained as historical defect/rejection evidence only. No hashes are retroactively rewritten.

The old skin classifier tags a sampled vertex only when an individual foot/toe weight exceeds .5. R4-01 independently finds878 of9114 body vertices with combined foot/toe influence>.5 but no individual influence>.5, including eight in the original every96th sample. Thus the contact-tag subset undercounts foot-dominated geometry. All-skin sampled floor minima are unaffected by the tag predicate, but neither the sampled floor set nor the contact tag establishes full sole coverage. The raw compact report lacks per-frame full bases and the identity of the worst skin vertex; it does not support exact full posed replay.

Future replay must bind raw GLB, import descriptor and actual imported resource before/after execution, use summed foot/toe weights for contact classification, and preserve full pose/worst-vertex witnesses. R4-07 uses the separately frozen source/import-verified R2 capture project; final R7 continuous acceptance remains separate. No historical candidate is revived or promoted.
