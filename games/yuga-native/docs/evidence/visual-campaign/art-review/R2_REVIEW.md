the back sash has reduced broad white glare while indigo/gold/ivory identity

## Optional hair accent

Both matched original pairs were opened: `character-face-front-{baseline,candidate}`
and `character-temple-three-quarter-{baseline,candidate}`. Capture metadata
records equal camera and rig pose for each pair, with the existing title
portrait light enabled equally. The candidate is not present in the preceding
29 ordinary/milestone views and is not promoted to the runtime.

**Reject this candidate.** In the front view the added strands start around
the lateral brow line and form conspicuous black arcs that extend down past
the earrings. They read as wire/antenna contours rather than short natural
flyaways. In the three-quarter view an added diagonal strand crosses the
temple/cheek toward the earring and has an isolated-looking tip. The additional
contour makes the face less coherent. Preserve the original groom while an
artist revises root placement, taper and silhouette with a clear reference.

The close-up baseline itself reveals larger remaining defects: triangular
shading patches across temple, cheek and neck; a regular thick rope-like groom;
very simple eye/eyelid and lip forms; and limited expression. Investigate
normal/smoothing/material causes under controlled light before increasing mesh
count. These visible issues have greater impact than four decorative strands.
Existing crown, jewelry, bow and indigo/gold costume give a useful identity
anchor; their presence does not close Q01 or Q04.
