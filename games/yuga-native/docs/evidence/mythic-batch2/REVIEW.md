## Integration findings and repairs

Independent review found a stale damage notification and freed target during reentrant army reset; callback epoch/instance validation now rejects it. The player windup used a different clock from army resolution; an authoritative event latch now keeps movement and firing locked until accepted release/cancel/reset. Effects exposed sector geometry outside the warning footprint and replayed stale resets; both were corrected. Imported QOA audio did not preserve the intended PCM evidence; all sixteen cues now import as PCM and actual playback ownership retires within the measured test bound.

Actual Linux window review found a card covering the hero and stale Preparing text after zero-hit releases. Both were corrected and tested through the full lifecycle. A second extracted-package review proved Continue could remain clipped in the pause scroll viewport. Deferred explicit focus reveal and modal guards now preserve the current action and avoid stealing selector/controls focus. The retained `pause-focus/` reproduction and independent tests demonstrate the before/after edge. The two rejected window archives retain their original pixels, manifests and input ledgers.

Release review found stale-success and empty-report acceptance, incomplete source inventory, missing producer preview-field agreement and unchecked event chronology. Typed nonempty reports, required pins, exact inventories and lifecycle chronology now reject those cases. Final synthetic publication checks pass 155 Mythic, 169 traversal, 78 siege and 106 Triad cases. These counts overlap prior suites and are not summed as unique coverage.

## Frozen evidence and limitations

[Final ultimate studies](../mythic-ultimates-render-r3/ULTIMATE-RENDER-REVIEW.md) contain fourteen hero stills, three variants and a four-second 120-frame studio take. It is a staged Rama lifecycle and Sita study, not continuous rendering of every hero's full lifecycle. [Final traversal](../mythic-traversal-render-r2/TRAVERSAL-RENDER-REVIEW.md) records 180 frames/six seconds, flight, grapple and the actual HUD. Earlier versions remain historical. Source inventories include shaders, assets, scripts and the actual capture helpers; the final export verification binds both captures and sixteen typed reports to the actual exported manifest.

The `setup/` archive retains 47 successful reports across explicit initial/resumed prepared-environment runs, affected refreshed reports, browser 225 tests/typecheck and original failed obsolete test fixtures. It is not described as one uninterrupted setup run. The final integration archive records 113 checks with no error/warning/leaked nodes. Required release CI re-executes its checks at the immutable tag.

The effects remain sparse geometric studies: thin distant warnings, ring-like Ulupi silhouette and insufficient Karna/Bhima separation need further art work. Rama face/hair/cloth and weapon contact, animation polish, dense-combat readability and small letterboxed text remain production concerns. Software Vulkan demonstrates these recorded pixels, not consumer-GPU frame-time qualification. Windows is packaged and archive-validated only. Dummy audio and PCM checks do not establish human listening quality. Native online matches, hero health/death, all production hero models and AAA visual parity remain open.
