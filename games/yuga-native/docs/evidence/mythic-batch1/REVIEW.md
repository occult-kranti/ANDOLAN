## Review and corrections

Those failures and their successful repeats are retained under `independent/`. Final contract and catalogue source hashes match the accepted reports.

Actual rendering exposed reversed ornament normals and an overlapping offshore sculpture; winding and separation regressions now pass. Motion review exposed a non-looping capture fixture, capture timings differing from gameplay, and carry overriding the first firing tick. The accepted r3 uses the native blends/action duration and repeated-fire cadence, with immediate action ownership. Earlier runs remain rejected.

The first surface pass incorrectly applied an sRGB transfer to already encoded palette values. It produced pale skin and pastel indigo. The corrected runtime GLB is **`3424d72e74bb425c7e6bb2ff2fddc05c792657c437a888c56671fbd7d3ec3bcc`**. The r3 motion capture predates this material-only correction; exact original rig/skin/animation preservation and fresh pose/retarget checks establish its limited motion applicability. Final hero and world stills show the corrected material bytes. Do not call the earlier motion video a capture of the final surfaces.

Root independently inspected r3 motion frames 96/108/150, final hero front/face/linen views, and final world dusk/gallery-night/coastal-water/reed-night/river-loom views.
