# Creative hero catalogue verification

This is the bounded FG00-T03 creative-intake packet for the fourteen existing YUGA heroes. It delivers a source-compatible design catalogue, fourteen art/motion cards and a validated presentation contract. It does not close production art, native hero gameplay, animation contact, network or balance gates.

## Three review rounds

1. **Source and concept review.** Read every current browser hero definition and the relevant simulator dispatch, roster, animation and retained literary-source records. Preserve fourteen identities, fourteen primary entries and all 42 Q/E/R baselines. Author individual silhouettes, materials, motion language, counters and signature ultimate shapes. No new commercial-game extraction, third-party asset or full-book reading is claimed.
2. **Contract and adversarial review.** Require stable IDs, pinned current definitions, typed finite dimensions/timing, honest current-versus-proposed preparation, readable telegraphs, bounded resources and no gameplay mutation. The final owner suite passes **71 checks** in [codex-cases.json](codex-cases.json); [catalogue-check.json](catalogue-check.json) accepts the canonical data.
3. **Independent challenge and repair.** An independent reviewer found eight counterexamples, including huge-integer overflow, incompatible effect family, zero geometry/travel, booleans posing as numbers, invalid palette and false primary production status. [The first report](codex-probe-r1.json) retains the defects; [the repeated report](codex-probe-r2.json) accepts the canonical catalogue and rejects all eight malformed cases without exceptions. The original probe scripts are archived unchanged for provenance; they contain their original workspace/output paths.

The [source manifest](source-manifest.json) pins authored files, local inputs and evidence. The catalogue and final checker hashes match both final owner and independent reports. The source commit identifies the starting repository snapshot; new design files are separately pinned because they were uncommitted at this freeze.

## Acceptance boundary and next packet

The data/design contract passes. Rendered art and continuous demonstration footage have not been produced by this packet. Future practice implementation must keep the catalogue's `applies_gameplay: false`, preserve browser rules, label proposed timing, pool/recycle effects and retain readable warnings under low-effects and reduced-motion settings. Four concurrent casts and the per-cast budgets are provisional limits, not measured consumer-hardware performance.

The next demonstration packet requires actual moving render/input review, overlap/reset/invalid-ID tests and explicit scope in its UI. The full production path still requires accepted editable anatomy, faces, costume, hair, rigs, grip/sole contact, complete locomotion/aim/cast transitions, authority/network adoption and balance. Fourteen concept cards or decorative ultimate previews cannot establish fourteen production-ready heroes.
