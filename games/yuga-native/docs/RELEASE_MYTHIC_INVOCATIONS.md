# YUGA — Mythic invocations native preview

Fourteen original troop abilities now have playable lifecycles, distinct geometric effects and short synthesized cues. Rama channels the roster's invocations in the existing three-lane Confluence practice arena. This release also includes the reconstructed Rama surfaces, upper-body bow carry, original world ornaments and lighting from the first batch.

Extract the entire Linux or Windows ZIP and run `launch.sh` or `launch.bat`. Choose **Explore Confluence → F3 → Use invocation**, then press **X** on flat street ground. F2 opens key remapping; the HUD shows the actual saved binding. All invocations share a 12-second cooldown. The selector explains supported damage, healing, shielding, slows or temporary guards separately from its more ambitious art brief. Reduced effects and motion are saved there; Escape opens volume and other practice actions. F5 restarts the round.

Try Rama's three swept solar arrows, Ulupi's slowing water field, Sita's protective healing zone or Subhadra's temporary bow guards. Effects follow accepted troop events and bounded lifetimes. These are fourteen abilities through one avatar, not fourteen finished production characters. Hero health/death and native online matches remain unfinished.

Existing controls: WASD move, mouse aim, left click bow, Space jump/rise, Ctrl descend, Shift sprint, Q pulse, E dash, R reload, F flight, G tether/release, F1 overview, N daylight, Escape pause. The 140 × 260 m arena has three lanes, troop waves, cannon carts, royal chariots, last-hit gold and capturable supply relays. Escape → Siege practice opens a vehicle drill; Visit Lotus relay provides an immediate objective route. Flight and tether consume energy.

`yuga-ultimate-studies.mp4` is a four-second staged Rama/Sita effects take, accompanied by fourteen hero motif stills and three variants. `yuga-traversal-preview.mp4` is six seconds of source-matched flight/tether capture. Both are silent, software-rendered evidence, not hardware frame-rate benchmarks. The separately included Confluence duel film remains older cinematic evidence for its own source hashes. The older Triad and siege videos remain in their previous release.

The package and CI evidence cover gameplay, pause/focus/return, remapping, persistence, audio lifecycle, source-bound media and archive integrity. Linux execution was reviewed separately; Windows execution, physical-GPU performance and human listening/balance review remain unverified here. Forward+ requires Vulkan; `--rendering-method gl_compatibility` is a reduced-effects fallback.

Art is still a prototype. Sparse effect geometry, thin distant warnings, similar ultimate silhouettes, face/hair/cloth and weapon contact need further production work. This milestone is not the completed game or Deadlock/Overwatch graphics parity. The build handbook and dependency backlog document the next authority/controller, multiplayer, combat and art gates.
