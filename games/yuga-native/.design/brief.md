# YUGA native interface brief

Product: original mythology-inspired native Godot action game. Current playable content is Rama’s offline bow training and Confluence lane practice; the duel film remains available. No online matchmaking or unavailable roster entries belong in this launch surface.

Immediate job: understand the two modes, choose one and enter practice. The actual lit Rama actor is the title’s central visual evidence; UI must leave his face, silhouette and bow readable.

Direction: cinematic indigo, restrained warm gold, ivory text, large serif YUGA wordmark and native sans-serif controls. A left-side decision column uses a mode selector, concrete description and one gold launch action. Abilities and film are secondary; Help, Settings and Quit are quiet utilities. Avoid decorative dashboards, equal-weight card grids and unrelated feature claims.

Required behaviors: ordinary Godot Buttons, visible keyboard focus, logical Tab traversal, explicit selected mode, preserved offline behavior, loading and recoverable failure state, legible 960×600 / 1280×720 / 1366×768 layouts. Shorter windows scroll and follow focus. Theme tokens are shared in scripts/hero_ui_theme.gd.

Evidence: baseline reviewed from docs/evidence/visual-campaign/capture-final-anchors-r4/01-title-day.png. Its actual Rama portrait is retained; current mode intent and next action require clearer hierarchy. Commercial comparison is a quality target only and does not establish parity.
