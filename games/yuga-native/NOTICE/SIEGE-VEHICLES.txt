YUGA original siege vehicles and effects — 17 October 2026

Cannon cart and royal chariot geometry, materials, wheel/recoil/defeat poses,
combat logic, ground warnings, projectile arcs and impact visuals are original
procedural project work. No Deadlock, Overwatch or Dota 2 models, textures,
animation clips or binaries are included in these additions.

Sources: scripts/lane_vehicle_art.gd, scripts/lane_minion.gd,
scripts/lane_army.gd and scripts/siege_effects.gd.
Design/provenance: art/units/SIEGE_VEHICLE_DESIGN.md.

Four siege cues are original deterministic PCM synthesis. The generator is
tools/audio/build-siege-audio.py. Asset hashes, durations, synthesis and numerical
level limits are recorded in assets/audio/siege/provenance.json within the game
resource pack and source repository. No external recordings are sampled.
Numerical audio checks do not establish human listening approval.

These are prototype assets. Animated 3D, physics and temporal visual effects do
not imply an additional 4D/5D asset format or commercial visual-quality parity.
