YUGA traversal and lane army additions

The traversal controller, bounded additive poses, landmark meshes, articulated
lane-unit meshes, lane simulation, UI and original synthesized audio are project
work. No proprietary commercial-game assets or animation clips are included by
these additions. The existing Rama asset retains its separate notices.

The generated concept plate under art/concepts is a text-prompt visual target,
not gameplay footage or an assertion of implemented fidelity. Its source record
is stored beside it. The package excludes authoring tools and concept documents.

Music and cue sources, byte hashes and synthesis details are recorded in
TRAVERSAL-AUDIO-PROVENANCE.json. Game-design reading references are recorded in
docs/research/TRAVERSAL_ARMY_SOURCES.md; attribution does not imply endorsement.
