# Original SURYA carbine

The SURYA mesh, bevels, layered plates, open stock, curved magazine, solar-cell
lattice, ivory lotus geometry, formed grip and PBR materials are original YUGA
project work authored by `tools/studio/build_surya_carbine.py` in Blender.
The editable source is `art/tactical/surya-carbine/surya-carbine.blend`.
No commercial firearm mesh, game asset, texture, scan or extracted resource is
included. No new third-party asset license or runtime dependency is introduced.
Blender and Godot retain their existing tool and runtime notices.

The unchanged, originally generated `art/tactical/surya-reference.png` and
`assets/tactical/menu-keyart.png` guide form and palette. Their prompts are
retained at `art/tactical/SURYA_PROMPT.md` and `art/tactical/MENU_PROMPT.md`.
They are concept references, not gameplay captures or automatic mesh outputs.
The runtime GLB contains geometry and original material values; neither concept
image is projected onto the weapon or embedded as a model texture.

The weapon is fictional. Its solar-cell motif and lotus ornament establish the
game's original South Asian fantasy art direction; it is not a reproduction of
a religious artifact or real-world firearm model.
