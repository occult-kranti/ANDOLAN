YUGA mythic invocation practice — 17 October 2026

The fourteen effect designs and meshes, native practice rules and short audio
cues are original project work. Mythological names and public-domain narratives
inspire these fictional game adaptations; their abilities do not claim scriptural
accuracy. No commercial game meshes, maps, recordings or samples are included.

Audio is generated deterministically by tools/audio/build-invocation-audio.py:
48 kHz mono PCM16, windup/cancel and fourteen release signatures. The manifest in
assets/audio/invocations pins the source WAV bytes. Cues use mathematical synthesis,
not sampled instruments; no traditional-raga authenticity or listening-test claim.

The current Rama model channels all fourteen invocations. Runtime payloads affect
living lane troops in offline practice. This does not constitute fourteen finished
hero models, hero-versus-hero combat or a native online match. The accompanying
catalogue contains future art direction beyond the rendered effect construction.

See docs/ULTIMATE_PRACTICE.md, docs/ULTIMATE_EFFECTS.md, docs/INVOCATION_UX.md,
docs/hero-design/ and docs/evidence/mythic-batch2/ for rules, provenance, reviewed
evidence and limitations. Existing source-asset notices remain applicable to the
Rama mesh, motion and other assets in this package.
